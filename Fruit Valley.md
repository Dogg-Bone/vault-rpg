Known to [[Denizens]] throughout the Woodland simply as "The Valley," this clearing is the largest in the woodland by miles, geographically and by population. The Valley contains eight major districts, each one almost a clearing unto itself. All factions have presence here in one way or another, and may outright control certain districts, but no faction has been able to take control of the Valley in its entirety. 

Fruit Valley is so named for the orchards that span its breadth. The orchards are ancient, and only parts are actively cultivated by the valley's constituents. The city itself is also ancient, inhabited since time immemorial, before the earliest records of the [[Eyrie Dynasties]]. 

Fruit Valley is verdant and beautiful, with plentiful food and fresh water from the orchards and the [[Silver River]]. Fruit Valley is the prime example of the War among the factions spoiling the Woodland's beauty.
# Districts
## Gilded Lofts
upper crust, upriver and above of everything else, seated in the canopies of the tallest trees. Parties and politics. Hornets, hercules beetles
## Tanglewood
ancient gnarled trees, main orchards harvested by denizens located here. The orchards are ancient and chaotic, it's easy to get lost. Arboreal spiders, honeybees, carnivorous plants, fire ants
## The Deeps
a cavernous grotto set into the riverside, extending back into a network of partially explored ruins inhabited by hermits, outcasts, and any others with a need for sheltered secrecy. The ruins are incredibly extensive and no part has been fully explored, but the first level is mostly safe. Scorpions, snakes
## Citadel
a sprawling stone palace originally constructed by [[the Ancients]] but inhabited continuously since by the Eyrie. 
## Bazaar
chaotic market, extending below ground into interconnected burrows. The main burrows and above ground square are well lit day and night, but the Bazaar extends well beyond the manicured center
## The Warren
nominally a rabbit cultural district, this network of burrows houses an enormous population of all kinds of denizens. Snakes, fire ants
## Riverside 
industry, impoverished housing and company headquarters
## Red Ward
rotting slums, located at a bend in the [[Silver River]] downstream of the rest of the city, catches refuse discarded in the river. fire ants, honeybees, hercules beetles, carnivorous plants, scorpions, and hornets. also snakes

# Conflicts
1 core conflict that is readily apparent to newcomers
several additional conflicts to be unearthed (3)

- Keepers siege the Citadel (fight with Eyrie) or set up waystation in the Grotto (fight with Lizard Cult, if they have a garden there)
- Hundreds mobs in the Red Ward become increasingly violent and threaten key districts
- Plague - sickness causing hair and feathers to shed is highly fatal, factions dealing with it the wrong way, or someone started it, or have to find a cure or relic
- Rot - orchards infected with White Rot, ruining harvest and famine is imminent
- Explosions in the Citadel - Corvid Conspiracy seeks to incite Eyrie to attack Marquise in Riverside or quash mobs in the Red Ward
- Murder in the Gilded Lofts - an Important Resident is found dead in the gilded lofts
- Counterfeit River Scrip -  River Company captains discover they are being paid in false scrip, and have been for a while. They begin acting with a much rougher hand, foreclosing, evicting, and imprisoning those who fall behind on payments. Corvid counterfeiters managed to acquire enormous wealth before the counterfeits were discovered and can now bring that wealth to bear. Woodland Alliance support swells in the face of the River Company's force.
- Erratic Eyrie - the daughter of the Citadel's ruling house disappears from within her quarters. now Eyrie patrols randomly search shops, residences, and denizens without notice as the Eyrie leader grows increasingly paranoid
- Eye of the Dragon - A group of Lizard Cult fanatics awakens an enormous reptile (max size giant snake or alligator) which begins to rampage topside. Cultists actively work to prevent others from harming the monster, claiming it is here to sate itself on the blood of the wicked and that it will return to its lair soon. The monster doesn't seem to have an end to its appetite.
