A time prior to the [[Great Civil War]] when the [[Eyrie Dynasties]] were at the height of their power. They Dynasties great houses warred against each other and their dominance left little room for other factions to obtain power. 

The [[Keepers in Iron]] were yet to be expelled from [[The Woodland]] and continued to watch over the relics and history of the forest and its clearings. Unlike the Keepers of the modern woodland, they devoted themselves to safeguarding their Archive. Expeditions not expressly permitted by the ruling Dynasty were rare. 

The [[Lizard Cult]] thrived among the downtrodden non-bird [[Denizens]] of the woodland. The religion remains much unchanged from these earlier days.

The [[Corvid Conspiracy]] was present even then, though their influence was not as commonly known as in the woodland of today, as their machinations had not yet been witnessed so widely as during the [[Great Civil War]].
