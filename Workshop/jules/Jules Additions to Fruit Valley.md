# Fruit Valley
Known to Denizens throughout the Woodland simply as "The Valley," this clearing is the largest in the woodland by miles, geographically and by population. The Valley contains eight major districts, each one almost a clearing unto itself. All factions have presence here in one way or another, and may outright control certain districts, but no faction has been able to take control of the Valley in its entirety.

Fruit Valley is so named for the orchards that span its breadth. The orchards are ancient, and only parts are actively cultivated by the valley's constituents. The city itself is also ancient, inhabited since time immemorial, before the earliest records of the [[Eyrie Dynasties]].

Fruit Valley is verdant and beautiful, with plentiful food and fresh water from the orchards and the [[Silver River]]. Fruit Valley is the prime example of the War among the factions spoiling the Woodland's beauty.

**Control:** Stalemate (No single faction controls the valley as a whole).
**Dominant Inhabitants:** Mixed Metropolitan (slightly Mouse-dominant).

## Overarching Conflicts
**Core Conflict:** *The Powder Keg*
The factions are piled too tightly on top of one another. An uprising in the Red Ward, combined with explosive sabotage by the Corvid Conspiracy, threatens to ignite a massacre that will pull the Marquise de Cat, the Eyrie, and the Woodland Alliance into all-out war. The Valley is on a knife's edge, and any small spark could cause an unprecedented catastrophe.

**Additional Conflicts:**
*   *The White Rot:* The ancient orchards are becoming infected with White Rot, ruining the harvest. Famine is imminent if a cure or magical intervention is not discovered soon.
*   *Counterfeit Scrip:* Riverfolk Company captains have discovered they are being paid in false scrip. They have begun acting with a much rougher hand, foreclosing, evicting, and imprisoning those who fall behind. Corvid counterfeiters are using their acquired wealth to incite further chaos.
*   *Rampaging Swarms:* Someone has contaminated the food supply of a local hive with a psychoactive chemical, causing massive, uncoordinated insect swarms to attack the city in droves.

## Overarching Important Residents

### Mayor Silas Brightwhiskers (Mouse)
The nominal leader of the Valley Council, constantly trying to appease all sides.
*   **Location:** The Council Chambers (near the Bazaar).
*   **Fear:** Being assassinated by one of the major factions for showing favoritism.
*   **Secret:** He is heavily indebted to The Magpie Syndicate for funding his election.
*   **Request:** Discretely deliver a bribe to a Syndicate enforcer to buy him another month of peace, rewarding the Vagabonds with official travel papers and a handful of coin.
*   **Opinion:** Thinks the Eyrie are planning a major offensive from the Citadel and that the Marquise is hopelessly outmatched.

### Captain Vespera (Cat)
A seasoned Marquise officer tasked with securing the Valley's industry, exhausted by the constant politicking.
*   **Location:** Riverside Headquarters.
*   **Fear:** Her troops will mutiny due to lack of pay and constant harassment from the Alliance.
*   **Secret:** She has been siphoning Marquise funds to smuggle her family out of the Woodland.
*   **Request:** Escort her family safely downriver out of the Valley. She will pay handsomely in Marquise armaments (Value 3, +valuable).
*   **Opinion:** Suspects that the Riverfolk Company is intentionally starving the Riverside of materials to drive up prices.

### Commander Aldous Kingfisher (Bird)
A pragmatic Eyrie commander who prefers economic domination over military conquest.
*   **Location:** The Citadel's lower courtyards.
*   **Fear:** The erratic behavior of the current Eyrie leader will provoke an unwinnable war.
*   **Secret:** He is secretly communicating with the Marquise to arrange a localized truce.
*   **Request:** Intercept an Eyrie courier carrying aggressive orders to the frontlines and replace them with his forged, moderate orders. He offers access to the Citadel's restricted archives in return.
*   **Opinion:** Believes the Lizard Cult is gathering too much influence in The Deeps and needs to be purged.

### "Honest" Pip / Agent Whisper (Mouse - Corvid Conspiracy)
A seemingly harmless fruit vendor who is actually a high-ranking Corvid operative.
*   **Location:** Tanglewood's edge, selling candied apples.
*   **Fear:** Being discovered by the Rook Family and eliminated for encroaching on their territory.
*   **Secret:** He is the one distributing the psychoactive chemicals to the local hives.
*   **Request:** Plant a small, mundane vial of chemicals in the Marquise barracks to incite a swarm attack there. Offers a steady supply of poisons and intel.
*   **Opinion:** Knows that Mayor Silas is in the Magpie Syndicate's pocket.

### Skipper Barnaby (Otter - Riverfolk Company)
A boisterous and ruthless riverboat captain demanding payment for the counterfeit scrip.
*   **Location:** The Valley Docks.
*   **Fear:** The Company will demote him to a mere oarsman for losing so much profit.
*   **Secret:** He actually accepted the counterfeit scrip knowingly to pay off a gambling debt, hoping to pass it off as someone else's mistake.
*   **Request:** Track down the Corvid counterfeiter and rough them up to get the real coin back. Offers free passage on his boat anywhere on the Silver River.
*   **Opinion:** Swears he saw a massive, scaly beast dragging a denizen into the river near The Deeps.

## Overarching Important Locations

### The Great Spanning Bridge
An ancient, massive stone bridge arching over the Silver River, connecting the upper districts to the lower ones. It is heavily patrolled by shifting alliances of faction guards, making it a severe chokepoint for illicit travel.

### The Valley Docks
A sprawling, chaotic network of wooden piers and floating barges serving as the primary hub for the Riverfolk Company. It is loud, crowded, and the best place to find transport or smuggle goods, assuming you can pay the exorbitant fees.

## Special Rules

> [!tip] Navigating the Megalopolis
> When you **attempt to quickly traverse from one district of Fruit Valley to another without drawing attention**, roll with Finesse.
> **On a 10+**, you make it through the winding crowds and checkpoints unseen and unhindered.
> **On a 7-9**, you encounter a delay or complication; the GM chooses one:
> *   You must pay a toll or bribe (mark 1 depletion).
> *   You draw the attention of a faction patrol (mark 1 notoriety with them).
> *   You arrive much later than intended, and the situation has worsened.
>
> **On a 6-**, you are cornered by hostile forces, a rioting mob, or a rampaging swarm in transit.

# Districts

## Gilded Lofts
The upper crust, upriver and above everything else, seated in the canopies of the tallest trees. A district of endless parties and ruthless politics, mostly untouched by the grime below.

**Control:** The Rook Family (Subtle manipulation) / Eyrie Dynasties (Nominal protection).
**Dominant Inhabitants:** Mixed, heavily aristocratic. Swarms of hornets and hercules beetles are kept as exotic pets or guards.

### Conflicts
**Core Conflict:** *Murder in the Canopy*
An Important Resident—a vocal opponent of the current Eyrie leadership—was found dead in the Gilded Lofts. The murder was clean, professional, and is being covered up. The ensuing paranoia is tearing the aristocracy apart, as everyone suspects their neighbors of hiring assassins.

**Additional Conflicts:**
*   *Siphoning to the Cause:* A sympathetic aristocrat is secretly funneling her family fortune to the Woodland Alliance. She’s left a dangerous paper trail and needs someone expendable (like Vagabonds) to break into the local bank and destroy the ledgers before the Eyrie auditors find them.
*   *The Exotic Menagerie Breakout:* A noble’s prized collection of Hercules beetles has been intentionally released during a gala, causing massive structural damage to the branch-walkways and trapping several elites.

### Important Residents
**Lady Elara Softpaws (Fox)**
A wealthy socialite who hosts the most exclusive galas in the canopy.
*   **Location:** Her sprawling estate, "The Sun-Dappled Terrace."
*   **Fear:** Losing her social standing and being cast down into the lower districts.
*   **Secret:** She is the one funding the Woodland Alliance, using her parties to launder the money.
*   **Request:** Infiltrate the Eyrie auditor's office to burn specific financial records. She offers high-society connections and a beautifully crafted rapier (Value 4, +valuable, +wear).
*   **Opinion:** Claims Lord Reginald (below) is deeply in debt and faking his wealth.

**Lord Reginald (Badger)**
A gruff, newly-rich merchant trying desperately to buy his way into the old money circles.
*   **Location:** The Platinum Club (an exclusive gentlemen's club).
*   **Fear:** Being humiliated in front of the established nobility.
*   **Secret:** His wealth comes from fencing goods stolen by the Magpie Syndicate.
*   **Request:** Steal a rare vintage of blackberry wine from a rival's cellar so he can serve it at his next dinner party. He offers a heavy purse of coin.
*   **Opinion:** Believes Lady Elara is hiding something massive behind her vapid socialite facade.

**Matriarch "Auntie" Corvid (Bird - The Rook Family)**
An elegant, elderly rook who seemingly just knits and gossips, but actually runs the Gilded Lofts' blackmail ring.
*   **Location:** The highest balcony of the district, always observing.
*   **Fear:** A full-scale war in the Valley disrupting her lucrative extortion rackets.
*   **Secret:** She orchestrated the recent murder in the canopy to silence a client who refused to pay.
*   **Request:** Deliver a sealed, black envelope to Mayor Silas without letting him know who sent it. Offers a favor from the Rook Family.
*   **Opinion:** Knows that Lord Reginald is fencing Syndicate goods, and finds it adorably amateurish.

### Important Locations
**The Grand Canopy Promenade**
A wide, reinforced bridge of woven branches suspended high above the ground, lined with luxury boutiques and heavily guarded by Eyrie soldiers. It is the primary place to see and be seen.

**The Gilded Aviary**
A luxurious tavern catering to the highest-ranking Eyrie officers and their sycophants. Gossip flows here as freely as the expensive imported wines.

### Special Rules
> [!tip] High Society Etiquette
> When you **try to blend in or extract information at a high-society function**, roll with Charm.
> **On a 10+**, you perfectly play the part; you gain access to restricted areas or learn a valuable secret without drawing suspicion.
> **On a 7-9**, you slip up slightly; you get what you want, but you offend an aristocrat (mark 1 notoriety with a faction) or must agree to an unsavory favor to maintain your cover.
> **On a 6-**, you are outed as an imposter and security is called.

## Tanglewood
Ancient, gnarled trees make up the main orchards harvested by denizens. The orchards are chaotic, dense, and it's incredibly easy to get lost. Arboreal spiders, honeybees, carnivorous plants, and fire ants thrive in the darker thickets.

**Control:** Woodland Alliance (Hidden camps) / Uncontrolled.
**Dominant Inhabitants:** Mice and Rabbits (Orchard workers).

### Conflicts
**Core Conflict:** *The White Rot*
The ancient orchards are becoming deeply infected with White Rot, a blight that turns the fruit to ash and the wood to dust. Famine is imminent if the harvest fails, and the workers are growing desperate as their livelihoods vanish.

**Additional Conflicts:**
*   *The Swarm's Envy:* A massive hive of honeybees has become hyper-aggressive, attacking anyone who tries to harvest the uninfected fruit. They seem almost organized in their defense of the remaining healthy trees.
*   *Lost in the Thicket:* A group of denizen children wandered too deep into the chaotic, unpruned sections of Tanglewood and haven't returned. The locals are too afraid of the arboreal spiders to mount a rescue.

### Important Residents
**Granny Pip (Mouse)**
An ancient, wizened mouse who has tended the orchards her whole life.
*   **Location:** A hollowed-out apple tree near the edge of the Tanglewood.
*   **Fear:** The White Rot will consume the entire forest and starve her people.
*   **Secret:** She knows the Rot isn't natural; she saw someone spreading it under the cover of night.
*   **Request:** Retrieve a rare, uninfected sapling from the deepest, spider-infested part of the woods so she can graft a cure. Offers a poultice that clears all exhaustion.
*   **Opinion:** Believes "Honest" Pip is a scoundrel, though she doesn't know the extent of his Corvid ties.

**Thorn (Rabbit)**
A hardened, scarred ranger and leader of the local Woodland Alliance cell hidden in the woods.
*   **Location:** A camouflaged camp deep within the gnarled roots.
*   **Fear:** The Eyrie will burn the orchards to root out his camp.
*   **Secret:** He provoked the honeybee swarms to create a natural defense perimeter around his camp.
*   **Request:** Ambush an Eyrie patrol before they find the trail to his camp. Offers stolen Eyrie armor (Value 2, +wear) and Alliance favor.
*   **Opinion:** Respects Granny Pip but thinks she's too old to see that war is the only solution.

**Silksong (Arboreal Spider - Monstrous NPC)**
A massive, intelligent, and surprisingly conversational spider that controls the deep thickets.
*   **Location:** The Deep Webs, in the darkest part of the canopy.
*   **Fear:** Fire, especially the torches carried by the Lord of Hundreds mobs.
*   **Secret:** She has captured the missing denizen children, but only to protect them from the White Rot she senses spreading.
*   **Request:** Bring her fresh, large prey (like a Marquise guard dog or a giant snake) to sate her brood, and she will release the children unharmed.
*   **Opinion:** Finds the Woodland Alliance loud and disruptive to her webs.

### Important Locations
**The Mother Tree**
The oldest and largest apple tree in the Valley. It is currently the epicenter of the White Rot infection, its massive trunk slowly turning pale and brittle.

**The Weeping Hollow**
A section of the orchard where the trees grow incredibly dense and carnivorous plants carpet the forest floor. It is practically a death trap for the unwary.

### Special Rules
> [!tip] Navigating the Tanglewood
> When you **try to find your way through the unpruned, shifting paths of Tanglewood**, roll with Focus.
> **On a 10+**, you navigate the hazards perfectly and find your destination quickly.
> **On a 7-9**, you find your way, but choose one:
> *   You stumble into a hazard (spiders, fire ants) and must suffer 1 harm (exhaustion or injury).
> *   You are delayed, giving your enemies time to prepare or act.
> *   You lose a piece of equipment to a carnivorous plant or deep mud (mark 1 wear).
>
> **On a 6-**, you are completely lost, stumbling directly into a monster's lair or an Alliance trap.

*   "Don't go near the deep webs. The spiders there don't just eat you, they talk to you first."
*   "The bees are guarding the last good apples. Someone must have trained them."

## The Deeps
A cavernous grotto set into the riverside, extending back into a network of partially explored ruins. It is inhabited by hermits, outcasts, and any others with a need for sheltered secrecy. The ruins are incredibly extensive and no part has been fully explored, but the first level is mostly safe.

**Control:** Lizard Cult (Spiritual influence) / Keepers in Iron (Securing relics).
**Dominant Inhabitants:** Reptiles (Snakes, Turtles, Lizards).

### Conflicts
**Core Conflict:** *Clash of Faith and Iron*
The Keepers in Iron have set up a heavily fortified waystation in the upper grotto, aggressively excavating the deeper ruins for relics. The Lizard Cult, who have maintained a sacred garden there for generations, view this as a desecration. Minor skirmishes are threatening to erupt into a bloody holy war within the enclosed caves.

**Additional Conflicts:**
*   *Eye of the Dragon:* A fringe group of Lizard Cult fanatics has awakened an enormous reptile deep in the ruins. They claim it will purge the wicked, but it is currently just rampaging and eating anyone it finds.
*   *The Sealed Door:* The Keepers have uncovered a massive stone vault door adorned with unknown runes. They cannot open it, but an ominous, rhythmic thudding can be heard from the other side, driving denizens mad with paranoia.

### Important Residents
**High Priestess Ssithis (Snake - Lizard Cult)**
A massive, ancient snake who commands absolute loyalty from the Cult's followers in the Deeps.
*   **Location:** The Sunlit Garden (a cavern opening to the sky).
*   **Fear:** The Keepers will destroy the sacred hatching grounds hidden deeper in the ruins.
*   **Secret:** She does not know how to control the "Dragon" her fanatics awakened and is terrified of it.
*   **Request:** Sabotage the Keepers' main excavation rig so they are forced to halt their digging. Offers access to the Cult's healing pools and a rare venom-coated dagger (Value 3, +valuable, +wear, *poison*).
*   **Opinion:** Believes the Keepers are actually fleeing something worse from the outside world.

**Warden Kaelen (Badger - Keepers in Iron)**
A stoic, scarred leader of the Keeper expedition, utterly devoted to recovering the Ancients' secrets.
*   **Location:** The Iron Waystation.
*   **Fear:** Returning to the Order empty-handed and being stripped of his rank.
*   **Secret:** He lost his best squad to the "Dragon" and is covering it up to maintain morale.
*   **Request:** Map the second level of the ruins and locate a specific relic before the Cult finds it. Offers heavy plate armor (Value 4, +wear, +valuable) and Keeper safe passage.
*   **Opinion:** Thinks High Priestess Ssithis is a charlatan using parlor tricks to control the weak-minded.

**Old Murk (Turtle)**
A crazy old hermit who claims to have mapped the entire first three levels of the ruins in his head.
*   **Location:** A cluttered hovel near the entrance to the Deeps.
*   **Fear:** The rhythmic thudding behind the sealed door will stop, meaning whatever is behind it is finally free.
*   **Secret:** He actually stole a minor relic from the Keepers and is using it to boil water for tea.
*   **Request:** Escort him past the Keeper blockades so he can return to his "true home" deeper in the caves. Offers a flawless, hand-drawn map of the upper ruins.
*   **Opinion:** Mutters that the Citadel above is going to collapse into the Deeps any day now.

### Important Locations
**The Sunlit Garden**
A massive cavern where the roof has caved in, allowing sunlight to stream down upon a lush, humid jungle of exotic plants. The Lizard Cult defends this area fiercely.

**The Echoing Chasm**
A seemingly bottomless pit near the Keeper excavation site. Dropping a stone yields no sound of impact, but occasionally, bizarre, mechanical screeching echoes up from the depths.

### Special Rules
> [!tip] Navigating the Dark Ruins
> When you **delve into the unexplored sections of the Deeps**, roll with Focus.
> **On a 10+**, you find your way safely and discover something useful (a shortcut, a minor trinket, or a safe resting spot).
> **On a 7-9**, you progress, but choose one:
> *   You trigger an ancient trap (suffer 1 injury).
> *   You stumble into a monster's territory (snakes, scorpions) and must fight or flee.
> *   Your light source fails or is lost (mark 1 depletion).
>
> **On a 6-**, you are completely lost in the pitch black, and something terrifying finds you.

*   "I heard a roar echoing from the grotto last night. Sounded like a bear, but... scaly."
*   "Old Murk knows the way to the treasure vault, but he charges a steep price: your sanity."

## Citadel
A sprawling stone palace originally constructed by the Ancients, inhabited continuously since by the Eyrie. It is a fortress of military might and bureaucratic gridlock.

**Control:** Eyrie Dynasties (Total control).
**Dominant Inhabitants:** Birds (Various species, highly regimented).

### Conflicts
**Core Conflict:** *The Erratic Eyrie*
The daughter of the Citadel's ruling house has disappeared from her quarters. The Eyrie leader has grown intensely paranoid, initiating random, brutal searches of shops and residences throughout the Valley. Martial law is looming, and the denizens are reaching their breaking point.

**Additional Conflicts:**
*   *Explosions in the Keep:* The Corvid Conspiracy has planted bombs within the Citadel’s outer walls, seeking to incite the Eyrie to attack the Marquise in Riverside by framing them for the sabotage.
*   *The Dungeons:* Marquise officers with knowledge of key Valley secrets are imprisoned deep within the Citadel's dungeons. Marquise leaders are desperate to hire Vagabonds to break them out before they crack under interrogation.
*   *Succession Crisis:* An ambitious Eyrie scion is secretly looking to hire assassins to remove the current, paranoid leader, believing he is the only one who can restore order.

### Important Residents
**Lord Commander Talon (Bird - House Strix)**
The current, highly paranoid ruler of the Citadel, desperate to find his missing daughter.
*   **Location:** The High Command Room.
*   **Fear:** His daughter is dead, and his rivals will use his grief to usurp him.
*   **Secret:** He knows his daughter wasn't kidnapped; she ran away because of his cruelty.
*   **Request:** Track down his daughter and bring her back, quietly. Offers an exorbitant sum of coin and a writ of absolute authority within the Valley.
*   **Opinion:** Blames the Corvid Conspiracy for the bombings but lacks the proof to execute them publicly.

**Lady Seraphina (Bird - House Condor)**
A ruthless, ambitious scion who believes Talon is unfit to rule and is plotting his downfall.
*   **Location:** The Aviary Gardens.
*   **Fear:** Talon's paranoia will cause the Eyrie to lose the Valley entirely.
*   **Secret:** She is the one who hired the Corvids to plant the bombs, hoping to frame Talon's leadership as incompetent.
*   **Request:** Assassinate Lord Commander Talon during his evening patrol. Offers a masterwork crossbow (Value 4, +valuable, +wear) and a permanent position in her new regime.
*   **Opinion:** Thinks Commander Aldous Kingfisher is too soft and needs to be purged from the ranks.

**Lieutenant Rook (Cat - Marquise Prisoner)**
A tough-as-nails Marquise officer currently enduring harsh interrogation in the dungeons.
*   **Location:** The Deep Dungeons.
*   **Fear:** He will break and reveal the Marquise's supply line routes.
*   **Secret:** He purposely got captured to map out the dungeon layout for a future Marquise assault.
*   **Request:** Break him out and escort him to Riverside. Offers a cache of hidden Marquise explosives (Value 3, +valuable).
*   **Opinion:** Swears the Eyrie guards are more afraid of what's beneath the dungeons (in the Deeps) than they are of the Marquise.

### Important Locations
**The Roosting Towers**
Massive, spired towers where the Eyrie elite reside. They are heavily guarded, opulent, and offer a vantage point over the entire Valley.

**The Black Iron Dungeons**
Located in the lowest levels of the Citadel, bordering the Deeps. It is cold, damp, and heavily fortified. It is said nobody escapes the Black Iron.

> **On a 6-**, your bluff is called. You are arrested and thrown into the Black Iron Dungeons.

## Bazaar
A chaotic, sprawling market extending below ground into an immense network of interconnected burrows. The main burrows and the above-ground square are well-lit day and night, but the Bazaar's labyrinthine alleys extend far beyond the manicured center, offering everything from fresh fruit to illicit weapons.

**Control:** The Magpie Syndicate (Economic dominance) / Riverfolk Company (Commercial enforcement).
**Dominant Inhabitants:** Mixed (Very diverse, many traveling merchants).

### Conflicts
**Core Conflict:** *The Counterfeit Crisis*
Riverfolk Company captains have discovered they are being paid in highly convincing false scrip, and have been for months. Enraged, they have begun foreclosing, evicting, and forcefully imprisoning denizens who fall behind on debts. The Magpie Syndicate, who orchestrated the counterfeiting, is using its newly acquired wealth to buy up the foreclosed properties at a fraction of their value, fueling a violent economic shadow war.

**Additional Conflicts:**
*   *The Protection Racket:* The Syndicate is aggressively expanding its protection rackets into the upper Bazaar. Honest merchants who refuse to pay are finding their stalls smashed or their goods tainted.
*   *The Stolen Cargo:* A massive shipment of Marquise weapons bound for Riverside was hijacked in the Bazaar. Both the Marquise and the Eyrie are tearing the market apart looking for it.

### Important Residents
**Don Corvus (Bird - Magpie Syndicate)**
The impeccably dressed, dangerously calm leader of the Syndicate in the Valley.

*   **Opinion:** Thinks the Eyrie are planning to burn the Bazaar down to root out the criminals.

### Important Locations
**The Sunken Square**
The massive, open-air center of the Bazaar. It is deafeningly loud, packed with stalls, and patrolled heavily by Riverfolk mercenaries enforcing contracts.

**The Undermarket**
A shadowy network of tunnels beneath the main Bazaar where illegal goods (poisons, stolen weapons, illicit relics) are openly traded.

### Special Rules
> [!tip] Haggling and Hustling
> When you **try to buy an illicit or rare item in the Bazaar**, roll with Finesse.
> **On a 10+**, you find exactly what you need at a fair price.
> **On a 7-9**, you find it, but choose one:

> *   It is slightly flawed or damaged (mark 1 wear immediately upon use).
> *   The transaction draws the attention of the Syndicate or Riverfolk.
>
> **On a 6-**, you are sold a convincing fake, or you are led into an ambush by thugs.

## The Warren
Nominally a Rabbit cultural district, this dizzying, three-dimensional network of burrows houses an enormous population of all kinds of denizens. It is tight, claustrophobic, and virtually impossible for outsiders to navigate.

**Control:** Woodland Alliance (Stronghold).
**Dominant Inhabitants:** Rabbits (Overwhelming majority).

### Conflicts
**Core Conflict:** *The Payroll Heist*
Woodland Alliance cells headquartered deep within the Warren are preparing to steal a month's worth of Marquise payroll from Riverside. The Marquise suspects a plot and has begun indiscriminately collapsing burrow entrances, threatening to bury hundreds of innocents alive in their attempts to crush the Alliance.

**Additional Conflicts:**
*   *The Flooded Tunnels:* A recent storm caused the Silver River to swell, flooding the lower levels of the Warren. The displaced denizens are crammed into the upper levels, causing food shortages and rising tensions.
*   *The Syndicate's Printing Press:* The Magpie Syndicate has hidden their massive counterfeit scrip printing press in an abandoned section of the Warren. The Alliance wants to destroy it, but doing so might collapse the tunnels above it.

### Important Residents
**Commander Hazel (Rabbit - Woodland Alliance)**
A fiery, uncompromising leader of the local Alliance cells, determined to break the factions' grip on the Valley.
*   **Location:** The War Room (deep underground).
*   **Fear:** The Marquise will collapse the entire Warren before she can launch her heist.
*   **Secret:** She is planning to use the stolen payroll to buy explosives from the Corvids to blow the Great Spanning Bridge.
*   **Request:** Map the patrol routes of the Marquise payroll guards in Riverside. Offers a brace of finely crafted Alliance pistols (Value 3, +valuable, +wear).
*   **Opinion:** Considers the denizens of the Gilded Lofts to be complicit in the Valley's suffering and deserving of whatever happens to them.

**Old Man Burrower (Rabbit)**
An eccentric, half-blind architect who claims to know every tunnel, collapsed or intact, in the entire Warren.
*   **Location:** A dusty, blueprint-filled hovel near the surface.
*   **Fear:** He will die before he finishes mapping the theoretical "Deepest Burrow."
*   **Secret:** He accidentally built a tunnel that connects directly into the Citadel's dungeons, and hasn't told anyone.
*   **Request:** Recover his lost glasses from a flooded section of the lower Warren. Offers a map of the secret tunnel into the Citadel.
*   **Opinion:** Mutters constantly that Commander Hazel is going to get them all killed with her foolish heist.

**"Whisper" (Mouse - Syndicate Enforcer)**
A quiet, menacing Syndicate operative tasked with guarding the counterfeit printing press.
*   **Location:** The Abandoned Tunnels.
*   **Fear:** The Woodland Alliance will discover the press and overwhelm him with sheer numbers.
*   **Secret:** He hates Don Corvus and is skimming a large percentage of the counterfeit scrip for himself.
*   **Request:** Eliminate an Alliance scout who is getting too close to the press. Offers a massive payout in (genuine) coin.
*   **Opinion:** Believes the Riverfolk Company is too stupid to ever find the source of the counterfeiting on their own.

### Important Locations
**The Grand Hearth**
A massive, central chamber where dozens of tunnels converge. It serves as a community center, mess hall, and rallying point for the denizens of the Warren.

**The Drowned Tunnels**
The lower levels of the Warren, currently completely submerged. They are dangerous, structurally unsound, and rumored to hide lost treasures from those who had to flee quickly.

### Special Rules
> [!tip] Navigating the Warren
> When you **try to quickly navigate the claustrophobic, three-dimensional maze of the Warren**, roll with Might (to push through crowds) or Finesse (to squeeze through tight shortcuts).
> **On a 10+**, you navigate the maze perfectly and arrive at your destination quickly.
> **On a 7-9**, you make it, but choose one:
> *   You are exhausted by the climb/squeeze (mark 1 exhaustion).
> *   You end up in a slightly wrong, potentially dangerous location first.
> *   You drop or damage something in a tight squeeze (mark 1 wear).
>
> **On a 6-**, you are hopelessly lost in the dark, or you trigger a tunnel collapse.

### Rumors & Hooks
*   "The Marquise collapsed three more tunnels today. How long until they bury us all?"
*   "Hazel is planning something big. I saw her hauling crates of black powder down into the deep warrens."
*   "Don't go into the abandoned tunnels. I heard rhythmic thumping down there, like a giant machine."

## Riverside
A district of heavy industry, impoverished housing, and grim company headquarters. The air here is thick with woodsmoke, and the constant grinding of sawmills drowns out the flow of the Silver River.

**Control:** Marquise de Cat (Industrial control).
**Dominant Inhabitants:** Mixed (Primarily laborers, Foxes, and Cats).

### Conflicts
**Core Conflict:** *The Imprisoned Officers*
Marquise officers with knowledge of key secrets regarding the Riverside supply lines are imprisoned within the Citadel's dungeons. Marquise leaders are desperate and fear the Eyrie will soon break them and learn the secrets. They seek out Vagabonds to break the officers out, but the resulting extraction threatens to turn Riverside into a battlefield if the Eyrie pursue them.

**Additional Conflicts:**
*   *The Sabotaged Mills:* Woodland Alliance saboteurs are actively destroying Marquise sawmills. The Marquise has responded with brutal crackdowns on the labor force, sparking near-riots.
*   *The Tainted River:* Runoff from the Marquise industry and secret Corvid chemical dumps have severely polluted this stretch of the river, causing illness among the laborers and angering the Riverfolk Company.

### Important Residents
**Foreman Briggs (Cat - Marquise de Cat)**
A brutal, overworked Marquise taskmaster in charge of the primary sawmills.
*   **Location:** The Great Mill.
*   **Fear:** The Alliance will destroy his mill, and the Marquise will execute him for failure.
*   **Secret:** He is suffering from the chemical pollution in the river and is slowly dying, making him increasingly desperate.
*   **Request:** Hunt down and eliminate the Alliance saboteurs operating in Riverside. Offers heavy Marquise weapons (Value 3, +wear, *cleave*) and free access to Marquise smithies.
*   **Opinion:** Hates Captain Vespera for caring more about her family than the Marquise's hold on the Valley.

**"Sooty" Sal (Fox)**
A cynical, soot-stained laborer who acts as the unofficial union leader for the Riverside workers.
*   **Location:** The Slag Heap (a cheap, grimy tavern).
*   **Fear:** The Marquise will massacre the workers if they actually attempt to strike.
*   **Secret:** She knows exactly where the Corvid chemical dumps are, but keeps quiet because they pay her well to look the other way.
*   **Request:** Smuggle a crate of medicine out of the Bazaar to treat the sick workers. Offers a pouch of hidden coin and the absolute loyalty of the Riverside laborers.
*   **Opinion:** Thinks the Woodland Alliance cares more about hurting the Marquise than actually helping the workers.

**Agent Nightshade (Bird - Corvid Conspiracy)**
A seemingly low-level Marquise clerk who is actually orchestrating the chemical dumping to destabilize the district.
*   **Location:** The Logistics Office.
*   **Fear:** The Eyrie will capture the Marquise officers and discover the Corvid plot hidden within the supply lines.
*   **Secret:** She planted the evidence that got the Marquise officers captured by the Eyrie in the first place.
*   **Request:** Ensure the imprisoned Marquise officers die in the Citadel dungeons before they can talk. Offers unmarked, untraceable gold and Corvid protection.
*   **Opinion:** Finds Foreman Briggs’s slow demise from the pollution highly amusing.

### Important Locations
**The Great Mill**
A massive, terrifying structure of spinning blades and roaring furnaces. It is the heart of Marquise industry in the Valley and heavily guarded.

**The Slag Heap**
A decrepit, filthy tavern where the laborers drink away their meager pay. It is a hotbed of resentment, Alliance recruitment, and desperate deals.

### Special Rules
> [!tip] Industrial Hazards

> **On a 10+**, you use the environment to your advantage, trapping an enemy or escaping cleanly.
> **On a 7-9**, you succeed, but you or a companion must dodge a spinning blade, scalding steam, or falling timber (suffer 1 injury or mark 1 wear on armor).
> **On a 6-**, you are cornered against a lethal hazard, or your weapon is caught and destroyed by the machinery.

## Red Ward
A rotting, desperately impoverished slum located at a sharp bend in the Silver River downstream of the rest of the city. The ward catches all the refuse, sewage, and chemical runoff discarded by the upper districts. It is a harsh, miserable place infested with fire ants, honeybees, hercules beetles, carnivorous plants, scorpions, hornets, and snakes.

**Control:** Lord of Hundreds (Mob rule).
**Dominant Inhabitants:** Mice (Overwhelming, desperate majority).

### Conflicts
**Core Conflict:** *The Uprising*
The Hundreds mobs in the Red Ward have grown massive and increasingly violent, driven mad by starvation, pollution, and the fiery rhetoric of their Warlord. They threaten to spill out of the slums and burn key districts (Riverside and the Bazaar) to the ground. An uprising here will lead to a massacre that sparks an all-out war involving all factions.

**Additional Conflicts:**
*   *The Plague:* A sickening plague, causing fur and feathers to shed violently before death, is sweeping through the Red Ward. Factions are blockading the ward instead of helping, and the denizens are rioting for a cure.
*   *The Swarm Infestation:* The refuse piling up in the ward has attracted monstrous swarms of fire ants and hornets, making entire streets impassable and forcing the mobs into even tighter quarters.

### Important Residents
**Warlord Scorch (Mouse - Lord of Hundreds)**
A charismatic, terrifyingly zealous mouse covered in ash and scars, leading the mobs.
*   **Location:** The Pyre (a massive, constantly burning pile of refuse in the center of the ward).
*   **Fear:** The mobs will realize he has no actual plan for feeding them after they burn the city.
*   **Secret:** He is completely immune to the Plague ravaging the ward, and believes it makes him a god.
*   **Request:** Assassinate the Marquise commander blockading the ward (Captain Vespera) so the mob can cross the bridge. Offers the title of 'Chosen' and an artifact weapon looted from the Deeps (Value 5, +valuable, +wear, *fiery*).
*   **Opinion:** Screams that the Eyrie in the Gilded Lofts deserve to be burned alive in their canopies.

**Sister Amara (Mouse)**
A desperate, exhausted healer trying to cure the Plague with scavenged herbs and zero resources.
*   **Location:** The House of the Shedding (a makeshift, overflowing quarantine tent).
*   **Fear:** She will catch the Plague and die before finding a cure.
*   **Secret:** She discovered that a specific type of White Rot fungus from Tanglewood can be distilled into a cure, but she can't reach it.
*   **Request:** Brave the Tanglewood and bring her a massive sample of the White Rot fungus. Offers a powerful, experimental healing tonic (clears all injury and exhaustion, but causes hallucinations).
*   **Opinion:** Believes Warlord Scorch is intentionally spreading the Plague to make the denizens more desperate.

**"Gutter" (Rat - Outcast)**
A massive, scarred rat who survives by hunting the giant scorpions and snakes that infest the refuse piles.
*   **Location:** The River Catch (where the garbage piles up).
*   **Fear:** A swarm of fire ants will finally overwhelm him while he sleeps.
*   **Secret:** He found a cache of highly advanced Ancient technology buried in the sludge, but doesn't know how to use it.
*   **Request:** Help him clear out a nest of giant scorpions so he can safely excavate the Ancient cache. Offers first pick of the Ancient artifacts (Value 4, +valuable).
*   **Opinion:** Thinks the Lord of Hundreds are just noisy children playing with fire.

### Important Locations
**The Pyre**
A massive, ever-burning mountain of trash, effigies, and stolen goods. It is the religious and military center for the Lord of Hundreds in the Valley.

**The Blockade Bridge**
The only sturdy crossing out of the Red Ward, currently heavily fortified by a terrifying alliance of Marquise and Eyrie soldiers explicitly ordered to shoot anyone trying to leave.

### Special Rules
> [!tip] The Filth and the Fury
> When you **spend significant time in the Red Ward without proper protection**, roll with Fortitude.
> **On a 10+**, you endure the oppressive stench, pollution, and disease without issue.
> **On a 7-9**, you are affected by the miserable conditions; mark 1 exhaustion or 1 depletion (as your supplies are tainted or stolen).
> **On a 6-**, you contract a mild strain of the Plague (mark 1 injury that cannot be healed until you receive specific medical treatment) or are swarmed by fire ants.

*   "Sister Amara is a saint, but she's losing her mind. She told me the rot in the trees is the only cure for the rot in our blood."
