RIVERFOLK COMPANY
**Captain Mondo Yafir**
*Riverfolk Company Skipper*
Drive: To turn a massive profit by walking the razor's edge between completely legal trade and outright piracy.
*A grizzled, eccentric possum wearing a hodgepodge of stolen naval uniforms from various factions. He has a wooden peg leg and a perpetually manic grin. He gestures wildly with a rusted spyglass when he speaks.*

**Equipment**
- Heavy Cutlass (Close, 2-injury, +heavy)
- A highly inaccurate but terrifying hand-cannon (Close, 3-injury, +loud, +reload)
- A sturdy, patched-up Riverfolk trade boat
- Forged customs manifests for three different factions

**Found at:** The docks of a major river clearing, loudly haggling over cargo, or holding court in a waterfront tavern.

**Found doing:** Swearing that his clearly stolen goods are "legitimate salvage," enthusiastically ripping off a naive merchant, or drinking his crew under the table.

**Moves**
- Produce a vaguely legal document to justify an illegal act
- Abandon a sinking ship (or failing deal) with surprising speed
- Betray an ally, but frame it as a necessary, tragic business decision

**Movement Anchor:** Light, Sudden, Indirect, Free energy

**Center:** Wildly gesturing hands

**Key Facts**
1. He runs an absolutely, completely legal operation that is totally above board in every way (according to him, and only him).
2. He is a charismatic survivor who will talk his way out of any situation, playing the fool when it suits him and a ruthless pirate when it doesn't.

**Immediate Pressure:** A [[Marquise de Cat|Marquise]] river patrol has blockaded his usual smuggling route, and his current cargo of "liberated" Eyrie weapons is becoming a massive liability.

**Persuasion Tactics:**
1. Enthusiastic, overwhelming charm and blatant lies
2. Promising ridiculous, impossible profits

**Social Status:** 2 - Lower (Technically a merchant, treated like a pirate)

**Perception of Vagabonds:** *"Ah, my favorite kind of business partners! Expendable, deniable, and usually willing to work for a fraction of what a professional crew demands!"*
