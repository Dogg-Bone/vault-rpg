> [!quote] from Travelers & Outsiders:
> The Grand Duchy arose from a group of disparate mole domains, all tired of what they perceived as oppression by surface-dwelling denizens. As the Duchy formed, it created a series of ministerial roles to oversee different aspects of its newly formed government. The Duchy hoped it would promote unity as well as ensuring no one mole was in charge of all the others. 
> As the Duchy grew into its own, the ministers felt they knew best and refused to act unless action served their interests. What was supposed to be a system of checks and balances turned into a race to garner favors and support in order to get anything done. This codified into the Grand Duchy a highly political atmosphere, where actions take a great deal of haggling and planning to coalesce. The Duchy’s motivations never wavered, though. It will never again bend to surface-creature whims, and its preference would be for the Duchy to just rule everyone.
> Most denizens rarely see a Duchy citizen, but they are around. Those denizens who travel the less trodden paths, or who have illicit dealings, find them at the head of small smuggling operations, using their tunnels to smuggle goods and people between clearings.

Corpulent, slow, and nearly blind, the moles of the Underground Duchy once lived in fear of the surface dwellers, but have used the unique gifts of their species to become a force for the surface to fear. Their tunnels extend beneath more clearings than any outsider knows. Combine that with their shrewd intellects, a culture foreign to surface-dwellers, and near limitless quantities of wealth excavated from the earth, and it is no mystery how the Duchy intimidate the inevitable colonial intentions of [[Le Monde de Cat]].

Moles look out for their own, but regard other species as inferior to themselves. Their myths place Molekind among the divine. They find ways to show the other [[Denizens]] of the marches their superiority by surrounding themselves with opulent and gaudy displays of wealth. They refuse to act unless action benefits themselves, or another Mole is threatened or wronged by a surface-dweller. Their disregard for lesser species' rights and interests makes their domain a lawless place. A Mole may act with impunity as long as they do not cross a Mole of superior status, and non-Moles that cross the Duchy are punished. Many Moles treat revenge as a sort of cultural pastime. 

Those who work for the Duchy are well paid, and there are many benefits to a role in a powerful Mole's entourage. Acting against a servant of the Duchy risks the ire of ruthless powers. Those fleeing other factions can often find a place to hide in the shadowy tunnels of the Duchy. Illicit and unsavory business finds an accommodating home in the territories under Duchy control. The [[Talpid March]] is almost entirely controlled by members of the Duchy and is home to the [[Grand Parliament of the Great Underground Duchy]]. Various Moles operate in other marches as well, extending the Duchy's influence into every crack and crevice.
