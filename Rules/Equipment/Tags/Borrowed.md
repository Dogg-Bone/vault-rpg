> [!danger] Borrowed
> This item is on loan from a high-status benefactor and must eventually be returned. When you create an item with this tag, name the faction, and the GM names the benefactor. Your benefactor is always able to find you, and has many envoys at their disposal. When time passes, one comes to collect; roll with Luck. On a hit, the envoy asks for a favor; do it, and you can keep the item. On a 10+, the envoy also gives you a chance to purchase the item instead; the GM will tell you the cost. On a miss, you must return the item or face grave consequences. This tag refunds 6-Value instead of 1-Value. (Anything)
