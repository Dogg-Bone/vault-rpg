- **Thrills:** Advance when you escape from certain death or incarceration.
- **Crime:** Advance when you illicitly score a significant prize or pull off an illegal caper against impressive odds.

### Playbook Moves
Choose three:

> [!tip] Explosive Personality
> When you *wreck something* with flagrantly dangerous means (explosives, uncontrolled flame, etc.), roll with Luck instead of Might.

> [!tip] Create to Destroy
> When you **use available materials to rig up a dangerous device**, roll with Finesse. On a hit, you cobble together something that will do what you want, one time. On a 10+, choose one. On a 7-9, choose two. The device is:
> • more dangerous than intended
> • larger or more unwieldy than intended
> • more temperamental and fragile than intended
> On a miss, you need some vital component to finish it; the GM will tell you what.

> [!tip] It's a Distraction!
> You gain the roguish feat *Blindside* (it does not count against your limit). When you *attempt a roguish feat* to blindside someone while they are distracted by environmental dangers (a raging fire, an oncoming flood, etc.), roll with Luck instead of Cunning.

> [!tip] Daredevil
> You're at your luckiest when you go into danger without hesitation. When you **dive into a dangerous situation without forethought or planning**, treat yourself as having "Luck Armor," with 1 box of wear (remember, armor is only "destroyed" when you would mark another box of wear, and all its boxes are full). The "Luck Armor" automatically goes away once the danger has passed, and the next time you would have "Luck Armor," you gain it as if it was brand new with clear boxes.

> [!tip] Danger Mask
> You have a mask or outfit you wear when you go about your most destructive work—more of a calling card, an identifier of "the real you," than a disguise. Treat it as a piece of equipment with two boxes of wear. While you have your mask on, any notoriety you gain is doubled, any prestige you gain is halved, and take +1 to *trust fate* and all Scoundrel playbook moves. If your mask is ever taken from you, mark exhaustion. If your mask is ever destroyed, mark 4-exhaustion. If your mask is destroyed, you can make a new mask when time passes.

> [!tip] Better Lucky than Good
> When you **use a weapon move (basic or skilled)**, mark exhaustion to roll with Luck instead of the listed stat.

### Notes on Playbook Moves

For *Explosive Personality*, if your flagrantly dangerous means are liable to explode in your face before you can use them, the GM will probably tell you to make a different move first, like *trust fate* or *try a roguish feat*.

For *Create to Destroy*, what you create doesn't have to literally be an explosive, but it's bound to be dangerous in some way. If you pick "more temperamental and fragile than intended," then the device is liable to trigger on its own, at an inopportune moment, or fall apart right when you need it. If you pick "larger or more unwieldy," then the device is going to be hard to move, plant, or hide. If you pick "more dangerous than intended," the actual effects of the device will be larger or more terrible than you wanted, probably causing massive collateral damage.

For *It's a Distraction!*, you only gain the benefit of the move if the targets of the move are distracted by the environmental dangers. If they're completely ignoring the raging fire—somehow—then the move won't apply.

For *Daredevil*, if a move or effect forces you to ignore armor, you must ignore Luck Armor, too. Also, you can only gain the benefit of Luck Armor if you genuinely dive in without forethought or planning; if you sat outside the keep discussing your plan for ten minutes, no Luck Armor for you.

For *Danger Mask*, if you take it off, it's just a normal mask—anyone could put it on. Putting it on or taking it off takes just a moment. As long as you have the mask on, you're wearing it, even if someone else can see through it, pull it up, etc. If it's taken from you and you aren't wearing it, mark the exhaustion as soon as you find out you lost it; if it's destroyed while you aren't wearing it, mark the 4-exhaustion as soon as you find out it's destroyed. Remember that armor is destroyed when its wear track is full and you are forced to mark wear again; you don't take 4-exhaustion just for filling its wear track.
