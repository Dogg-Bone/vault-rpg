## Location
Found wandering the clearings under Marquise control, surrounded by a pair of heavily armed guards, meticulously checking ledgers.

## Fear
Being audited themselves by a superior. They are skimming off the top and terrified of being caught.

## Secret
They have stashed a small fortune of "lost" taxes in a hollowed-out stump near the edge of the clearing.

## Request for the Vagabonds
"My ledger was stolen by some ruffians—or so I'll claim. If you can retrieve it before the Duke's inspectors arrive tomorrow, I will pay you handsomely from my... private reserve."

## Opinion/Info
"The commander of the local garrison? A fool. He spends more time polishing his sword than patrolling the borders. I wouldn't trust him to guard a turnip cart."
