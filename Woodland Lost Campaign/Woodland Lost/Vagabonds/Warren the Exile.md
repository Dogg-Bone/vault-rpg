A grasshopper mouse, son of the last [[Lord of Hundreds]] and next in line for the title. Warren has been on the run as a Vagabond ever since his father was murdered by his uncle [[Ramus Darkfur]]. His personal insignia is the [[Lord of Hundreds]] mark, crossed out. Personal friends with the Tinker [[Galen Erso]].
