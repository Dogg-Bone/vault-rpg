The [[Sibilant Snake]] is harassing timber shipments on the river leaving Swanholde.
[[Warren the Exile]] counterfeited [[Lord of Hundreds]] messages and sold them to Smiles in exchange for [[Galen Erso]]'s box - see [[Jyn Erso Rescue]]. He sold the other false message to Slip the fox, then spent the next few nights on a cot in a barren burrow provided by Slip. He told Slip his intentions of breaking into Ironwood Mill, and promised Slip to retrieve evidence of Captain Aridara's side hustle as blackmail from her office in exchange for help preparing (clearing harm) and a payday afterward. 

Before meeting Slip, Warren followed and knocked out Kaelen the fox, who bit Warren's tail. Kaelen was carrying a small bag of gunpowder, which Warren took. Warren then planted hallucinogenic mushrooms on Kaelen to discredit him in case he tells others what happened.

Warren is staking out Ironwood Mill and making a plan to infiltrate it to rescue [[Jyn Erso]].
