Concealed within a deep, mist-shrouded swamp illuminated by eerie bioluminescent flora, Murkwater is a secretive and foreboding clearing primarily home to mice. The [[Marquise de Cat|Marquisate]] controls the area, training elite troops in the harsh terrain. The mice are kept in line through strict martial law. The Marquise have expropriated local's homes for quartering troops and commandeered clearing resources.

The [[Keepers in Iron]] have arrived in force and are exploring a ruin adjacent to the clearing, too close for comfort to the [[Marquise de Cat|Marquise]] leader's eyes. Additionally, a [[Lord of Hundreds]] contingent has arrived in the clearing seeking an agreement to march jointly on an Eyrie stronghold.
