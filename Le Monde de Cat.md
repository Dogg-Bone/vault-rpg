Le Monde de Cat is the Cat Empire, literally, "The World of Cat." Le Monde is the dominant power of the [[Root Continent]]'s eastern reach. 
[[Carte du Monde]]
The center of the empire's power is [[La Capitale]], a sprawling megacity built around the enormous [[Palais Magnifique]], the [[Empereur]]'s opulent and impenetrable residence.
Beyond La Capitale, the Le Monde divides the empire into five marches:
- [[North March]]
- [[Saurian March]]
- [[Anuran March]]
- [[Talpid March]]
- [[Avian March]]
## Marquis
These marches are each governed by a [[Marquise de Cat]], who reports directly to the Empereur. Within his or her assigned march, a Marquis has practically absolute power. Each Marquis commands vast armies and resources to secure the marches and incentivize their productivity. Their most key responsibility is to ensure the continuous, consistent output of their respective marches. Each Marquis employs one or more Marechaux to lead the armies of the march. The armies of the individual marches differ in composition and training, and therefore maintain a variety of hierarchies below the Marechaux. 
## Senechaux
Nominally administrators, the Senechaux function as the imperial secret police. Their inquisitorial role is to identify and punish dissent, treason, and corruption at every level of Le Monde. Their political power rivals the Marquis. They operate with little oversight and are provided with whatever access or resources they require to ascertain the loyalty of Le Monde's servants.
