**Le Monde de Cat** (literally, "The World of Cat") is the grand, sprawling Cat Empire and the uncontested dominant power of the [[Root Continent]]'s eastern reach. Renowned for its industrial might, complex bureaucracy, and formidable military presence, Le Monde casts a long shadow over the continent.

## Geography & The Five Marches
The sheer scale of Le Monde is staggering, a stratified patchwork of ancient ruined kingdoms paved over by cobblestone, industrial scaffolding, and imperial monuments.

The undisputed center of the empire's power is [[La Capitale]], a sprawling, smog-choked megacity built upon the bones of older, forgotten metropolises. At its heart lies the enormous [[Palais Magnifique]], the [[Empereur]]'s opulent and impenetrable residence, which serves as a stark contrast to the churning factories below.

Beyond La Capitale, Le Monde divides its vast territorial holdings into five distinct regions known as marches. These territories encompass a massive variety of climates, peoples, and perils:
- **[[North March]]**
- **[[Saurian March]]**
- **[[Anuran March]]**
- **[[Talpid March]]**
- **[[Avian March]]**

## Governance and Hierarchies

### The Marquis & Marechaux
The marches are each governed by a **[[Marquise de Cat]]** (or Marquis), acting as imperial governors who report directly to the Empereur. Within their assigned march, a Marquis wields practically absolute power. Their most vital responsibility is ensuring the continuous, consistent, and uninterrupted economic output of their respective territories.

To achieve this, each Marquis commands vast armies and resources to secure their borders and ruthlessly incentivize productivity. A Marquis will employ one or more **Marechaux** to lead the armies of the march. Because the climates and threats of the marches vary so wildly, the armies of individual marches differ greatly in composition, tactics, and training, and therefore maintain a variety of complex military hierarchies below the rank of Marechaux.

### The Senechaux
While the Marquis control the armies and resources, the shadows of the empire are governed by the **Senechaux**. Nominally operating as administrators and auditors, the Senechaux function in reality as the imperial secret police.

Their inquisitorial mandate is to ruthlessly identify and punish dissent, treason, and corruption at every level of Le Monde. Operating with terrifyingly little oversight, they are provided with whatever access, resources, or legal authority they require to ascertain the absolute loyalty of Le Monde's servants. Consequently, their political power deeply rivals—and often threatens—that of the Marquis, creating a constant, silent war of espionage and leverage within the empire's own ranks.
