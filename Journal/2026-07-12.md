# [[Woodland Lost]] Campaign Update
In the last session, [[Warren the Exile]] broke into [[Ironwood Mill]] and rescued [[Jyn Erso|Jyn]] from imprisonment. He won her trust by showing her the device that contained a message from her father [[Galen Erso|Galen]]. In the course of the rescue, [[Warren the Exile|Warren]] cut off the ear of a conscript named Klaus, fought off [[Captain Aridara]] (who fled). He also sacked Aridara's office, and stole her fancy sword and a large snakeskin pelt that was hanging on the wall. Warren made sure to leave his personal mark in multiple places to claim responsibility for the heist.
