# House Kestrel

**Species:** Kestrel
**Focus:** Numbers, Populism, and Agility
**Naming Convention:** Celtic

## Overview
House Kestrel is the swarming, chaotic heart of the Eyrie Dynasties' lower echelons. What they lack in the sheer martial power of the Strix or the political cunning of the Mallards, they make up for in overwhelming numbers and fierce, communal loyalty. They are the foot soldiers, the fast outriders, and the zealous believers in the Eyrie's divine right to rule. They are highly adaptable and view the rigid traditions of the older Houses as suffocating.

## NPC Leader: Chieftain Rhys Kestrel
Rhys is a wiry, high-energy kestrel with a tattered cloak and a habit of pacing restlessly. He leads from the front and is beloved by the common soldiers.
- **Location:** The center of a massive, sprawling encampment in a newly captured or highly contested clearing, surrounded by squawking soldiers.
- **Fear:** That the aristocratic leaders of the other Houses view his people merely as acceptable losses in a war they won't survive.
- **Secret:** Rhys has a sibling who defected to the Woodland Alliance, and he secretly sends them supplies to keep them safe, an act of treason against the Eyrie.
- **Request for the Vagabonds:** He needs Vagabonds to quietly extract a squad of Kestrel scouts pinned down behind enemy lines, without alerting the Strix commanders who ordered them there as bait. Compensation: A flock of trained messenger birds the Vagabonds can use to communicate across the Woodland, and the permanent loyalty of the Kestrel rank-and-file.
- **Opinion/Info:** He considers House Strix to be "stiff-necked fools who would rather die in formation than win by adapting," though he fears their discipline.

## Brief History
House Kestrel was historically treated as second-class citizens within the Dynasties, relegated to border patrols and skirmishing duties. During the collapse, their sheer numbers allowed them to survive while smaller, elite Houses were decimated. In the modern era of the Eyrie's resurgence, their numbers make them indispensable. They have recently begun demanding a louder voice in the viziers' council, causing friction with the traditionalist Houses who view them as upstarts.

## Notable Facts, Connections, and Resources
- **The Swarm:** In battle, House Kestrel relies on speed and overwhelming numbers, performing hit-and-run tactics that frustrate the slower, heavily armored Marquisate forces.
- **Populist Appeal:** Because they live closer to the ground and interact more with the common denizens of the Woodland, House Kestrel often has better relations with non-bird locals than the rest of the Eyrie.
- **Agility:** They are unmatched aerial acrobats, capable of navigating dense canopy that would ground larger birds like Owls or Condors.
- **Rivalry:** They deeply resent House Condor's despotic rule and House Strix's rigid, unyielding military hierarchy, preferring a more egalitarian, tribal structure.
- **Resources:** They control the largest pool of manpower within the Eyrie, and possess an extensive network of low-level informants and lookouts throughout the canopy.
