# House Finch

**Species:** Finch
**Focus:** Scholarship, History, and Information Gathering
**Naming Convention:** Greek

## Overview
House Finch is the intellectual core of the Eyrie Dynasties. While other Houses train soldiers or build physical fortresses, the Finches build libraries and archive the vast, sprawling history of the Woodland. They are the scribes, scholars, and historians who believe that those who control the past dictate the future. They possess an insatiable curiosity and are functionally independent, maintaining their own hidden archives and a massive network of inconspicuous observers to gather knowledge.

## NPC Leader: Archivist Theron of House Finch
Theron is an elderly, bespectacled finch whose plumage is perpetually dusted with chalk and old parchment flakes. He is absent-minded about daily routines but has a flawless memory for historical treaties and ancient lineages.
- **Location:** Deep within a heavily fortified, hidden repository built into the roots of an ancient tree, surrounded by towering stacks of scrolls.
- **Fear:** That the Marquisate's rapid industrialization will inadvertently destroy a buried Woodland relic or ancient text before it can be recovered and cataloged.
- **Secret:** Theron has actively hidden historical evidence that contradicts the Eyrie's claim of "divine right" to rule the Woodland, believing the stability of the Dynasties is more important than absolute historical truth.
- **Request for the Vagabonds:** He needs Vagabonds to infiltrate a high-society Marquisate gala to transcribe the contents of an old, decorative tapestry hanging in the governor's hall before it is shipped out of the Woodland. Compensation: Access to House Finch's restricted historical archives and a hefty sum of coin.
- **Opinion/Info:** He views House Condor as "anti-intellectual thugs who burn libraries out of ignorance," but avoids direct confrontation by simply hiding his most valuable archives from them.

## Brief History
House Finch has always served as the record-keepers of the Dynasties. During the years of civil war, while other Houses bled each other for control of clearings, House Finch quietly retreated to obscure corners of the Woodland to protect the Eyrie's accumulated knowledge. Because they presented no direct military threat, they were largely ignored by the warring factions. When the Marquisate invaded, it was House Finch who used ancient maps to guide the fragmented Eyrie forces to forgotten, defensible roosts, cementing their current value to the Dynasties.

## Notable Facts, Connections, and Resources
- **The Silent Observers:** Because of their small size and unassuming nature, Finches make excellent, passive spies. They do not assassinate or sabotage; they simply watch, listen, and record, creating a vast database of Woodland activities.
- **Keepers of Lore:** They are unmatched in their knowledge of the Woodland's geography, ancient ruins, and the historical precedents of the various factions.
- **Independent Archives:** While they frequently share strategic intelligence with House Strix or diplomatic history with House Mallard, House Finch maintains total control over their own archives, deciding what information to release and what to bury.
- **Rivalry:** They have a deep-seated, intellectual rivalry with the Corvid Conspiracy. While the Corvids deal in rumors, blackmail, and lies, House Finch deals in absolute facts, frequently working to expose Corvid plots.
- **Resources:** They control the largest remaining collection of pre-collapse Eyrie literature, ancient maps, and have the most comprehensive, albeit slow-moving, intelligence network in the Woodland.
