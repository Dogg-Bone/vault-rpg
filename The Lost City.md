The Lost City is the common name for the arcology know in former times as Serpent's Grave, a name given by an ancient [[Eyrie Dynasties|Eyrie]] king. It had been inhabited for centuries by [[Lizard Cult|lizardfolk]] before being captured and was the capitol city of an ancient nation of lizardfolk. It bears many strata of continuous and shifting inhabitance, blending architectural styles of mouse, fox, rabbit, and lizard origin, along with older structures of markedly different provenance.

The city itself is enormous tripling the size of any normal clearing in the modern woodland, and has been filled to the brim with all manner of denizen since during the [[Eyrie Golden Age]]. Serpent's Grave served as the seat of Eyrie power and intrigue for centuries, well into the golden age.
