Southeast of [[La Capitale]] lies a sandstone desert. Hot, dry, and prone to sandstorms, those that call this region home are tough, clever, or especially suited to a scorching sun.

Primarily inhabited by [[Lizardfolk]], the march is also home to burrowing nocturnal rodents such as meerkats and rats, owls, fennec foxes, and some squirrels. The wilder regions are home to monstrous [[Giant Spider|Tarantulas]], [[Giant Snake]]s, [[Giant Fire Ants]], [[Giant Antlion|Antlions]], [[Giant Centipede]]s, and even [[Giant Crocodile]]s along the sparse rivers and watering holes.
