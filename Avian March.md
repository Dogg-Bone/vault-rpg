# Avian March (The Woodland)

> *"They call it a 'March' as if they've tamed it. It is merely a line drawn on a map by bureaucrats who have never heard the trees scream when the darkness falls. The Woodland belongs to no one but itself."*
> — A wandering Arbiter

> *"The Avian March represents the untamed potential of the West. It is unruly, yes, and teeming with insubordinate elements, but the resources beneath that canopy will fund La Capitale for a century. We need only harvest it."*
> — A Senechal's report to the Marquis

> *"They call it the Avian March, a name they stole. This is The Woodland, our birthright. We ruled these roosts long before the Cats arrived, and we will rule them long after they are gone."*
> — An Eyrie Commander

Located directly to the west of [[La Capitale]], the **Avian March** is an expansive, ancient, and deeply contested region. While imperial cartographers refer to it strictly as the Avian March, it is known to its inhabitants, and to much of the wider world, simply as **[[The Woodland]]**.

It is a place of deep, old-growth forests, ancient overgrown ruins, and isolated clearings, characterized by a constant, simmering tension between imperial ambitions, rebel factions, and the primal forces of the forest itself.

## Demographics & Inhabitants
The Woodland is a melting pot of species and cultures, representing one of the most diverse demographics in the entire empire. Historically, this march has been home to large populations of birds, foxes, rabbits, and mice.

However, political shifts and environmental disasters across the continent have led to a massive influx of recent arrivals, drastically changing the social landscape. The march now hosts significant populations of **[[Frogs]]**, **[[Corvids]]**, and **[[Lizardfolk]]**, alongside some of almost every sort of known **[[Denizens]]**. This dense mixing of cultures frequently leads to friction, making the clearings of the Avian March hotbeds of political intrigue and rebellion.

## Geography & Hazards
The geography of the Avian March is defined by its thick, towering trees and the dangerous, overgrown paths that connect the civilized clearings. The ruins of ancient, forgotten empires are scattered throughout the deep woods, drawing vagabonds and scavengers alike.

The untamed spaces between the clearings are incredibly dangerous, teeming with massive beasts and monstrous insects. The march is home to a wide, terrifying variety of perils, including:
- **[[Giant Snake]]s** and **[[Giant Spider]]s** that lurk in the canopy and ruins.
- Swarms of **[[Giant Hornets]]** and **[[Giant Honeybees]]**.
- **[[Giant Alligator]]s** and **[[Giant Carnivorous Plant]]s** in the wetter, boggy regions.
- Massive, territorial mammals that wander the deep woods, such as **[[Deer]]**, **[[Black Bear]]s**, **[[Grizzly Bear]]s**, **[[Stag]]s**, **[[Moose]]**, and roaming herds of **[[Wood Bison]]**.
