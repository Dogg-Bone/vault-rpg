Located to the west of [[La Capitale]], the Avian March is known to its inhabitants simply as [[The Woodland]].

This march is home to birds, foxes, rabbits, and mice, along with recent arrivals like [[Frogs]], [[Corvids]], and [[Lizardfolk]] and some of almost every sort of [[Denizens]].

The march is also home to a wide variety of perils, including [[Giant Snake]]s, [[Giant Spider]]s, [[Giant Hornets]], [[Giant Honeybees]], [[Giant Alligator]]s, [[Giant Carnivorous Plant]]s, [[Deer]], [[Black Bear]]s and [[Grizzly Bear]]s, [[Stag]]s, [[Moose]], and [[Wood Bison]].
