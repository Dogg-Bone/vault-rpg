# House Condor

**Species:** Condor
**Focus:** Despotic Rule, Infrastructure Repurposing, and Intimidation
**Naming Convention:** Slavic

## Overview
House Condor casts a dark, imposing shadow over the Eyrie Dynasties. They are the authoritarians and the enforcers of the old ways, believing that the Woodland denizens must be ruled with an iron talon to maintain order. They specialize in capturing clearings and brutally reshaping them to fit their needs. Rather than building from scratch, they have a propensity for seizing the infrastructure left behind by other factions—such as [[Marquise de Cat|Marquisate]] sawmills or Alliance hideouts—repurposing what is useful for their war machine and unceremoniously demolishing anything that does not serve their immediate despotic purposes.

## NPC Leader: Regent Vladimir of Condor
Vladimir is an ancient, massive condor whose presence alone can quiet a bustling clearing. He speaks softly, but his decrees are absolute and backed by terrifying enforcement.
- **Location:** The highest point of a violently repurposed [[Marquise de Cat|Marquisate]] keep in a central, heavily subjugated clearing.
- **Fear:** That the "softer" approaches of Houses like Mallard or the chaotic populism of House Kestrel will lead to another devastating civil war that shatters the Eyrie forever.
- **Secret:** Vladimir's ruthless demolition of old [[Marquise de Cat|Marquisate]] buildings is partially to cover up the fact that he has been stealing hidden caches of [[Marquise de Cat|Marquisate]] gold to personally fund his own loyalist guard.
- **Request for the Vagabonds:** He needs Vagabonds to quietly eliminate a charismatic, dissenting elder in a mouse village who is inciting a tax revolt, without it looking like an official Eyrie execution. Compensation: Confiscated [[Marquise de Cat|Marquisate]] trade goods and a writ of immunity from House Condor tax collectors.
- **Opinion/Info:** He views House Kestrel as "a rabble of noisy insects pretending to be an army," and frequently orders them on suicidal vanguard missions just to thin their numbers.

## Brief History
In the height of the Dynasties' power, House Condor served as the highest judges and inquisitors of the royal court. When the civil wars broke out, they attempted to seize complete control through brutal purges, earning the lasting enmity of the other Houses. Though they were eventually forced into an uneasy coalition with the rest of the Eyrie to fight the [[Marquise de Cat|Marquisate]], they still view themselves as the rightful, absolute rulers of the Woodland. They prefer to let other Houses do the initial fighting, sweeping in afterward to establish martial law and extract resources.

## Notable Facts, Connections, and Resources
- **The Iron Beak:** House Condor maintains a fearsome internal police force that roots out dissent not just among the woodland denizens, but within the lower ranks of the Eyrie itself.
- **Scavenger Engineering:** While they do not possess the artisanal brilliance of House Cardinal, House Condor engineers are unparalleled at rapidly retrofitting captured enemy siege engines and fortifications for their own use.
- **Psychological Warfare:** They frequently leave the demolished ruins of "useless" buildings as stark, public warnings to any denizens considering rebellion or collaboration with the enemy.
- **Rivalry:** They are despised by almost every other House for their cruelty, but especially by House Mallard, whose efforts to win the "hearts and minds" of the denizens are constantly undermined by Condor atrocities.
- **Resources:** They control massive stockpiles of scavenged materials, hoarded wealth extracted via heavy taxation, and a terrifying network of informants coerced into service.
