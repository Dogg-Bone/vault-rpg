## Location
In a small, cluttered shop filled with the smell of leather and polish, near the edge of the clearing.

## Fear
That mass-produced boots from the Marquise's factories will put them out of business.

## Secret
They have a pair of magical boots that grant incredible speed, but they are too afraid to use them.

## Request for the Vagabonds
"I need a specific type of leather—the hide of a Great Horned Boar. Bring me one, and I'll make you boots that let you walk on water."

## Opinion/Info
"The mayor is stepping out on his wife. I saw him sneaking into the tavern through the back door late last night."
