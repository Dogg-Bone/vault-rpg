## Location
Behind the heavy oak desk of the Better Burrow Bank, located in the wealthiest district of a Rabbit clearing.

## Fear
A run on the bank. They don't actually have enough funds to cover all the deposits if everyone asks for their money at once.

## Secret
They have been lending money to the Corvid Conspiracy to fund their illicit operations.

## Request for the Vagabonds
"There is a debtor who has... skipped town. An Eyrie outcast. Find them and convince them to repay their loan, with interest, and I will wipe out your own debts."

## Opinion/Info
"Those 'artisans' in the next clearing are nothing but charlatans. Their goods fall apart in a week, but they charge extortionate rates."
