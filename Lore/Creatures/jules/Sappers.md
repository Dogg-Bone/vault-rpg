## Location
Hidden in a series of tunnels beneath a clearing controlled by the Marquise, directing excavation efforts.

## Fear
A cave-in. They lost half their squad to one last winter and are hyper-paranoid about tunnel stability.

## Secret
They have inadvertently dug into an ancient, sealed tomb and haven't told their superiors yet.

## Request for the Vagabonds
"We need a distraction above ground. Blow up the Marquise's supply depot, and it will draw the guards away from our tunnel entrance."

## Opinion/Info
"The Riverfolk merchants? They sold us faulty blasting powder last month. Nearly blew my whiskers off. Don't trust 'em."
