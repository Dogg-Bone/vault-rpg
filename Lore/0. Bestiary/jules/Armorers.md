## Location
Working at a large, soot-stained forge near the center of a bustling clearing.

## Fear
That their secret technique for forging unbreakable steel will be stolen by a rival smith.

## Secret
They sometimes use scavenged Keepers in Iron artifacts to strengthen their own creations, a highly dangerous and illegal practice.

## Request for the Vagabonds
"I need a rare ore found only deep within the Bear's territory. Bring me enough to forge a masterwork breastplate, and I'll craft you a weapon of unparalleled quality."

## Opinion/Info
"The local militia is a joke. Their armor is rusted through. A stiff breeze could knock them over, let alone a determined raiding party."
