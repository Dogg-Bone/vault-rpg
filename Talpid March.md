# Talpid March

> *"They dig in the dark and hoard the wealth of the earth, ignoring the light of the Empereur. We tolerate them only because uprooting them would collapse the very mountains upon our own heads."*
> — A frustrated Senechal, reporting on Duchy tariffs

> *"The empire thinks it rules the world, but it only rules the surface. The real power is down below, in the dark, where the gold is mined and the Iron Keepers march."*
> — A mole merchant in the markets of La Capitale

The **Talpid March** encompasses the soaring mountains and treacherous ravines to the north and west of [[La Capitale]]. The peaks of the Talpid Mountains soar high above the continent, while the valleys and subterranean networks delve impossibly deep into the earth. It is a region characterized by its imposing verticality, suffocating depths, and immense, hidden wealth.

## Governance & The Underground Duchy
The political reality of the Talpid March is unique within [[Le Monde de Cat]]. This march is home to the great **[[Underground Duchy]]**, a massive, highly organized faction that represents the closest force capable of matching the raw power of the Cat Empire itself.

Though technically subject to the empire on paper, the Duchy operates in practical, stubborn independence of imperial law. The Duchy wields enormous economic clout both within and outside of Le Monde, controlling the flow of precious metals, gems, and deep-earth resources—enough leverage to put serious economic pressure on the entire Monde de Cat. Because the Duchy is generally isolationist and not inclined to outright conflict, the [[Empereur]] has pragmatically elected to allow them a form of sovereignty over the majority of the Talpid March, avoiding a costly subterranean war.

## Demographics & Inhabitants
The region is primarily inhabited by moles and bats who thrive in the deep tunnels, alongside some hardy species of birds that nest in the high peaks.

Historically, the march also became a refuge. When the heavily armored ascetics known as the **[[Keepers in Iron]]** were exiled from [[The Woodland]], they migrated to the Talpid Mountains, establishing formidable fortresses among the peaks and integrating their militant presence into the rugged landscape.

## Geography & Hazards
The surface of the Talpid March is as dangerous as its political landscape. To the south of the mountains lie **[[The Scars]]**, a treacherous, desolate plain violently gouged by deep ravines and sheer gorges.

Navigating the surface passes or the deep caverns is perilous, as the entire region is home to apex predators that hunt among the crags and caves. Known dangers of the mountains include:
- Massive **[[Giant Snake]]s** and **[[Giant Spider]]s** that haunt the deep caverns and ravines.
- Herds of sure-footed **[[Elk]]** that navigate the high passes.
- Terrifying **[[Grizzly Bear]]s** that claim the mountain caves as their own.
