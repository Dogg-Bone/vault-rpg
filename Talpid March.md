The mountains and ravines to the north and west of [[La Capitale]] soar above the continent. This march is home to the great [[Underground Duchy]], the closest force to matching the power of [[Le Monde de Cat]]. Though technically subject to the empire, the Duchy operates in practical independence of imperial law. The Duchy wields enormous economic clout within and outside of Le Monde, enough to put serious pressure on the entire Monde de Cat. As the Duchy is not inclined to conflict, the [[Empereur]] has elected to allow them a form of sovereignty over the majority of Talpid March. 

This region is home to moles, bats, and some birds. When the [[Keepers in Iron]] were exiled from [[The Woodland]], they came to the Talpid Mountains.

The dangers of the mountains are many. [[The Scars]] are treacherous plains gouged by ravines and gorges to the south of the mountains, and the entire region is home to [[Giant Snake]]s, [[Giant Spider]]s, [[Elk]], and [[Grizzly Bear]]s.
