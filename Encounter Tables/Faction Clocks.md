# Faction Endgame Clocks

Each major faction in the Woodland has a grand design—an endgame plot that corresponds to their ultimate victory condition. These are represented as 6-segment **Endgame Clocks**.

When a faction advances its agenda, the Woodland changes. Vagabonds should clearly see and feel the effects of the advancing clocks in the world around them.

## Advancing Faction Clocks

Faction Clocks advance through the following GM Move:

> **When time passes in the Woodland, or when the Vagabonds ignore a faction's machinations in favor of other pursuits,** advance a Faction Clock by 1 segment.

Each time a clock advances, the corresponding event takes place in the fiction, serving as a new adventure hook or complication for the Vagabonds.

## Turning Back the Clocks

Vagabonds have the power to disrupt these grand designs. If the Vagabonds strike a critical blow against a faction—such as destroying a major base, assassinating a key leader, sabotaging crucial infrastructure, or turning a clearing against them—you may **remove 1 or more segments** from that faction's Endgame Clock, depending on the severity of the setback.

## Marquise de Cat: Total Industrialization
*The Marquisate seeks to strip-mine the Woodland, converting its natural beauty into a relentless engine of industry and order.*

**The 6-Step Clock:**
1. **Surveyors Dispatched:** Marquisate engineers map the woodland, marking ancient groves and independent clearings for "development."
2. **The Great Deforestation:** Massive logging camps are established. A major trade route is heavily guarded and cleared of all cover.
3. **The Iron Smelters:** A massive foundry is built in a central clearing, choking the sky with black smoke and polluting a major waterway.
4. **Forced Conscription:** Denizens are forcefully drafted into labor camps or the Marquisate military to fuel the growing industrial machine.
5. **The Keep's Expansion:** The Marquise's central keep is heavily fortified with new, devastating siege weapons and iron-clad walls.
6. **Total Industrialization:** (Win Condition) The Woodland is fully industrialized. The Marquise declares absolute martial law, crushing any remaining resistance with overwhelming, mechanized force.

**How to Turn Back the Clock:**
*   Destroy a major sawmill, foundry, or supply train.
*   Assassinate a high-ranking Marquisate commander or chief engineer.
*   Incite a massive worker strike or riot in an industrialized clearing.

## Eyrie Dynasties: Reclaiming the Roosts
*The Eyrie believes the Woodland is theirs by divine right. They seek to reclaim their ancestral roosts and demand fealty from all denizens.*

**The 6-Step Clock:**
1. **The Decree is Written:** A new, charismatic Eyrie commander issues a sweeping decree, promising to retake a specific sector of the Woodland.
2. **Sky Patrols Intensify:** Eyrie warriors aggressively patrol the canopy, harassing merchants and grounding all rival aerial movement.
3. **Ancestral Roost Seized:** The Eyrie violently captures a major clearing, erecting a massive, fortified roost and displacing the local leadership.
4. **Tithe of Fealty:** The Eyrie demands crippling taxes (food and resources) from neighboring clearings under threat of martial retaliation.
5. **The Grand Migration:** A massive influx of Eyrie nobles and soldiers flock from the edges of the Woodland to the newly conquered territories.
6. **Total Dominion:** (Win Condition) The Eyrie reclaims all major ancestral roosts. Denizens are subjugated into a rigid caste system under absolute Eyrie rule.

**How to Turn Back the Clock:**
*   Assassinate a charismatic commander, forcing the Eyrie into political turmoil (a "Turmoil" event).
*   Destroy a major ancestral roost.
*   Sabotage their supply lines to starve a newly established garrison.

## Woodland Alliance: The Grand Revolt
*The Alliance seeks to overthrow the oppressors by uniting the common folk in a bloody, woodland-wide revolution.*

**The 6-Step Clock:**
1. **Sympathy Spreads:** Underground pamphlets and secret meetings become rampant in oppressed clearings.
2. **The First Spark:** A small but significant act of sabotage occurs—a supply depot burns, or a low-level official is assassinated.
3. **Open Rebellion:** A minor clearing openly violently revolts, expelling its rulers and declaring itself a sovereign Alliance stronghold.
4. **The Guerrilla War:** Alliance fighters begin ambushing major supply lines and military patrols, making travel highly dangerous.
5. **The Grand Coalition:** Multiple clearings simultaneously rise up, overwhelming local garrisons and forming a unified rebel army.
6. **The Grand Revolt:** (Win Condition) The oppressed rise up everywhere at once. The occupying factions are violently expelled or executed, and a radical, unpredictable new government takes over.

**How to Turn Back the Clock:**
*   Expose and capture a high-ranking Alliance leader or sympathetic operative.
*   Find and destroy an underground Alliance base.
*   Resolve the grievances of a clearing peacefully, removing the need for a violent revolt.

## Lord of Hundreds: The Scorched Earth
*The Lord of Hundreds seeks to burn the old world to the ground, ruling over a mob of fanatical followers who revel in chaos and destruction.*

**The 6-Step Clock:**
1. **The Horde Gathers:** Rumors spread of a massive mob of mice gathering on the fringes of the Woodland, whipped into a frenzy by a warlord.
2. **The First Pyre:** A small clearing or important historical site is razed to the ground, leaving nothing but ash and terrified refugees.
3. **Looters and Pillagers:** Warlord raiding parties become a constant threat, terrorizing merchants and hoarding stolen artifacts.
4. **The Warlord's Decrees:** The Lord of Hundreds issues erratic, chaotic demands to nearby clearings, promising destruction if ignored.
5. **The Sea of Fire:** A major forest fire is intentionally started, threatening to consume multiple clearings and critical infrastructure.
6. **The Scorched Earth:** (Win Condition) The Woodland is reduced to a chaotic, burning wasteland. The Lord of Hundreds rules absolutely over a starving, fanatical mob.

**How to Turn Back the Clock:**
*   Steal or destroy the Warlord's prized artifacts, severely damaging their morale and legitimacy.
*   Assassinate the Lord of Hundreds or their top lieutenants.
*   Defeat a massive Warlord mob in a pitched battle, scattering their forces.

## Riverfolk Company: Economic Monopoly
*The Riverfolk seek to control all trade, wealth, and movement within the Woodland, holding every faction hostage through debt and dependency.*

**The 6-Step Clock:**
1. **The Market Opens:** Riverfolk establish highly visible, lucrative trading posts in key clearings, offering rare goods on credit.
2. **Mercenary Contracts:** The Riverfolk begin leasing out well-armed mercenary companies to the highest bidder, escalating local conflicts.
3. **Monopolizing the Waterways:** The Riverfolk blockade a major river, demanding exorbitant tolls from anyone trying to pass or trade.
4. **The Debt Collectors:** Aggressive Riverfolk repo-squads begin seizing businesses, homes, and assets from denizens unable to pay their massive debts.
5. **The Economic Chokehold:** The Riverfolk buy out or destroy all competing merchants, creating a total monopoly on food and weapons.
6. **Economic Monopoly:** (Win Condition) The Riverfolk Company owns the Woodland. Faction leaders are reduced to puppet rulers, entirely dependent on Riverfolk loans and supplies to survive.

**How to Turn Back the Clock:**
*   Rob a major Riverfolk vault or intercept a massive shipment of wealth.
*   Establish and protect a rival, independent trade network.
*   Destroy a major Riverfolk trading post or blockade-enforcement fleet.

## Corvid Conspiracy: The Shadow Syndicate
*The Corvid Conspiracy seeks to rule from the shadows, manipulating the Woodland through extortion, blackmail, and subtle sabotage.*

**The 6-Step Clock:**
1. **Whispers in the Dark:** Strange symbols appear in clearings. Paranoia spreads as denizens suspect their neighbors are Corvid agents.
2. **The First Extortion:** A prominent clearing leader is publicly humiliated or forced to step down due to meticulously forged blackmail.
3. **The Syndicate Expands:** Illegal gambling dens and smuggling rings operate openly, heavily taxing the local economy and funding Corvid plots.
4. **Sabotage and Ruin:** Key infrastructure (bridges, supply depots) inexplicably fails at critical moments, crippling other factions.
5. **Puppet Rulers:** Multiple clearing leaders are revealed to be either Corvid operatives or firmly under their blackmail control.
6. **The Shadow Syndicate:** (Win Condition) The Corvids achieve total, unseen control. The woodland appears normal, but every decision, trade, and law is dictated by the Conspiracy for their own profit.

**How to Turn Back the Clock:**
*   Expose a major Corvid plot or unmask a puppet ruler.
*   Destroy a Corvid smuggling ring, gambling den, or hidden base.
*   Recover critical blackmail material before it can be used.
