Each faction has an endgame, grand scheme corresponding to a board game win condition or a major narrative climax. These use 6-segment **Faction Clocks** consisting of key events leading up to their ultimate win condition.

> [!tip] Advancing Faction Clocks
> Faction clocks advance via PbtA-style GM moves—typically when time passes, when Vagabonds ignore their machinations, or as a hard move on a 6-. Vagabonds must see the effects of the clocks advancing in the fiction, and they should have the opportunity to 'turn back' these clocks, throwing off the faction's timeline and averting disaster.

## Endgame Faction Clocks

### Marquise de Cat: Total Industrialization
*The Marquise seeks to turn the Woodland into a perfectly efficient machine, consuming all natural resources to fuel their sawmills and workshops.*
1. A key clearing is clear-cut entirely, leaving only stumps and a sprawling new sawmill.
2. Major pathways are paved and fortified, completely cutting off traditional denizen travel routes.
3. A massive draft is instituted, forcing denizens of multiple clearings into grueling factory labor.
4. The Keep is expanded into an impenetrable fortress city, serving as a hub for mass weapon production.
5. A powerful, terrifying war-machine (like a massive steam-powered logging engine) is deployed to crush any resistance.
6. **Win Condition:** The Gilded Cage. The Woodland is fully industrialized and pacified. The forests are gone, replaced by sprawling factories and endless patrols. The Marquise's rule is absolute and inescapable.

### Eyrie Dynasties: The Old Empire Restored
*The Eyrie seeks to reclaim their ancestral glory, demanding absolute fealty and re-establishing their rigid caste system across the canopy and the ground.*
1. A prominent clearing is annexed, and its denizens are forced to build a massive, towering roost.
2. An Eyrie commander (e.g., of House Strix or House Kestrel) begins heavily taxing a major trade route to fund the war effort.
3. A brutal decree is enacted outlawing any unsanctioned assembly or speech against the Dynasties, strictly enforced by House Condor despots.
4. The Eyrie establishes a massive blockade around a defiant clearing, starving them into submission.
5. The various Houses (Cardinal, Mallard, Kingfisher, etc.) unite their forces under a single charismatic leader for a massive, coordinated offensive.
6. **Win Condition:** The Sky's Shadow. The old caste system is violently reinstated over the entire Woodland. Dissenters are exiled or executed, and all ground-dwelling species are forced into servitude beneath the absolute rule of the roosts.

### Woodland Alliance: The Great Uprising
*The Alliance seeks to tear down all established powers, igniting a violent, uncontrollable revolution across the Woodland.*
1. A sudden, coordinated sabotage destroys a major military asset of a ruling faction.
2. Sympathy spreads like wildfire; an entire clearing openly refuses to pay taxes or obey faction laws.
3. The Alliance establishes a highly fortified hidden base, openly training denizen militias.
4. A beloved denizen leader is martyred, sparking widespread riots in multiple clearings.
5. The Alliance launches a massive, multi-clearing revolt, turning the forest pathways into deadly guerrilla warzones.
6. **Win Condition:** The People's Fire. All established factions are completely overthrown. The Woodland descends into chaotic, bloody anarchy as local militias exact vengeance on their former oppressors, leaving no central authority and no safety.

### Riverfolk Company: Complete Economic Monopoly
*The Riverfolk seek to own the Woodland—not through conquest, but through inescapable debt, controlling all trade and shipping.*
1. The Company buys out or undercuts all local merchants in a key region, establishing a complete monopoly on essential goods.
2. A clearing falls into deep debt to the Company, forcing its leaders to sign away their autonomy and resources.
3. The Riverfolk construct massive tolls and blockades on all major rivers, strangling independent travel.
4. The Company introduces a new, predatory currency that devalues local goods and forces everyone into their economic system.
5. Mercenary bands hired by the Company begin openly enforcing debts, seizing homes, and indenturing denizens.
6. **Win Condition:** The Ledger's Rule. Every clearing, faction leader, and denizen is hopelessly indebted to the Riverfolk. The Company effectively rules the Woodland through economic extortion, where everything—even freedom—has an exorbitant price.

### Lizard Cult: The Awakening of the Great Dragon
*The Cult seeks to bring the Woodland into a new dark age of blind faith, sacrificing all non-believers to their draconic god.*
1. A wave of fanatical proselytizing sweeps through a clearing, converting the majority of the population and displacing the ruling faction.
2. The Cult establishes a massive, forbidding Temple in the ruins of a prominent landmark, hoarding food and supplies.
3. Dissenters and "heretics" begin disappearing in the night, taken for re-education or sacrifice.
4. The Cult openly crusades, zealots swarming pathways and clearings, overwhelming defenders with sheer numbers and fanaticism.
5. A terrifying omen (a blood-red moon or a massive forest fire) is claimed by the Cult as a sign of their god's imminent return, inciting mass hysteria.
6. **Win Condition:** The Theocratic Dark Age. The Woodland is completely subsumed by the Cult's dogma. Non-believers are purged, all knowledge and history are burned, and the denizens are live in perpetual, terrifying subjugation to the Cult's mysterious leaders.

### Corvid Conspiracy: The Invisible Throne
*The Conspiracy (including The Rook Family and The Magpie Syndicate) seeks to rule from the shadows, controlling the Woodland through extortion, blackmail, and organized crime.*
1. A prominent faction leader is completely compromised by a scandal engineered by The Rook Family, becoming a puppet.
2. The Magpie Syndicate orchestrates a massive artificial shortage of essential supplies, causing widespread panic and dependency on their black market.
3. Key infrastructure (bridges, storehouses) is subtly sabotaged, making travel and survival reliant on Corvid "protection."
4. Paranoia grips the Woodland; denizens turn on each other as Corvid operatives spread disinformation and frame innocents.
5. A coordinated, simultaneous assassination of several key figures across multiple factions throws the Woodland leadership into chaos.
6. **Win Condition:** The Shadow Syndicate. The Corvids achieve total, unseen control. Every official is bought, every trade is taxed by the mob, and any opposition vanishes quietly in the night. They rule absolute, yet no one can point to the true leaders.

### Grand Duchy: Subterranean Annexation
*The Duchy seeks to undermine the Woodland literally and figuratively, bringing the surface world under their bureaucratic, underground rule.*
1. Massive sinkholes appear in a clearing, swallowing buildings and revealing extensive Duchy tunneling operations.
2. A Duchy Minister establishes a permanent, heavily fortified surface citadel, demanding tribute from the surrounding clearings.
3. The Duchy corners the market on a vital subterranean resource, forcing surface clearings to trade on unfavorable terms.
4. A labyrinthine network of tunnels connects major clearings, allowing Duchy forces to strike anywhere instantly and control all subsurface movement.
5. A powerful Lord of the Duchy launches a massive, multi-pronged invasion from below, bypassing surface defenses entirely.
6. **Win Condition:** The Sunless Empire. The surface Woodland is completely subjugated, reduced to a resource colony for the sprawling underground Duchy. The denizens are forced into strict, endless bureaucratic servitude to their mole overlords.

### Lord of Hundreds: The Consuming Fire
*The Hundreds, led by fanatical mice, seek to burn away the old world, leaving nothing but ashes and absolute loyalty to their warlord.*
1. A massive, chaotic horde of Hundreds forces swarms a clearing, stripping it bare of resources and conscripting its denizens.
2. A terrifying Warlord rises, uniting splintered warbands and launching brutal, indiscriminate raids across the paths.
3. The Hundreds begin destroying ancient landmarks and historical records, seeking to erase all culture but their own.
4. A major stronghold or clearing is completely razed to the ground, leaving only a smoldering ruin as a warning to others.
5. The Warlord demands total submission from the entire Woodland, threatening a scorched-earth campaign that will destroy the forest itself.
6. **Win Condition:** The World in Ashes. The Hundreds ravage the entire Woodland. Civilizations are destroyed, the forests burn, and the survivors are forced into a brutal, nomadic existence under the absolute, terrifying whim of the Warlord.

### Keepers in Iron: Relic Dominion
*The Keepers seek to control the Woodland through martial orthodoxy and the monopolization of ancient, powerful relics.*
1. A heavily armored Keeper expedition forces its way into a sacred or restricted site, claiming an ancient relic and establishing a heavily fortified waystation.
2. The Keepers declare a "holy quarantine" over a region, strictly controlling all movement and violently suppressing dissent.
3. A charismatic but dogmatic Keeper leader begins aggressively recruiting denizens, forcing them into brutal martial training and indoctrination.
4. The Keepers unearth a massive, ancient artifact of immense power (e.g., a lost ruin with defensive capabilities) and use it to intimidate neighboring clearings.
5. The Keepers launch a coordinated crusade to seize all known relics from other factions, turning the Woodland into a massive, heavily armored battleground.
6. **Win Condition:** The Iron Order. The Keepers establish a strict, militant theocracy over the Woodland. All ancient knowledge and power are hoarded by the Keepers, and the denizens are forced into absolute obedience and martial servitude to "protect" the relics.
