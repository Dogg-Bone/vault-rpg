> [!tip] Elmwood Reach
> **Control:** Marquise de Cat
> **Dominant Inhabitants:** Frogs, Mice, and Rabbits
> **Atmosphere & Effort State:** Suffocating & Press
> **Shape Flow:** Bureaucratic control is condensing downward

### Description & History
Located deep within the humid, swampy expanses of the [[Anuran March]], Elmwood Reach is a sprawling, vertical clearing built directly into the massive, ancient elm trees that rise from the dark waters. Historically the home of the inventor [[Vulcan]] and a loose network of amphibian and woodland communities, the clearing was characterized by its perilous rope bridge paths suspended over the water and the cultivation of giant, luminous mushrooms.

When the Marquise de Cat arrived, they recognized the strategic value of the waterways. They forcefully consolidated control, attempting to impose rigid order on the organic, vertical architecture. Unlike their usual tactics, the Marquise adapted to the environment, utilizing the sturdy elm wood rather than stone to fortify the lower trunks and build standardized tollhouses on the thickest branches. However, these new structures still clash violently with the older, woven wooden homes and giant fungal growths. Meanwhile, the Riverfolk Company has entrenched itself at the water level, constructing official, bustling docks that handle immense wealth, creating an economic powerhouse that the Marquise relies on but desperately wants to control fully.

### At First Sight
The clearing is a chaotic, multi-tiered spectacle. High above the water, precarious rope bridges sway between massive tree trunks, illuminated by the eerie, bioluminescent glow of giant mushrooms clinging to the bark. The air is thick, humid, and smells heavily of damp rot and woodsmoke.

Down at the water level, the chaos is purely mercantile. The Official Riverfolk Docks are a clamor of loading cranes, shouting merchants, and the clinking of coin. Throughout the vertical layers, the rigid presence of Marquisate patrols is unavoidable; heavily armed guards march across swaying bridges, their bright tabards stark against the dark, fungal greens of the swamp, attempting to enforce taxes and curfews in a place that fundamentally resists order.

### Problems / Conflicts
**The Core Conflict: The Price of Order**
The Marquisate is tightening its grip on Elmwood Reach, increasing taxes and attempting to regulate the chaotic vertical sprawl to fund their military campaigns. The Riverfolk Company, led by Finnian Rapids down at the water level, is resisting these measures, using their vast wealth to bribe Marquisate officials and subvert authority. Inspector Valerius is desperately trying to root out this corruption and assert total control over the lucrative docks.
*How it Develops:* If the vagabonds do not intervene, Inspector Valerius will eventually uncover Finnian's bribery ledger. The Marquisate will launch a massive, violent raid down into the docks, leading to a brutal clash that will likely end in the destruction of the Riverfolk operations and devastating collateral damage to the fragile rope bridges and innocent denizens above.

**Side Conflict: The Fungal Blight**
The Marquisate's heavy wooden construction and industrial waste from the docks are poisoning the water and the roots of the giant elms. A faction of local druidic frogs believes the only way to save the giant mushrooms and the trees is to sabotage the Marquisate structures and the Riverfolk cranes, causing them to collapse into the swamp.

### Important Locations
- **The Canopy Keep:** A heavy, imposing wooden fortress built precariously onto the thickest branches of the central elm. It houses the Marquisate garrison and the central tax office, looming threateningly over the lower levels.
- **The Elmwood Company Docks:** The chaotic, sprawling Riverfolk hub located at water level, where wealth and illicit goods flow freely under the noses of frustrated Marquise guards.
- **Vulcan's Workshop:** A secluded, cluttered array of treehouses suspended near the edge of the clearing, filled with strange mechanical parts, half-finished inventions, and the glow of hot forges.

### Notable NPCs
- **Inspector Valerius:** A rigid, meticulous Marquisate customs inspector determined to bring order to the vertical chaos, regardless of the cost to the denizens. (See [[Inspector Valerius]])
- **Finnian Rapids:** The slick, manipulative Riverfolk dockmaster who prioritizes profit above all else and operates with near impunity down at the water level. (See [[Finnian Rapids]])

### Special Resource / Rules
**Navigating the Canopy**
When you engage in a chase, combat, or rapid movement across the precarious rope bridges and giant mushrooms of the upper clearing, roll with Acrobatics.
- On a 10+, you move with grace and speed; you gain the upper hand or escape cleanly.
- On a 7-9, you make it, but the precarious environment takes a toll: you drop an item into the swamp below, mark 1-exhaustion, or leave yourself exposed to an enemy attack.
- On a 6-, the bridge gives way, a mushroom cap is dangerously slick, or you misjudge a jump; you fall toward the swampy waters below, suffering harm or landing directly in a Marquisate patrol.

### Rumors, Secrets & Hooks
- *Whisper:* The giant mushrooms aren't just for light and food; the older frogs say they are part of a massive, interconnected root mind that is growing angry at the Marquisate's heavy wooden structures.
- *Secret:* Finnian Rapids holds the deed to Vulcan's rickety airship and plans to strip it for parts to build a new, heavily armed Riverfolk patrol boat.

- **4-6:** A squad of Marquisate guards stops the vagabonds on a narrow bridge, demanding exorbitant "canopy transit taxes."
- **7-9:** The vagabonds witness a heated argument between a Riverfolk merchant and a local frog over the pollution of the swamp water.
- **10-11:** Finnian Rapids sends a messenger up to the vagabonds, offering a lucrative job involving the "liberation" of a specific item from Vulcan's workshop.
- **12:** A massive riot breaks out at the docks and spills upwards, with denizens clashing against Marquisate guards on the swaying rope bridges high above the water.
