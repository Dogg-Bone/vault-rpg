# Anuran March

> *"To the uninitiated, it is a rotting, sinking mire. To us, it is a crucible of life and industry. The mud here swallows the weak, but from it, we extract the empire's lifeblood."*
> — Marechal Thibault, Commander of the Southern Garrison

> *"You don't fight the swamp. You make deals with it. Keep your boots dry, keep your eyes on the waterline, and never, ever trust a ripple."*
> — 'Slip,' Otter River-Guide

The **Anuran March** is a vast, humid, and sprawling wetland located at the southern edge of [[Le Monde de Cat]]. Defined by its oppressive atmosphere, submerged ruins of older civilizations, and winding waterways, the march is an untamed frontier of mud and mist, where the empire's industrial ambitions constantly battle against creeping nature.

## Demographics & Inhabitants
The march is famously home to a staggering number of **[[Frogs]]**. The population has swelled massively in recent years, as waves of refugees and immigrants from the frozen, desolate [[North March]] migrated south, integrating with the native frog populations that have inhabited the swamps for generations.

Beyond the amphibians, the wetlands are a thriving ecosystem of avian and aquatic denizens. Flocks of mallards, gulls, and kingfishers nest in the canopy and along the riverbanks, while the waterways themselves are heavily navigated and populated by otters, beavers, and snapping turtles, many of whom work as guides, fishers, or scavengers among the submerged ruins.

## Geography & Hazards
The Anuran March is as dangerous as it is ecologically rich. The landscape is a moldering labyrinth of sunken stone architecture and thick mangrove-like vegetation, where the water levels dictate the flow of travel and commerce.

Vagabonds and imperial patrols alike must be constantly vigilant, as the region is infamous for its massive, deadly fauna. Known perils of the deep swamps include:
- **[[Giant Snake]]s** that drop from the dense canopy.
- Swarms of **[[Giant Leech]]es** and **[[Giant Tick]]s** that plague the stagnant pools.
- **[[Giant Alligator]]s** that ambush boats in the deeper waterways.
- Territorial, water-logged **[[Moose]]** that wade through the shallows.
- Massive, monstrous **Giant [[Bullfrog]]s**.
- Patches of highly aggressive **[[Giant Carnivorous Plant]]s** that thrive in the nutrient-rich muck.
