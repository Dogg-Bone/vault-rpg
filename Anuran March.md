A great wetland at the southern edge of [[Le Monde de Cat]], Anura is home to a staggering number of [[Frogs]], as immigrants from the frozen [[North March]] joined with the native Frog population. The wetland is also home to birds, particularly mallards, gulls, and kingfishers, as well as otters, beavers, and turtles. 

The dangers of the Anuran March include [[Giant Snake]]s, [[Giant Leech]]es, [[Giant Tick]]s, [[Giant Alligator]]s, [[Moose]], Giant [[Bullfrog]]s, and [[Giant Carnivorous Plant]]s.
