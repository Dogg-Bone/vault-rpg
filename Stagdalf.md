> I thought I was dead. I could not count the arrows that the orcs had pierced me with. That was my last memory as a man. My next memory begins when I awoke in a markedly different forest, this one forested with a larger variety of flora and fauna. When I arose, I beheld a [[Stag]], standing upright like a man, and towering over me like a cave troll. The creature fixed me with a frighteningly intelligent gaze and a strange wind echoed between my ears. I saw a vision.
> 
> It was the One Ring, in a dark torchlit stone chamber. It was hidden, but now discovered. Its invincible guardian had fallen. A figure stood over the ring, its features cloaked in shadow. The figure's voice chilled my spine. Speaking as if to the ring, it said over and over, "*Preciousss...*"
> 
> The stag pointed into the wood, and I knew he was directing me toward the Ring. I needed to protect it or it would prove the unmaking of this realm.
