# Obligations
During character creation, each [[Vagabonds|Vagabond]] chooses an obligation from the list below or creates their own:

| Obligation     | Notes                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Betrayal       | The vagabond is either the target of a betrayal or the betrayer, and the aftermath continues to dog them                                                              |
| Blackmail      | The vagabond has a secret, and someone knows it and has evidence                                                                                                      |
| Bounty         | A legal warrant, a criminal contract, a collections agency, or even an individual is willing to pay to capture or kill the vagabond                                   |
| Criminal       | Could be ties to the underworld or embroilment in a faction's legal system such as being defendant in an active trial or parole, probation, or even community service |
| Debt           | Whether to an individual or a faction, the vagabond owes someone a great deal                                                                                         |
| Duty           | The vagabond owes someone or something their services and/or loyalty                                                                                                  |
| Favor          | Similar to duty or debt, but this obligations comes without a contract                                                                                                |
| Oath           | Sworn to a group, cause, or religion in a way that may that may yield complications                                                                                   |
| Obsession      | Includes compulsions like gluttony, gambling, [[Kleptomaniac\|kleptomania]], or buying overpriced baubles as well as fixations on a person, place, thing, or idea     |
| Responsibility | Strong sense of accountability to something or someone that weighs on the vagabond's mind                                                                             |
> [!tip] Obligation Track
Each vagabond has an obligation track with 4 boxes. Vagabonds mark obligation at the start of each session, when they cross a [[Reputation Reference|Reputation]] threshold, or as part of a move. Vagabonds can clear obligation by somehow fulfilling their obligation in the game, the GM will tell you how many boxes to clear. 
>
> When the obligation track is full, tell the GM, clear the track, and roll. Take +1 for each +2 or -2 Reputation you have with a faction, along with +1 for each "yes" to the following questions:
> - Are you in a clearing?
> - Is someone looking for you?
> - Have you made a reputation move this session?
>
> On a hit, the GM activates a downside of your obligation. On a 10+, it comes without warning. On a miss, an ordinary denizen warns or reminds you of your obligation, mark obligation.

> [!tip] GM Move
> Bring an obligation closer: make the players mark obligation
