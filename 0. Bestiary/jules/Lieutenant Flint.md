## Details
**Location**: Leaning against the main gate of a recently conquered, heavily barricaded clearing. They rarely sit down, preferring to stand post like a living statue for hours on end, occasionally adjusting their massive hammer.
**Fear**: Being forced to step backward. Retreating, to Flint, is not a tactical decision; it is a profound moral failure and an admission of weakness.
**Secret**: Flint is deeply, secretly terrified of loud, sudden noises—specifically the sound of collapsing structures—due to a childhood mining accident. When the Hundreds begin tearing down a village, Flint often volunteers for 'perimeter duty' far away from the noise to hide their panic. They overcompensate by acting like an unfeeling brute.
**Request for the Vagabonds**: "That west wall. It's weak. The cats hit it with a ram, it'll fold. I need stone. Heavy stone. And thick oak beams. You find a wagon, you fill it from the old quarry, you drag it here. Do it, and I'll let you take your pick from the weapons we pulled off the cat wardens."
**Opinion/Info**: "Sparkle is soft. Too much time grabbing shiny things, not enough time holding the line. You can't patch a breached wall with silk, and you can't bludgeon an Eyrie knight with a gold necklace. The fox doesn't understand grit."
