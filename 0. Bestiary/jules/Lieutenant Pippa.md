## Details
**Location**: At the center of the loudest, most chaotic part of a raid, singing off-key work songs while helping to pull down a watchtower.
**Fear**: Being trapped in a cage, physical or metaphorical. The structure and rigid rules of the other factions are deeply traumatic to her.
**Secret**: Pippa's cheerful demeanor is a coping mechanism. She was imprisoned in a Marquise work camp for years, and the sheer trauma of it broke something in her. The joy she finds in destruction is the only way she knows how to process her grief.
**Request for the Vagabonds**: "The cats built a dam upriver. It's choking the forest! It's so ugly and straight and *wrong*. Sneak in and open the spillways. All of them. At once. Let the river be free! I'll give you a map of the patrol routes, you'll be able to move unseen for a month."
**Opinion/Info**: "Alden thinks we're building a new empire. Poor, silly Alden. We aren't building anything. We are the forest taking itself back. You don't build a new cage to replace the old one!"

## Background
**Why They Joined the Hundreds**: After escaping the Marquise work camp, Pippa wandered the woodland aimlessly until she encountered a Hundreds raid tearing down a toll bridge. The sight of the oppressive structure falling into the river was the most beautiful thing she had ever seen. She picked up a crowbar and joined in.
**How They Rose to Lieutenant**: Pippa's utter lack of fear and terrifying enthusiasm is infectious. During a grueling siege, when morale was at its lowest, Pippa led a seemingly suicidal charge against the gatehouse, laughing the entire way. The sheer audacity of it broke the defenders' resolve and inspired the horde to follow her. The Lord of Hundreds recognized her value as a mascot of their chaotic cause.

## GM Moves
- Unleash indiscriminate destruction that catches both friend and foe.
- Laugh maniacally in the face of mortal danger, unnerving onlookers.
