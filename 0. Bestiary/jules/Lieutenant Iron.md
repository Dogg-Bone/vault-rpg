## Details
**Location**: Standing knee-deep in the mud of a newly conquered clearing, aggressively drilling troops in the pouring rain, or personally leading the vanguard to smash a barricade.
**Fear**: Stagnation. They believe resting is a fatal weakness and that if the Hundreds ever stop their relentless march forward, the horde will collapse in on itself.
**Secret**: Iron is physically breaking down. Their refusal to rest has led to chronic, severe exhaustion. They are secretly hooked on highly stimulating (and toxic) herbs purchased illicitly from Riverfolk smugglers just to stay upright. If cut off, they would collapse in days.
**Request for the Vagabonds**: "You move fast. Faster than an army. Take these charges, go upstream, and blow the foundation of the old bridge. The Alliance uses it to flank us. Drop the bridge, keep our advance moving, and I'll let you pick through the Marquise armory we just took."
**Opinion/Info**: "Grudge? The creature wastes energy whining about unfairness. The world doesn't care if it's fair. We march, we crush, we conquer. That is all there is. Grudge's resentment is a distraction from the objective."
