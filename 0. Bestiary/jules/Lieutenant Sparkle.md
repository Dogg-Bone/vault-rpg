## Details
**Location**: Sitting comfortably on a stolen velvet chaise lounge in the center of the camp, surrounded by meticulously sorted piles of loot. They are usually haggling with their own underlings over the value of scavenged trinkets.
**Fear**: Destitution. The mere thought of returning to a life of poverty and sleeping on dirt floors makes their fur stand on end. They would gleefully sell out the entire horde to secure their hoard.
**Secret**: Sparkle is playing a very dangerous game. They are in massive, knee-breaking debt to a Don in the Magpie Syndicate (Corvid Conspiracy). They joined the Hundreds specifically to exploit the chaos of conquest and skim enough loot off the top to pay the mob off before Corvid enforcers find them.
**Request for the Vagabonds**: "Darlings! You look like professionals who appreciate the value of a closed mouth. I have a... meeting... with a very particular bird who fencing some goods for me.
