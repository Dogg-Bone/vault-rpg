## Details
**Location**: Personally leading scouting parties or drilling the vanguard at the edge of the war camp, rain or shine.
**Fear**: Complacency. He has seen armies destroyed because they stopped to celebrate a victory before the war was won.
**Secret**: Bram's original motivation was simply survival. His village was caught in the crossfire between the Eyrie and Marquise, leaving him a refugee. He joined the Hundreds because it was the largest armed group that would take him, and now he is trapped by the terrifying responsibility of keeping them alive.
**Request for the Vagabonds**: "There's an Alliance arms cache hidden in the old fox dens to the north. They're waiting to ambush us when we march. Clear it out. Take the weapons, I don't care. Just ensure the road is clear. I'll pay you in Marquise silver from our last raid."
**Opinion/Info**: "Fenn is a liability. Personal vendettas cloud judgment. If he can't put his petty grudges aside for the good of the advance, I will eventually have to leave him behind."

## Background
**Why They Joined the Hundreds**: As a refugee displaced by faction warfare, Bram found that the woodland was hostile to individuals. The Hundreds offered a harsh, simple proposition: march with us, fight, and eat. It wasn't ideological; it was survival math.
**How They Rose to Lieutenant**: Bram survived. While other recruits died charging fortified positions or deserted during hard winters, Bram simply kept marching, kept fighting, and kept surviving. He earned lieutenant status through sheer attrition—he was the only veteran left standing who knew how to keep a column moving under arrow fire.

## GM Moves
- Push exhausted troops far past their breaking point to achieve an objective.
- Ignore a direct plea for mercy in favor of a tactical advantage.
