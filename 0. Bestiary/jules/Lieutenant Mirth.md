## Details
**Location**: Skipping merrily through the smoldering ruins of a freshly looted village, tossing handfuls of stolen confetti into the air, or sitting atop a pile of shattered catapults kicking their legs.
**Fear**: Silence and boredom. The quiet aftermath of a battle physically pains them. If there isn't something being smashed, yelled at, or torn down, Mirth begins to suffer intense, visible anxiety.
**Secret**: Mirth's obsession with destruction is deeply ideological. They believe the woodland has become 'cluttered' by the cats and birds, and that reducing everything to rubble is the only way to return to a pure, natural state. Years ago, they intentionally collapsed their own family's burrow just because the tunnels 'felt too organized.'
**Request for the Vagabonds**: "Oh, friends! Want to play a game? The cats have a big, ugly sawmill by the river. It's so... *structured*. Go inside, sneak down to the basement, and just pull the main load-bearing pins out of the foundation logs. Don't break anything else, just the pins! Let gravity do the fun part! I'll give you these pretty blueprints I found if you let me watch it fall!"
**Opinion/Info**: "Roar thinks everyone is watching them. It's so silly! Nobody is watching *them*, they're watching the walls come down! Listen to the crunch of the timber! Look at the way the dust hangs in the air! Why would you look at a mouse in a cape when the whole world is falling apart?!"
