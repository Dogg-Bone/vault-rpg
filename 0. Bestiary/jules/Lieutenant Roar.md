## Details
**Location**: Standing atop a hastily constructed wooden stage or the highest ruined wall in the war camp, literally posing for their troops while a small, terrified retinue tries to keep their cape clean of mud.
**Fear**: Obscurity. The idea of being a footnote in history terrifies them more than death. They dread being considered 'ordinary' or being overshadowed by another lieutenant.
**Secret**: Roar cannot actually read the epic poems they force their troops to write about them. They just pretend to peruse the scrolls, nodding sagely at random intervals. Also, their ornate sabre was looted from a dead, impoverished Marquise noble—Roar has zero formal fencing training and just mimics moves they saw in a play once.
**Request for the Vagabonds**: "Ah, the so-called 'heroes'! Perfect timing. I require a magnificent crown to match my station. Sneak into the Eyrie roost two clearings over and liberate the 'Sun-Crest' they hold. Bring it to me, and you shall have coin and the honor of being mentioned in my next epic verse!"
**Opinion/Info**: "Iron? Ha! A dull, joyless, unimaginative creature. If they spent half as much time practicing their posture and diction as they do marching through the mud, they might actually inspire someone. A leader must have *panache*, not just a heavy mace!"
