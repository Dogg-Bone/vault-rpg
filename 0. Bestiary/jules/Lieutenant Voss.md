## Details
**Location**: Sitting comfortably in a well-furnished command tent, surrounded by ledgers and recently acquired loot, haggling with smugglers.
**Fear**: Destitution. The idea of returning to a life of poverty and scraping by in the dirt terrifies him.
**Secret**: Voss maintains lucrative ties with the Magpie Syndicate. He fences the Hundreds' most valuable loot through them at a steep discount, skimming a significant portion off the top for his personal retirement fund.
**Request for the Vagabonds**: "I have acquired a... sensitive item. A rather exquisite tapestry from a recently liberated Marquise manor. I need it transported to a contact in a Riverfolk trade post without the rest of the horde knowing. Discretion is key. I pay very well for discretion."
**Opinion/Info**: "Pippa is a fascinating psychological study, but her methods are incredibly wasteful. Why burn a perfectly good manor when you can strip it of its valuables first? It's simply bad economics."

## Background
**Why They Joined the Hundreds**: Voss was a moderately successful merchant whose business was ruined by exorbitant Eyrie taxes. He realized that in a woodland consumed by war, the only real power was raw force. He joined the Hundreds not to fight, but to manage their chaotic economy and get rich in the process.
**How They Rose to Lieutenant**: The Hundreds are excellent at taking things, but terrible at managing them. The horde was starving despite sitting on piles of looted silver because no one knew how to fence it. Voss stepped in, established supply lines with the Riverfolk, and turned stolen trinkets into food and weapons. He is a lieutenant because they literally cannot afford to lose him.
