## Details
**Location**: Personally overseeing the reinforcement of barricades or standing guard at the main gate of a conquered clearing.
**Fear**: Losing the territory they have fought so hard to secure. Retreat means exposing the vulnerable camp followers to the enemy.
**Secret**: Tilde is deeply conflicted about the Hundreds' destructive tendencies. She wants to build, not just tear down, and is quietly hoarding building materials and tools, hoping for the day the horde finally settles down.
**Request for the Vagabonds**: "We took this old mill, but the foundation is rotten. If the river floods, we lose our supply point. I need proper, treated lumber from the Eyrie roost upriver. Sneak it down to me. I'll pay you well, and you'll always have a safe bed here."
**Opinion/Info**: "Voss is a parasite. War isn't an excuse to line your own pockets while the soldiers bleed. If I catch him stealing from the common stores again, I'll break his legs."

## Background
**Why They Joined the Hundreds**: Tilde's village was razed during an Eyrie 'pacification' campaign. As a master mason, she watched her life's work reduced to rubble. She joined the Hundreds seeking a group strong enough that no one would ever be able to tear down her home again.
**How They Rose to Lieutenant**: During a disastrous counter-attack by the Marquise, the Hundreds were routed. Tilde rallied a small group of stragglers, fortified a narrow pass with overturned wagons, and held off a company of cat soldiers for three days, allowing the main horde to escape and regroup. She earned her rank through unshakeable reliability.
