## Details
**Location**: Lurking in the shadowed edges of the war camp, habitually sharpening their rusted dagger. They rarely sleep, preferring to wander the perimeter looking for underlings slacking off so they have an excuse to dole out vicious punishments.
**Fear**: Being laughed at, pitied, or dismissed as 'insignificant.' Grudge desperately craves fear and respect, believing violence is the only way to earn either from the woodland's elites.
**Secret**: Years ago, Grudge traveled to an Eyrie-controlled clearing hoping to enlist as a lowly scribe or servant to escape poverty. They were laughed out of the courtyard for their 'peasant stench.' This singular humiliation is the fuel for their absolute, murderous hatred of the birds.
**Request for the Vagabonds**: "See that fat Riverfolk merchant down at the docks? They sold me a rotten bag of meal three winters ago and smiled while they did it. Steal their master ledger. Don't bring it to me—throw it in the mud and stomp on it. Do that, and I'll slip you a vial of my best rust-toxin. Good for taking down big targets."
**Opinion/Info**: "Flint? The big idiot is a walking target. Standing still and taking a beating isn't a strategy, it's a death wish. Let the cats and birds break their teeth on Flint's thick skull; the rest of us will slip around the back and cut their hamstrings while they're distracted."
