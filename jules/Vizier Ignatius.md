# Vizier Ignatius Condor

**Species:** Condor
**Faction:** Eyrie Dynasties
**Role:** Vizier of Justice and Internal Affairs

## Concept
Ignatius is an intimidating, gaunt figure who rarely speaks above a whisper. He serves as the primary inquisitor and internal enforcer for the Eyrie's ruling council. He firmly believes in the "divine right" of the Eyrie to rule the woodland and sees dissent as a disease that must be violently purged.

## Details
- **Personality Word:** Unrelenting
- **Prime Drive:** To root out weakness, heresy, and treason within the Eyrie's own ranks, ensuring absolute ideological purity.
- **Will Never Do:** He will never accept a bribe or show mercy to a confessed traitor, regardless of their rank or past service.
- **Location:** In the dark, heavily guarded lower levels of the central Eyrie keep, surrounded by instruments of "persuasion."
- **Fear:** That the constant shifting of power between the different Houses will eventually fracture the Eyrie beyond repair, leaving them vulnerable to the Marquisate.
- **Secret:** Ignatius has been slowly poisoning a rival vizier from House Mallard to ensure that his own draconian policies are adopted by the ruling council without opposition.
- **Request for the Vagabonds:** He needs the Vagabonds to discreetly break into the quarters of a high-ranking Strix officer to plant forged documents that implicate the officer in a Corvid conspiracy plot. Compensation: The return of confiscated, highly valuable Vagabond gear and a "get out of jail free" writ from the inquisition.
- **Opinion/Info:** He believes Fiona Kestrel is the single greatest threat to the Dynasties' survival and is actively searching for an excuse to execute her for treason.
