> [!tip] The Sunken Silos
> **Type:** Subterranean Ruin (Beneath the Sun-Kissed Terraces)
> **Atmosphere & Effort State:** Suffocating & Wring
> **Shape Flow:** Creeping nature is slowly spreading and choking the architecture

### Description & Stratified History
Located deep beneath the Sun-Kissed Terraces of [[Bone Gulch]], The Sunken Silos are the remnants of a pre-Dynastic subterranean agricultural and water-processing facility. Built into the deep sandstone beneath the ancient leviathan's skeleton, this ruin was once a marvel of early industrial technology, featuring advanced pneumatic tubes and massive clockwork water purifiers designed to sustain life in the harsh [[Saurian March]]. Over the centuries, the facility suffered catastrophic collapse, burying the lower levels. Recently, a colony of massive Giant Tarantulas has burrowed into the silos, utilizing the ancient, rusted clockwork as structural supports for their thick, suffocating webs.

### At First Sight
The air here is bone-dry and smells of stale dust and bitter venom. The rhythmic, echoing dripping of condensation from the gardens high above provides the only sound. Thick, gray webbing coats the ancient, rusted brass pipes and gears that line the sandstone walls. The layout is precarious, with ancient metal walkways crumbling over deep, dark vertical shafts that disappear into the abyss below.

### 5-Room Dungeon Progression

#### Room 1: The Rusted Airlock (Entrance)
The entrance from the Sun-Kissed Terraces above has collapsed, revealing a heavy, rusted brass airlock door half-buried in the sandstone.
- *Notable Features:* A jammed pneumatic wheel mechanism, thick layers of undisturbed dust, and a desiccated, web-wrapped corpse of a previous explorer.
- *Challenge:* The vagabonds must pry open or repair the heavy gears of the airlock without causing a cave-in or making enough noise to wake the colony below.

#### Room 2: The Filtration Maze (Puzzle)
A massive, multi-level chamber filled with towering, rusted water filtration tanks and broken metal catwalks. Thick webbing stretches between the tanks like bridges.
- *Notable Features:* Pools of stagnant, toxic water, ancient control valves, and tripwire-like strands of spider silk.
- *Challenge:* Navigating the room requires avoiding the trip-webs. To progress, the vagabonds must manipulate the old control valves to drain the stagnant water blocking the lower exit, all while keeping their balance on the slippery, webbed catwalks.

#### Room 3: The Brood Chamber (Setback)
A former subterranean greenhouse, now completely choked with web. This room serves as the nesting ground for the Giant Tarantulas.
- *Notable Features:* Glowing, bioluminescent fungi clinging to the ancient stone, dozens of basketball-sized spider egg sacs, and the skittering sounds of juvenile tarantulas.
- *Challenge:* A sudden cave-in blocks the way back, and the vibrations stir the hatchlings. The vagabonds must quickly find an alternate path through a ventilation shaft or risk being swarmed by hundreds of tiny, biting spiders.

#### Room 4: The Core Purifier (Climax)
The heart of the facility, featuring a massive, dormant clockwork turbine suspended over a deep chasm. This is the lair of the massive broodmother Giant Tarantula.
- *Notable Features:* The towering, multi-eyed broodmother, the whirring of the damaged turbine catching a draft, and the crushed remains of a Marquisate patrol.
- *Challenge:* The broodmother attacks. She uses the heavy, rusted gears as cover and shoots webbing to immobilize her prey. The vagabonds must utilize the environment (perhaps dropping a loose clockwork gear on her) or fight her head-on to survive.

#### Room 5: The Architect's Office (Resolution & Loot)
A sealed, pristine office overlooking the core purifier that the spiders could not penetrate.
- *Notable Features:* A heavy oak desk, a wall of intact blueprints, and a sturdy, locked iron safe.
- *Loot & Resources:*
  - *Pre-Dynastic Blueprints:* Highly detailed schematics of the water system, worth 4-Value to the Marquisate or the Duchy.
  - *The Pneumatic Gauntlet (Relic):* An ancient, mechanical glove powered by compressed air. (Wear track: 3. When you punch a structural object or heavy obstacle, mark wear to shatter it completely).

### Current Occupants & Hazards
The primary occupants are the Giant Tarantulas, which are highly territorial and sensitive to vibrations. The structural integrity of the ruin itself is a severe hazard; walkways are rusted, and the sandstone is prone to collapsing if heavy explosives or loud noises are used.

### Secrets & Clues
- The blueprints in the final room reveal that the Sunken Silos actually connect directly to an aquifer that could supply enough water to turn Bone Gulch into an oasis, bypassing the Marquisate's control of the surface cisterns.
- Scratched into the wall of the airlock is a desperate warning: *"The Moles sent us down here to find the Gauntlet. Don't trust Mozz."*

### Twist / Plot Hook
**The Extortionist:** Upon defeating the broodmother and securing the loot, a squad of heavily armed Underground Duchy enforcers—sent by [[Mozz]]—blow open a side wall. They demand the vagabonds hand over the relic and the blueprints as a "toll" for trespassing in Duchy territory.
