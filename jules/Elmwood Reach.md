### Description & History
Elmwood Reach was originally a loose network of small, independent farming and fishing communities nestled among the roots of massive, ancient elm trees. When the Marquisate arrived, they saw the strategic value of its river access and forcefully consolidated the communities into a single, highly regulated clearing. The Marquise clear-cut large swathes of the surrounding forest to build rigid, standardized stone structures that clash sharply with the older, organic wooden homes woven into the elm roots.

The economy is now entirely focused on resource extraction and taxation to fuel the Marquisate war machine. However, the Riverfolk Company has entrenched itself in the docks, creating a powerful economic counter-weight that the Marquise desperately relies upon but deeply distrusts. The result is a clearing suffocating under heavy regulation and taxation, where the only true power lies with the heavily armed guards or the incredibly wealthy merchants.

### At First Sight
The clearing is a stark study in contrasts. The central avenues are paved with cold, grey stone, lined with uniform Marquisate barracks and administration buildings. Banners of the Marquise hang from every street corner, and heavily armed patrols march in synchronized rhythm.

However, towards the edges of the clearing and especially near the river, the ordered stone gives way to chaotic, sprawling wooden structures built into the enormous elm roots. The air smells of woodsmoke, cheap ale, and the ever-present musk of the river. The noise of the bustling docks can be heard throughout the clearing, a constant hum of commerce that the Marquisate cannot completely silence.

### Problems / Conflicts
**The Core Conflict: The Price of Order**
The Marquisate is tightening its grip on Elmwood Reach, increasing taxes and imposing strict curfews to fund their military campaigns elsewhere. The Riverfolk Company, led by Finnian Rapids, is resisting these measures, using their wealth to bribe officials and subvert Marquisate authority. Inspector Valerius is attempting to root out this corruption and assert total control.
*How it Develops:* If the vagabonds do not intervene, Inspector Valerius will eventually uncover Finnian's bribery ledger. The Marquisate will attempt a hostile takeover of the docks, leading to a violent clash that will likely end in the destruction of the Riverfolk operations and severe collateral damage to the innocent denizens caught in the crossfire.

**Side Conflict: The Smuggler's Route**
A small, desperate cell of the Woodland Alliance is trying to secure a steady supply of weapons from the Riverfolk Company to arm a rebellion. They are relying on the chaos at the docks to cover their movements, but the increased Marquisate patrols are making this nearly impossible.

### Important Locations
- **The Marquisate Keep:** A grim, imposing stone fortress dominating the center of the clearing, surrounded by a cleared fire zone. It houses the garrison and the central tax office.
- **The Elmwood Company Docks:** The chaotic, sprawling Riverfolk hub where wealth and illicit goods flow freely under the noses of frustrated Marquise guards.
- **The Root Tunnels:** A network of old, natural tunnels beneath the elm trees that the Woodland Alliance uses to move around the clearing unseen.

### Notable NPCs
- **Inspector Valerius:** A rigid, meticulous Marquisate customs inspector determined to bring order to the chaos, regardless of the cost to the denizens. (See `Inspector Valerius`)
- **Finnian Rapids:** The slick, manipulative Riverfolk dockmaster who prioritizes profit above all else and operates with near impunity thanks to his wealth. (See `Finnian Rapids`)

### Special Resource / Rules
**The Bureaucratic Maze**
When you attempt to navigate the Marquisate bureaucracy to secure official permits or avoid a patrol, roll with Finesse.
- On a 10+, you navigate the red tape successfully; you get what you need without arousing suspicion.
- On a 7-9, you get what you need, but it costs you: pay a bribe (1-Value), mark 1-exhaustion from the sheer frustration, or draw the attention of Inspector Valerius.
- On a 6-, you are caught in a bureaucratic trap; you are detained, fined, or your goods are confiscated.

### Rumors, Secrets & Hooks
- *Whisper:* The Marquisate guards are spread dangerously thin; a coordinated attack on the Keep right now might actually succeed.
- *Secret:* Finnian Rapids doesn't just tolerate the Woodland Alliance; he actively funds them to keep the Marquisate distracted and off balance.

### First Session Encounters (2d6)
- **2-3:** A desperate Woodland Alliance sympathizer begs the vagabonds to help them smuggle a crate of weapons past a Marquisate checkpoint.
- **4-6:** A squad of Marquisate guards stops the vagabonds, demanding exorbitant "traveler taxes" and threatening to confiscate their gear.
- **7-9:** The vagabonds witness a violent altercation between Riverfolk muscle and a denizen who couldn't pay their debts.
- **10-11:** Finnian Rapids approaches the vagabonds, offering a lucrative but highly illegal job involving the sabotage of a Marquisate supply wagon.
- **12:** A massive riot breaks out at the docks as denizens finally snap under the pressure of new Marquisate taxes, catching the vagabonds in the middle of a brutal crackdown.
