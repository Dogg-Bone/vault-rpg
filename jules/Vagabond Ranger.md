## Details

**Location:** Perched in the high branches overlooking a well-traveled forest path, or sitting quietly by a hidden, smokeless campfire.
**Fear:** That they are the last of their pack, and that their efforts to save the forest are ultimately futile.
**Secret:** They know the location of a forgotten, heavily armed Marquise de Cat supply cache, but refuse to touch it out of disgust for their machinery.
**Request for the Vagabonds:** "A logging crew is tearing down the elder trees in the western grove. Sabotage their equipment quietly so they think the forest is cursed. I will give you a pristine longbow in return."
**Opinion/Info:** "The Scoundrel is a menace, but predictable. You want to watch out for the Adventurer. They delve into ruins best left buried, stirring up ancient troubles."
