# Red Ward Rising: A Fruit Valley Adventure

**Premise:** The Red Ward—a rotting slum at the bend of the [[Silver River]] where the city's refuse gathers—has always been a volatile place. Recently, however, the Lord of Hundreds mobs have grown increasingly violent, organized, and worryingly well-armed. What started as disorganized brawling now threatens to spill over into key districts like the Bazaar and the Riverside. The local Denizens are caught in the crossfire, and an investigation is required to uncover who is arming the mobs, how they are coordinating, and how to stop them before the entire valley burns.

**Tone:** Investigation, Gritty Urban Tension, Social Maneuvering

## Key NPCs

### Garrick "The Spark"
*Lord of Hundreds Demagogue*
Garrick is a charismatic, fiery mouse whose speeches are currently inciting the Red Ward. He’s the public face of the recent uprising.
**Location:** The Red Ward (usually standing atop a makeshift soapbox near the largest refuse piles).
**Fear:** Losing the momentum he’s built and looking weak in front of his Warlord.
**Secret:** He isn't the one sourcing the weapons; he's being manipulated by an outside supplier he only knows as "The Benefactor."
**Request for the Vagabonds:** "Find the rat who betrayed our last meeting spot to the guards." (Reward: 3 Value in plundered Riverside goods and free passage through Hundreds-controlled streets).
**Opinion/Info:** "Lila thinks she's protecting the denizens, but her 'peace' is just a slow death. Only fire cleanses."

## Encounters

### 1. The Extortion (Investigation Hook / Social)
**The Scene:** The Vagabonds witness a group of three Lord of Hundreds thugs shaking down a local baker at the edge of the Bazaar, demanding "protection" coin and claiming the Red Ward will soon consume the district.
**The Catch:** The thugs aren't just armed with crude clubs; they are carrying brand-new, high-quality steel shortswords (2-Harm, Value 3) that bear no maker's mark.
**Complications:**
- If the Vagabonds intervene violently, the thugs fight fiercely but are clearly untrained with such fine weapons.
- If the Vagabonds talk them down (e.g., using *Trick* or *Persuade*), the thugs might slip up and mention "The Spark" in the Red Ward and a "new shipment down by the docks."
- **Loot:** Unmarked Steel Shortsword (2-Harm, Wear 2, Value 3, +valuable).

### 2. The Informant's Alley (Investigation / Chase)
**The Scene:** Following leads to the Bazaar, the Vagabonds locate **Snitch**, the opossum informant. He's cornered in a dead-end alley by two Corvid Syndicate operatives (disguised as common mice).
**The Catch:** The operatives are trying to silence him before he can blab about the smuggling routes.
**Complications:**
- If the Vagabonds engage the operatives, Snitch uses the distraction to bolt. This triggers a chase through the crowded stalls of the Bazaar.
- If the Vagabonds lose him, they'll have to rely on rumors or *Read a Tense Situation* to figure out he fled toward the Riverside docks.
- If they save him and corner him, he demands his Request (a forged writ) before giving up the cache location. He'll begrudgingly point them toward **Skipper Marrow** and **Constable Vane** for free.

> [!tip] Chase through the Bazaar
> When a Vagabond **chases Snitch through the crowded Bazaar**, they must roll with Finesse.
> **On a 10+**, they navigate the crowds perfectly, cornering him quickly.
> **On a 7-9**, they catch him, but choose one complication:
> - They cause a scene, drawing the attention of the city guard.
> - They lose or damage something in the rush (mark 1 Wear on an item).
> - They suffer 1 Exhaustion from the exertion.
> **On a 6-**, Snitch escapes into the Warrens, and the Vagabonds must spend a day tracking him down, advancing the Hundreds' timeline.

### 3. The Compromised Watch-post (Social / Investigation)
**The Scene:** To find out how weapons are getting into the Red Ward, the Vagabonds must deal with **Constable Vane** at his watch-post on the Riverside bridge.
**The Catch:** The bridge is heavily fortified. Vane is sweating; he knows the Eyrie inspectors are prowling Riverside today.
**Complications:**
- Vane refuses to let anyone pass without a "toll" (bribe).
- If the Vagabonds snoop around his post, they can find a hidden compartment containing Skipper Marrow's ledger and Corvid-minted River Scrip.
- Confronting Vane with his gambling debts (if they learned of them) or the ledger allows them to bypass the post or extract information. If pushed too hard, Vane panics and calls for backup, trying to arrest the Vagabonds to cover his tracks.

### 4. The Mob's Rally (Stealth / Information Gathering)
**The Scene:** In the heart of the Red Ward, **Garrick "The Spark"** is holding a massive rally atop a pile of refuse, handing out newly uncrated weapons to eager recruits.
**The Catch:** The Vagabonds need to find out where the crates are coming from without alerting the mob of 50+ angry mice.
**Complications:**
- **Lila Bright-tail** is present at the edge of the crowd, looking distressed. If the Vagabonds approach her, she begs them not to start a fight, as it will lead to a slaughter.
- The Vagabonds must use *Sneak* or disguises to get close to the stage.
- By examining the discarded crates, they can find River Company shipping manifests that lead directly to an abandoned warehouse near the docks—the smuggler's cache.

### 5. The Smuggler's Cache (Dungeon / Climax)
**The Scene:** An abandoned, rotting River Company warehouse at the edge of the Red Ward. The floorboards are slick with river water, and the air smells of mildew and black powder.
**The Catch:** This is where the Magpie Syndicate is stockpiling weapons for the Hundreds to destabilize the valley.
**Hazards & Layout:**
- **Entry:** The main doors are barred. A rotting skylight offers stealthy access.
- **The Floor:** Rigged with simple tripwires that drop heavy crates (2-Harm) on intruders.
- **The Guards:** Three Magpie Syndicate enforcers (Rooks) are managing the handover.
- **The Climax:** The Vagabonds can ambush the deal, destroy the cache (using oil/torches), or steal the evidence to expose the Syndicate. If a fight breaks out, the enforcers will attempt to ignite a small powder keg to cover their escape, giving the Vagabonds mere moments to disarm it or flee.

## 3 Meaningful Choices

Throughout the investigation, the Vagabonds will be faced with critical decisions that alter the state of Fruit Valley and their standing with its factions.

### 1. The Fate of Constable Vane
When the Vagabonds discover Constable Vane’s corruption and his gambling debt to the Magpie Syndicate, they must decide how to handle him:
- **Expose Him:** Reporting him to the Eyrie inspectors gets Vane arrested. The Eyrie locks down the bridge, heavily restricting movement into the Red Ward (making it much harder for the Vagabonds to get in/out) but cutting off the smugglers' route. (Increases Eyrie reputation, decreases Denizen/Hundreds reputation).
- **Blackmail Him:** Using the ledger to force Vane's cooperation gives the Vagabonds free reign to cross the bridge and access to guard patrols, but leaves the corrupt guard in power and the Magpie Syndicate with leverage over the Riverside border.
- **Help Him:** Fulfilling his request by breaking up the illicit gambling den in Tanglewood clears his debt. Vane becomes a loyal, permanent ally to the Vagabonds, but the Magpie Syndicate will mark the Vagabonds as enemies.

### 2. Handling the Smuggler's Cache
At the climax of the adventure, the Vagabonds discover the Magpie Syndicate’s weapons cache intended for the Lord of Hundreds. They must decide what to do with the armaments:
- **Destroy the Cache:** Burning or blowing up the warehouse removes the immediate threat. The Hundreds' uprising loses momentum without weapons, saving the Red Ward from a massacre. However, the Vagabonds gain no tangible reward and make enemies of both the Hundreds and the Syndicate.
- **Steal the Cache:** The Vagabonds can appropriate the high-quality weapons for themselves or sell them to another faction (like the Woodland Alliance or the River Company). This yields massive profits (Value 10+), but the Hundreds will know who stole their prize and will relentlessly hunt the Vagabonds.
- **Deliver the Cache to Lila Bright-tail:** Giving the weapons to the non-combatant Denizens allows Lila to form a genuine neighborhood watch. The Red Ward becomes a fortified, independent enclave. (Massive boost to Denizen reputation, but the city's ruling factions will view the Red Ward as an open rebellion).

### 3. Dealing with Garrick "The Spark"
Garrick is the public face of the uprising. Once the truth of the Syndicate’s manipulation is revealed, the Vagabonds have a choice on how to deal with him during the Mob's Rally:
- **Assassinate/Defeat Him:** Taking Garrick out scatters the immediate mob, causing the Hundreds to lose their foothold in the Red Ward entirely. However, Garrick becomes a martyr, and the Hundreds will send a much more brutal Warlord to replace him in the future.
- **Reveal the Truth:** Providing Garrick with irrefutable proof (like Skipper Marrow's ledger) that he is being used by the Magpie Syndicate shatters his pride. He will turn his mob's fury against the Syndicate instead of the city, plunging the Red Ward into an underground mob war.
- **Recruit Him:** By helping Garrick find "the rat" who betrayed him and keeping the truth hidden, the Vagabonds can earn his trust. They gain a powerful, dangerous ally who commands a mob, but they become complicit in the upcoming violence against the Riverside and Bazaar districts.
