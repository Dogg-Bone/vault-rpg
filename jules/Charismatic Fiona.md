# Fiona Kestrel, The Charismatic

**Species:** Kestrel
**Faction:** Eyrie Dynasties (House Kestrel)
**Role:** The Charismatic

## Concept
Fiona is a firebrand populist and an inspiring orator who leads from the front lines. She eschews the rigid, aristocratic traditions of the other Eyrie leaders, instead rallying the common soldiers and woodland denizens with promises of a united, equitable future. She thrives on momentum and mass appeal, driving her forces into a frenzy of zealotry.

## Details
- **Location:** In the center of a bustling encampment, standing on a makeshift stage and delivering a fiery speech to a crowd of adoring Kestrel soldiers and local sympathizers.
- **Fear:** That she will lose the momentum of her movement, and the traditionalist, aristocratic leaders of the Eyrie will execute her for insubordination.
- **Secret:** Fiona has secretly met with Woodland Alliance leaders to discuss a temporary truce, hoping to use them to weaken the Marquisate before turning on the Alliance—an act of treason in the eyes of the other Eyrie leaders.
- **Request for the Vagabonds:** She needs Vagabonds to distribute propaganda leaflets and incite a riot in a Marquisate-controlled clearing to draw their forces away from her impending assault. Compensation: A small fortune in collected "donations" and the absolute loyalty of any Kestrel soldier the Vagabonds encounter.
- **Opinion/Info:** She thinks the Despot (Boris Condor) is a tyrant whose brutal methods are no better than the Marquisate, and she plans to overthrow him once the cats are dealt with.
