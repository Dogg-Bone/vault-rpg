> [!tip] Murkwater Ruins
> **Type:** Ruin (Sunken Ziggurat)
> **Atmosphere & Effort State:** Oppressive & Sink
> **Shape Flow:** Ancient, lingering energy is slowly sinking into the mire

### Description & Stratified History
Located just past Murkwater's eastern border, the Murkwater Ruins are a testament to an ancient, forgotten civilization whose grasp on early industrial and pneumatic technology was unparalleled. The ruins consist of massive, moss-covered stone ziggurats that are slowly being swallowed by the deepest, most treacherous parts of the swamp.

This location is a prime example of stratified history. The original stone structures, built for an unknown grand purpose, have become the foundation for a massive, intrusive excavation by the Keepers in Iron. The Keepers have erected heavy iron scaffolding, complex pulley systems, and steam-powered pumps directly onto the crumbling stone, attempting to drain the flooded lower chambers. The contrast between the silent, sinking ancient stone and the loud, rhythmic clanking of the Keepers' heavy machinery highlights the ongoing violation of this forgotten place.

### At First Sight
Approaching the ruins, the oppressive weight of the ancient stone seems to sink into your very soul. The air here is unnaturally still, the usual swamp noises silenced, replaced only by the rhythmic clinking of excavation tools and the hiss of steam from the Keepers' water pumps. The visual impact is striking: colossal, algae-covered stone steps descend directly into the dark, stagnant water, while aggressive, heavy iron scaffolding clings to the stone like a metallic parasite.

The Keepers in Iron, heavily armored and singularly focused, move about the scaffolding with grim determination. They rig thick chains and hoist massive, dripping blocks of stone out of the muck, delving deeper into the pitch-black, flooded chambers below. The atmosphere is one of a slow, inevitable descent into darkness and forgotten secrets.

### Distinct Areas
- **The Scaffolding (Upper Level):** A precarious network of iron catwalks and wooden planks erected around the highest point of the exposed ziggurat.
  - *Notable Features:* A large, sputtering steam pump attempting to clear water from a shaft below; heavily armored Keeper sentries patrolling the walkways; a makeshift command post with topographical maps of the ruins.
- **The Flooded Undercroft (Mid Level):** A partially submerged, cavernous chamber where the water is waist-deep and thick with algae, illuminated by harsh, portable lanterns set up by the Keepers.
  - *Notable Features:* Massive, carved stone pillars depicting unrecognizable denizens; a large, complex system of chains and pulleys lifting debris; a rusted, ancient metal vault door that is partially wedged open.
- **The Specimen Vault (Deep Level):** A nearly fully submerged, claustrophobic lower chamber that the Keepers have only just breached, dominated by a series of enormous, thick-glass vats.
  - *Notable Features:* Several intact transparent vats containing strange, preserved flora and the unsettling forms of unknown, massive creatures suspended in murky fluid; a rusted but complex mechanical life-support system of brass pipes; a sealed, pressure-locked central cylinder humming with residual tension.

### Current Occupants & Hazards
The ruins are occupied by an aggressive expedition of the Keepers in Iron, led by the fanatical Barek. Their sole goal is to recover the ancient relics hidden within, and they violently oppose any interference. Hazards include the heavily armored Keepers, the precarious and slippery iron scaffolding, the deep, stagnant water that can easily drown the unwary, and the unsettling potential that the ancient fluid or preserved specimens in the deep vats might be toxic or unexpectedly viable if disturbed.

### Secrets & Clues
- *The Doomsday Specimen:* Topographical maps and hastily translated notes in the Scaffolding command post reveal that the Keepers believe the central, heavily reinforced vat in the lower chamber houses an ancient, dormant biological weapon or apex predator, rather than a mechanical relic.
- *The Weakened Foundation:* A close inspection of the Flooded Undercroft reveals that the Keepers' heavy steam pumps are rapidly eroding the ziggurat's structural integrity, threatening an imminent, catastrophic collapse of the entire ruin into the swamp.
- *The Vat Release:* A hidden sequence of stone panels near the jammed metal vault door in the Undercroft acts as a master purge valve for the mechanical preservation systems; triggering it could drain the vats or flood the chamber with toxic preservative fluid.
