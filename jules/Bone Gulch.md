> [!tip] Bone Gulch
> **Control:** Marquisate (Nominal) / Lizard Cult (Influence)
> **Dominant Inhabitants:** Lizardfolk, Meerkats, Desert Rats
> **Atmosphere & Effort State:** Oppressive & Press
> **Shape Flow:** Stagnant heat is sinking into the earth

### Description & History
Bone Gulch is a harsh, arid settlement in the [[Saurian March]], built directly into and around the fossilized ribs and skull of a colossal, ancient desert leviathan. The bleached bones rise from the shifting sands like the framework of a forgotten cathedral, providing sparse shade and structural support for the sandstone dwellings that cling to them. The settlement represents a classic example of moldy worldbuilding: the current inhabitants have excavated the leviathan's marrow-hollowed bones and the sandstone beneath to escape the relentless sun, building upon layers of older, buried foundations from long-dead desert civilizations.

### At First Sight
The scorching sun beats down on the bleached white ribs that arch over the main thoroughfare, casting harsh, striped shadows across the dusty ground. The air is stifling and oppressive, thick with the smell of dry dust, roasted sweetroot, and the faint, coppery tang of old blood.

Sandstone structures are haphazardly built into the curves of the ancient bones, their flat roofs covered in canvas awnings that flap lazily in the sluggish breeze. Below, the ground is honeycombed with entrances to cooler subterranean levels, where the true life of the clearing thrives away from the punishing heat.

### Factions & Control
- **Marquisate:** The Marquisate holds nominal control over Bone Gulch, maintaining a small, sweltering garrison near the leviathan's skull. Their soldiers are demoralized, under-supplied, and largely ignore the deeper underground workings of the town.
- **Lizard Cult:** The Lizard Cult exerts the true influence over the populace. They see the leviathan's bones as a sacred monument to the [[Great Wyrm]] and preach under its ribcage.
- **Underground Duchy:** Operating entirely in the shadows, the Duchy uses the natural and excavated tunnels beneath the town for a thriving smuggling and gambling operation.

### Core Conflicts & Impending Escalations
- **The Cult vs. The Garrison:** The Lizard Cult is growing bolder, inciting the locals against the Marquisate's tax collectors. If the tension breaks, a full-scale riot could erupt, leading the Marquisate to call for brutal reinforcements.
- **The Duchy's Greed:** Mozz's gambling den is drawing too much attention. The Lizard Cult views his operation as a sinful distraction, and they are preparing to raid the underground tunnels to purge the "unbelievers."

### Important Locations

> [!tip] The Velvet Cavern
> **Type:** Subterranean Gambling Den & Smuggler's Hub
> **Atmosphere & Effort State:** Sultry & Glide
> **Shape Flow:** Illicit wealth is condensing in the shadows
>
> ### Description & Stratified History
> Hidden deep beneath Bone Gulch, accessible through a maze of sandstone tunnels and hollowed-out fossilized roots, lies the Velvet Cavern. Originally a cistern for an ancient, buried outpost, the cavern has been repurposed by the [[Underground Duchy]]. The cold stone walls are draped in heavy, stolen Marquisate tapestries, and the air is thick with the sweet smoke of exotic herbs.
>
> ### At First Sight
> Stepping into the Velvet Cavern is a jarring transition from the blazing heat above. The air is cool, humid, and heavy with murmured conversation and the clinking of coins. Dim lanterns cast a warm, golden glow over plush velvet cushions and scarred wooden tables where denizens risk their meager earnings.
>
> ### Distinct Areas
> - **The High-Stakes Pit:** A sunken area in the center of the cavern surrounded by a wrought-iron railing.
>   - *Notable Features:* A massive, polished mahogany gambling table, heavily armed Duchy guards in the shadows, and a glittering chandelier of stolen crystals.
> - **Mozz's Parlor:** A raised alcove sealed off by heavy velvet curtains, where Mozz conducts his most sensitive business.
>   - *Notable Features:* A luxurious silk chaise lounge, an iron lockbox bolted to the floor, and a secret escape tunnel hidden behind a tapestry.
> - **The Smuggler's Warren:** A series of rough-hewn tunnels branching off the main cavern, used for storing illicit goods.
>   - *Notable Features:* Stacked crates of stolen Marquisate supplies, barrels of rare Anuran wine, and concealed tripwire alarms.
>
> ### Current Occupants & Hazards
> Mozz (a corpulent mole and Duchy agent) oversees the operation, flanked by heavily armed mole enforcers. Desperate locals, corrupt Marquisate guards, and shady merchants fill the room. The main hazard is the volatile mix of armed, drunken, and indebted patrons.
>
> ### Secrets & Clues
> - One of the Marquisate guards, deep in debt, has the patrol schedule for the next incoming supply caravan written on a napkin.
> - A ledger in Mozz's parlor contains a coded entry detailing the recent passage of a rare, exotic creature matching the description of "Perry the Platypus."
> - A Cultist spy is disguised as a waitress, taking notes on Mozz's operations for an impending raid.
>
> ### Loot & Resources
> - *Duchy Lockbox:* Contains 5-Value in mixed coins and smuggled gems (Requires heavy tools or lockpicking to open).
> - *Smuggled Marquisate Rations:* 3 uses of high-quality food.

### Notable NPCs
- **[[Mozz]]:** A corpulent mole who runs the Velvet Cavern. He controls the smuggling routes and holds the debts of half the town.
- **Acolyte S'sura:** A zealous [[Lizardfolk]] preacher who views the bones of the leviathan as holy and seeks to purge the town of Marquisate and Duchy influence.
- **Captain Vane:** The exhausted feline commander of the Marquisate garrison, who just wants to survive her deployment and ignores the town's illicit activities.

### Special Resource / Rules
- **The Heat of the March:** Any vagabond spending a full day on the surface of Bone Gulch without proper shade or ample water must mark 1-exhaustion due to the oppressive sun.

### Rumors
- "I heard that the local Cultists are furious about a Heretic in the area. A lizardfolk named [[Savage Wyrmbourne]]—they say there's a hefty bounty for anyone who brings them in to face the Cult's judgment."
- "The garrison commander is taking bribes from the Moles. She hasn't sent a patrol down into the tunnels in months."

### Secrets & Hooks
- **The Trail to Perry:** [[Wren Greenboot]]'s tracking of their target has hit a wall, but Mozz's smugglers recently transported a strange, aquatic creature in a sealed tank heading deeper into the Duchy tunnels. Mozz will trade this information, but only for a steep price or a dangerous favor.
- **The Heretic's Welcome:** If [[Savage Wyrmbourne]] arrives in Bone Gulch, they will find the local Lizard Cult intensely hostile, actively hunting for them due to their radical beliefs regarding the Great Wyrm.

### First Session Encounters (2d6)
- **2-3:** A sudden, violent sandstorm forces everyone underground, trapping the vagabonds in a tunnel with a desperate Marquisate patrol and angry Cultists.
- **4-5:** Duchy enforcers attempt to shake down the vagabonds for "tolls" to use the subterranean passages.
- **6-8:** Acolyte S'sura leads a mob of Lizard Cultists in a fiery protest right outside the Marquisate garrison.
- **9-10:** Mozz summons the vagabonds, offering them a lucrative job to deal with a Giant Tarantula that has nested in one of his smuggling tunnels.
- **11-12:** A frantic local begs the vagabonds for help—their child wandered too deep into the leviathan's buried lower ribcage and hasn't returned.
