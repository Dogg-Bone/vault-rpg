# Fruit Valley

A sprawling metropolis nestled in a fertile basin, Fruit Valley is the breadbasket of the Woodland—or at least, its fruit basket. Boasting a massive population and divided into eight distinct districts, the city is a chaotic melting pot where every faction vies for dominance. No single power controls the city; instead, the Marquise de Cat maintains strongholds in industrial zones, the Eyrie Dynasties claim the highest peaks, the Riverfolk dominate the waterways, and various insurgent and expansionist factions carve out their own territories in the streets, cellars, and slums.

Mice are the most populous denizens overall, but each district has its own dominant culture and demographic. The sheer size and wealth of Fruit Valley make it a vital strategic prize, resulting in a delicate, powder-keg peace where outright war is bad for business, but shadow warfare is a daily reality.

## Special Resource: Valley Preserves
Because of the immense agricultural output of the valley, a specialized industry of preserving fruit has emerged.
**Valley Preserves** (1-Wear, 1-Depletion): When you consume a jar of Valley Preserves while resting, clear 1 Exhaustion. If you share it with an NPC, they will be favorably disposed to you for the duration of your interaction, granting you +1 to `Trust Fate` when attempting to persuade or negotiate with them.

## Observed Holidays

Fruit Valley celebrates three major holidays, each temporarily halting the bitter factional conflicts as the denizens come together—mostly out of tradition, but also out of a desperate need to maintain morale.

### The Blossom Festival
Celebrated in early spring when the orchards burst into color. The city is decorated with petals and lanterns. It is a time of courtship, new contracts, and frantic planting. Factions often use the noise and distraction of the festival to move illicit goods or assassinate rivals under the cover of fireworks.

### The Great Harvest Crush
Occurring in late autumn, this is a week-long frenzy of labor and celebration. The entire city smells of crushed apples, berries, and fermenting cider. Everyone, regardless of faction or species, is expected to help with the harvest.
> [!tip] The Crush Exhaustion
> During The Great Harvest Crush, any Vagabond who helps with the labor (which is expected if they want to remain in the city's good graces) must mark 1 Exhaustion at the start of the session, but gains the temporary tag *Respected Worker*, making it easier to `Meet Someone Important` among the working class.

### The Frost Vigil
A somber, quiet night in deep winter. Denizens light single candles in their windows to guide the spirits of those lost in the Woodland back to the hearth. It is considered extremely taboo to shed blood on the night of the Frost Vigil; even the Marquise and the Eyrie observe a strict ceasefire.

## Districts

### 1. The Tangled Orchards
The agricultural heart of Fruit Valley, dominated by massive, overgrown fruit trees. Paths here are winding dirt trails beneath a thick canopy of branches.
* **Dominant Population:** Mice
* **Factions Present:** Woodland Alliance (Hidden camps among the roots), Marquise de Cat (Tax collectors and lumberjacks eyeing the older trees).

#### Notable NPCs
**Brie "Thorn" Meadowsweet** (Mouse, Woodland Alliance Organizer)
* **Location:** A disguised root-cellar beneath the oldest apple tree.
* **Fear:** That the Marquise will start clear-cutting the orchards to flush out her rebels.
* **Secret:** She has been stockpiling black powder stolen from Marquise shipments, hidden in hollowed-out tree trunks.
* **Request for the Vagabonds:** Sabotage a Marquise logging saw; offers a finely crafted Alliance shortbow and safe passage through the deep woods.
* **Opinion/Info:** "I hear the Riverfolk at the market are inflating the prices of wheat just to starve the poorest districts."

#### First Session Encounters (2d6)
| Roll | Encounter |
| :--- | :--- |
| **2-3** | A Woodland Alliance ambush gone wrong; Marquise guards are pinning down a few poorly-armed mouse rebels. |
| **4-6** | An overly aggressive Marquise tax collector is shaking down an elderly orchard-keeper for more fruit. |
| **7-9** | A sudden, localized swarm of hungry beetles threatens to ruin a section of the crop; locals are desperately fighting them off. |
| **10-11** | You stumble upon a hidden cache of Alliance weapons, but it's rigged with a crude alarm trap. |
| **12** | A massive, ancient apple tree is creaking ominously, threatening to crush several homes beneath its rotting weight. |

### 2. The Cider Wards
An industrial district filled with the constant sound of grinding presses and the sharp smell of vinegar and cider. The buildings are brick and timber, constantly slick with spilled juice.
* **Dominant Population:** Rabbits
* **Factions Present:** Marquise de Cat (They own the largest presses and control the export).

#### Notable NPCs
**Foreman Thaddeus** (Cat, Marquise Overseer)
* **Location:** The balcony of the grandest cider press, shouting orders.
* **Fear:** Failing to meet the Marquis's exorbitant export quotas.
* **Secret:** He is secretly skimming profits to pay off massive gambling debts he owes to the Corvid Conspiracy.
* **Request for the Vagabonds:** Recover a "lost" ledger from a rival press without alerting the Marquise command; offers a pouch of Marquise minted coins and a writ of safe passage.
* **Opinion/Info:** "Those Eyrie birds in the Canopy Heights look down on us, but they'd starve without the cider we ship up there."

#### First Session Encounters (2d6)
| Roll | Encounter |
| :--- | :--- |
| **2-3** | A massive vat of fermenting cider bursts, creating a dangerous, sticky flood that threatens to drown several workers. |
| **4-6** | Exhausted rabbit workers are staging an impromptu strike, and Marquise guards are moving in with batons drawn. |
| **7-9** | A Corvid debt collector is subtly threatening a Marquise foreman in a dark alleyway. |
| **10-11** | A runaway cart loaded with heavy oak barrels is careening down a steep cobblestone street. |
| **12** | You catch a rabbit worker attempting to sabotage a Marquise press by throwing a heavy wrench into the gears. |

### 3. The River Market
A bustling, chaotic district built on docks, piers, and barges along the river that cuts through the valley. It is loud, colorful, and smells of fish and wet timber.
* **Dominant Population:** Foxes
* **Factions Present:** Riverfolk Company (They control the trade routes and the main trading post).

#### Notable NPCs
**Skipper "Silver-Tongue" Vix** (Fox, Riverfolk Merchant)
* **Current Wares:** Exotic spices, finely woven ropes, slightly damaged Marquise armor.
* **Can Acquire:** Rare poisons, forged transit papers.
* **Location:** A lavishly decorated trading barge moored near the center of the market.
* **Fear:** A newly formed pirate gang downstream disrupting her shipping lanes.
* **Secret:** She occasionally sinks her own competitors' barges and blames it on the pirates.
* **Request for the Vagabonds:** Escort a highly valuable (and illegal) shipment of root-wine past the Marquise checkpoints; offers a lifetime discount at her trading post and a sturdy Riverfolk skiff.
* **Opinion/Info:** "If you want true power in this city, don't look at the Cats or the Birds. Look at the shadows in the Old Tunnels."

#### First Session Encounters (2d6)
| Roll | Encounter |
| :--- | :--- |
| **2-3** | A heated dispute between a Riverfolk merchant and a local buyer escalates into a full-blown dockside brawl. |
| **4-6** | A barge breaks loose from its moorings and is rapidly drifting toward the perilous rapids at the edge of town. |
| **7-9** | A slippery thief snatches a valuable artifact from a merchant's stall and darts through the crowded market. |
| **10-11** | Riverfolk enforcers are heavily taxing a small family of mice trying to cross the toll bridge. |
| **12** | A strange, heavily guarded Riverfolk shipment arrives in the dead of night, and several factions have sent spies to watch it. |

### 4. The Canopy Heights
Built into the highest branches of the oldest, most massive trees at the edge of the valley. Connected by rope bridges and walkways, it offers a strategic view of the entire city.
* **Dominant Population:** Birds
* **Factions Present:** Eyrie Dynasties (House Strix maintains a heavily fortified roost here).

#### Notable NPCs
**Commander Alden Strix** (Owl, Eyrie Commander)
* **Location:** The tactical map room in the highest roost.
* **Fear:** That the Marquise forces below will eventually build siege engines capable of reaching the Heights.
* **Secret:** His forces are suffering from a terrible illness, and he is desperately hiding the weakness from his rivals in the Dynasties.
* **Request for the Vagabonds:** Infiltrate the Marquise armory in the Cider Wards and steal the plans for their new ballistae; offers high-grade Eyrie weaponry and a royal favor.
* **Opinion/Info:** "The rats in the Bramble Slums are organizing. They call themselves the Lord of Hundreds, but they are just barbarians waiting to burn this city to the ground."

| Roll | Encounter |

| **4-6** | An Eyrie patrol aggressively demands to inspect your belongings, citing a "security lockdown." |
| **7-9** | A fierce windstorm batters the heights, threatening to tear roofs off the more fragile nests. |
| **10-11** | You overhear two Eyrie soldiers discussing the Commander's secret illness in hushed, panicked tones. |
| **12** | A Marquise spy is caught attempting to scale the tree trunks and is about to be thrown from the heights without a trial. |

### 5. The Bramble Slums
A massive, sprawling shantytown on the outskirts of the city, choked with thorny blackberry bushes. It is fiercely independent and aggressively hostile to tax collectors and guards.
* **Dominant Population:** Mice
* **Factions Present:** Lord of Hundreds (A growing mob stirring up resentment against the "soft" city dwellers).

#### Notable NPCs
**Cinder** (Mouse, Lord of Hundreds Rabble-Rouser)
* **Location:** Standing on a ruined cart in the slum's central square, shouting to a crowd.
* **Fear:** That the mob will lose its fury and return to being compliant workers for the Marquise.
* **Secret:** He is secretly terrified of the Keepers in Iron, having barely escaped one of their purges in his youth.
* **Request for the Vagabonds:** Steal a cache of high-quality weapons from the Keepers in the Sunlit Terraces; offers to name them "Honorary Warlords" and promises a share of the plunder when the city burns.
* **Opinion/Info:** "The birds in the trees think they are safe, but dry wood burns just as well as anything else."

| **7-9** | A desperate beggar tries to pickpocket you, but immediately bursts into tears if caught. |

### 6. The Fermenting Cellars
A sprawling network of underground storage rooms, old basements, and catacombs beneath the city. It is damp, dark, and smells of mold and old wine.
* **Dominant Population:** Mice
* **Factions Present:** Corvid Conspiracy (They use the cellars as a base of operations for smuggling and blackmail), Underground Duchy (A small, struggling expeditionary force trying to establish a tunnel connection).

#### Notable NPCs
**"Granny" Hazel / Operative 'Nightshade'** (Mouse, Corvid Conspiracy Plotter)
* **Location:** A seemingly innocent knitting circle in a damp cellar room.
* **Fear:** That the Underground Duchy will dig into her secret smuggling tunnels.
* **Secret:** She is forging official Marquise export documents to steal entire shipments of cider right out from under their noses.
* **Request for the Vagabonds:** Deliver a "special" (tainted with mild, non-lethal sickness-inducing mushrooms) pie to a strict Marquise guard captain; offers a large sum of untraceable coins and a favor from the Magpie Syndicate.
* **Opinion/Info:** "The Duchy moles are digging in the wrong direction. They're about to hit an underground aquifer and drown themselves."

### 7. The Sunlit Terraces
The wealthiest and most pristine district, featuring beautiful stone terraces built into the hillside. The architecture is ancient and well-maintained.
* **Dominant Population:** Rabbits
* **Factions Present:** Keepers in Iron (They have established an enclave here to protect ancient relics hidden beneath the terraces).

#### Notable NPCs
**Brother Oakhart** (Badger, Keepers in Iron Devout)
* **Location:** Meditating in a serene courtyard surrounded by armed guards.
* **Fear:** That the chaos of the city will lead to the destruction of the relics they are sworn to protect.
* **Secret:** The "relic" they are guarding is actually an ancient map revealing the location of a forgotten super-weapon, not a religious artifact.
* **Request for the Vagabonds:** Escort an ancient, fragile tablet to a secure location outside the city; offers heavy badger-forged armor and an oath of protection.
* **Opinion/Info:** "The Corvid 'Granny' in the cellars is not who she seems. I smell deceit on her every time she comes to the surface."

### 8. The Old Tunnels
The deepest and oldest part of the city, a confusing warren of natural caves and ancient, forgotten masonry that pre-dates the current inhabitants.
* **Dominant Population:** Mice (mostly outcasts and exiles)
* **Factions Present:** Underground Duchy (Their main, albeit small, toehold in the city, trying to connect to the greater Duchy network).

#### Notable NPCs
**Captain Digby** (Mole, Underground Duchy Officer)
* **Location:** A hastily fortified camp in a large cavern, surrounded by maps.
* **Fear:** That his expedition will be deemed a failure by the Parliament, resulting in his permanent exile.
* **Secret:** He has lost communication with the main Duchy forces and is operating entirely without support or orders.
* **Request for the Vagabonds:** Clear out a nest of dangerous, subterranean beasts blocking their main digging route; offers high-quality digging tools and a writ of Duchy citizenship.
* **Opinion/Info:** "The Lord of Hundreds are fools. Real power isn't in burning the surface, it's in controlling the earth beneath it."

## Rumors & Hooks

* **The Stolen Harvest:** A massive shipment of Valley Preserves intended for the Marquise capital has gone missing. The Marquise blame the Woodland Alliance, the Alliance blames the Riverfolk, and the Riverfolk are quietly selling it off in the Fermenting Cellars. Vagabonds are hired by Foreman Thaddeus to find it before his superiors notice.
* **The Sinking City:** The Underground Duchy's desperate, uncoordinated digging in the Old Tunnels is beginning to destabilize the foundations of the Sunlit Terraces. The Keepers in Iron are demanding the Duchy cease operations immediately, and are preparing to send heavily armored badgers down into the tunnels to enforce it.
* **The Mad King of the Canopy:** Rumors are spreading through the city that Commander Alden Strix has succumbed to his secret illness and has been replaced by a body double controlled by House Condor. House Strix loyalists are quietly seeking discrete mercenaries to break into the Commander's private roost and discover the truth.
