# House Strix

**Species:** Owl
**Focus:** Military Might and Strategy
**Naming Convention:** Germanic

## Overview
House Strix is the indisputable martial backbone of the [[Eyrie Dynasties]]. Renowned for their imposing physical stature and unparalleled nocturnal vision, the owls of House Strix have historically commanded the vanguard of the Eyrie's armies. They are austere, disciplined, and view the reclamation of the Woodland as a purely military campaign to be won through overwhelming force and tactical superiority.

## NPC Leader: Commander Dietrich von Strix
Dietrich is a towering Great Horned Owl and a seasoned veteran of the Woodland wars. His plumage is scarred from numerous skirmishes with the Marquisate.
- **Location:** Usually found coordinating troop movements in high-altitude roosts overlooking contested clearings.
- **Fear:** That the Eyrie's constant infighting will render them too weak to repel the Marquisate permanently.
- **Secret:** Dietrich lost a crucial battle due to a tactical error he blames on an order from a previous ruler, but he claimed it was due to enemy sabotage to maintain morale.
- **Request for the [[Vagabonds]]:** He needs a group of denizens to map out a heavily fortified Marquisate supply line. Compensation: High-quality, military-grade rations and a commendation that opens doors with Eyrie patrols.
- **Opinion/Info:** Believes that the leaders of [[House Cardinal]] are "too busy hammering nails to hold a spear," though he secretly relies heavily on their craftsmanship for his troops' armor.

## Brief History
In the old days of the Woodland, before the collapse of the Dynasties, House Strix provided the personal guard for the reigning monarch. They were instrumental in the original consolidation of the Woodland under Eyrie rule, employing brutal but effective shock-troop tactics. When the Dynasties fell into civil war, House Strix suffered heavy casualties trying to maintain order, a loss that still breeds resentment towards the other, more politically minded Houses.

## Notable Facts, Connections, and Resources
- **Military Academies:** House Strix maintains rigorous training roosts in the tallest ancient trees, producing the finest commanders and soldiers in the Woodland.
- **The Night Watch:** They utilize their natural nocturnal abilities to run extensive scouting and night-raid operations, terrorizing enemy encampments.
- **Rivalry:** They view [[House Condor]]'s despotic tendencies as inefficient and bad for morale, preferring strict military law over arbitrary tyranny.
- **Alliances:** While they scoff at the artisans of House Cardinal, Strix strongly prefers to outfit their elite vanguard with Cardinal-forged heavy plate armor and weaponry, leading to a tense, mutually beneficial relationship.
- **Resources:** They control significant caches of pre-collapse Eyrie weaponry, hidden deep within forgotten roosts.
