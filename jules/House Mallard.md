# House Mallard

**Species:** Mallard Duck
**Focus:** Riverways, Naval Strength, and Machiavellian Diplomacy
**Naming Convention:** Dutch

## Overview
House Mallard is the sleek, silver-tongued serpent within the Eyrie's nest. They control the rivers and lakes of the Woodland, giving them unparalleled mobility and control over vital trade routes. Outwardly, they are affable diplomats, merchants, and ferrymen, known for their bright plumage and jovial demeanor. Inwardly, they are ruthless pragmatists who view the Woodland's politics as a grand game of chess, using espionage, blackmail, and economic pressure to maneuver the other Houses—and their enemies—into doing their bidding.

## NPC Leader: Ambassador Hendrik van Mallard
Hendrik is an impeccably preened mallard who always seems to be smiling, though the smile rarely reaches his cold, calculating eyes. He travels via a heavily guarded, luxurious river barge.
- **Location:** Aboard his flagship barge, usually moored in a clearing heavily reliant on river trade.
- **Fear:** That the Riverfolk Company will establish a complete monopoly over the waterways, rendering House Mallard's navy and influence obsolete.
- **Secret:** Hendrik actually orchestrated a recent "rebel" attack on a Marquisate lumber shipment, not to strike a blow for the Eyrie, but to artificially inflate the price of wood he was stockpiling.
- **Request for the Vagabonds:** He needs Vagabonds to deliver a sealed, highly sensitive (and likely forged) document to a prominent Woodland Alliance leader without being seen by Eyrie loyalists. Compensation: Forged transit papers allowing safe passage through any Eyrie-held territory, plus a substantial pouch of coins.
- **Opinion/Info:** He views the Riverfolk Company as "crass opportunists who lack the vision to rule," but he respects their sheer economic power and avoids direct conflict with them.

## Brief History
House Mallard has always kept one foot out of the canopy and one in the water. While other Houses fought bloody wars for control of the trees, the Mallards quietly seized control of the docks and ferries. During the Eyrie's collapse, they suffered the fewest casualties, using their naval mobility to retreat to heavily fortified island strongholds. They emerged from the civil wars wealthier and more influential than before, having profited by selling safe passage and smuggled goods to all sides.

## Notable Facts, Connections, and Resources
- **The River Navy:** The Eyrie's ability to move troops quickly across the Woodland relies heavily on House Mallard's fleet of flat-bottomed troop transports and swift patrol boats.
- **The Whisper Network:** Through their extensive trading contacts and ferrymen, House Mallard operates the most effective intelligence network in the Woodland, rivaling even the Corvid Conspiracy in sheer volume of gathered secrets.
- **Diplomatic Corps:** When the Eyrie needs to negotiate (or stall for time), they send the Mallards. They are masters of making treaties that sound fair but contain devastating loopholes.
- **Rivalry:** They are locked in a quiet, vicious cold war with the Riverfolk Company over control of the rivers, utilizing sabotage and price-gouging rather than open warfare.
- **Resources:** They control vast amounts of wealth, exclusive trade contacts, and a stranglehold on river-based transportation.
