# Bastion de la Lisière

The Bastion de la Lisière (Bastion of the Border) is a massive, soot-stained fortress built by the [[Marquise de Cat]]. Situated strategically near the edge of [[The Woodland]], it serves as the primary military command center, industrial hub, and defensive bulwark for the Marquise's ambitions in the region. Plumes of acrid smoke constantly rise from its central forges, and the rhythmic pounding of industry can be heard echoing through the surrounding forests.

The Keep is not just a military installation; it is a sprawling, multi-tiered complex designed to project power. The architecture is brutalist and utilitarian, favoring heavy stone blocks, iron reinforcements, and sharp angles designed to deflect siege weapons. It is a place of industry and war, where conscripts are trained, weapons are forged, and campaigns are planned.

## Keep Mechanics

### Custom Player Move: Infiltrate the Bastion
> [!tip] Infiltrate the Bastion
> When you **attempt to navigate or infiltrate a new wing of the Bastion de la Lisière**, roll with Finesse or Cunning (your choice).
> - On a 10+, you successfully navigate the area and remain undetected. Choose 1:
>   - You bypass an environmental hazard without spending resources.
>   - You glean a valuable piece of intelligence about the Keep's layout or guard rotations.
> - On a 7-9, you make progress, but face a complication. The GM chooses 1:
>   - You are spotted by a patrol or attendant, requiring a quick excuse, bribe, or violence.
>   - You trigger a minor trap or hazard; mark 1-wear on an item.
>   - You take longer than expected; the Keep's alert level rises.
> - On a 6-, the Keep's defenses bear down on you. The GM makes a hard move, and you are cornered or captured.

### GM Moves for the Keep
> [!tip] GM Moves: Bastion de la Lisière
> - **Sound the Alarm:** Increase the visible presence of guards (Conscripts, Gens d'armes, or Chevaliers).
> - **Industrial Hazard:** Introduce a sudden complication from the Keep's machinery (venting steam, a collapsed gantry, an automated mechanism).
> - **Bureaucratic Red Tape:** A patrol demands proper documentation, insignia, or a password that the Vagabonds lack.
> - **Lock Down:** Portcullises drop, doors lock, and pathways are sealed, funneling intruders toward a heavily fortified chokepoint.
> - **Deploy Elite Forces:** A Chevalier or a specialized strike team is dispatched specifically to hunt down the intruders.

## Layout: The Fractal Fortress (25 Rooms)

The Bastion is divided into five distinct wings, each serving a specific function. The structure itself is a massive 5-room dungeon, and each wing within it is also a 5-room dungeon sequence (Guardian, Puzzle/Roleplay, Setback, Climax, Reward).

### Wing 1: The Outer Ward (The Guardian of the Keep)
**Architecture & Atmosphere:** The first line of defense. Thick, imposing stone walls with iron-reinforced gates. The air is filled with the smell of mud, sweat, and woodsmoke. Courtyards are vast but cluttered with supply wagons, drilling conscripts, and temporary wooden structures. It feels chaotic but tightly controlled.

1. **The Iron Gate (Guardian):** Massive twin doors flanked by watchtowers. Heavily guarded by arbaletriers and inspected by a meticulous Senechaux. Access requires forged papers or a hefty bribe.
2. **The Muster Yard (Puzzle/Roleplay):** A sprawling, muddy courtyard where conscripts are drilled. Vagabonds must blend in with the chaotic movement or talk their way past the drill sergeants to cross the yard safely.
3. **The Quartermaster's Checkpoint (Setback):** A narrow chokepoint filled with requisition forms and skeptical quartermasters. A sudden inspection or a missing stamp threatens to expose the group.
4. **The Barracks Blockhouse (Climax):** A sturdy, fortified building housing off-duty soldiers. Bypassing or quietly neutralizing the guards here is necessary to access the inner Keep.
5. **The Smuggler's Postern (Reward):** A hidden, lesser-known sally port utilized by corrupt quartermasters. Provides a secure entry point into the Inner Ward and a stash of discarded, poorly tracked supplies (minor loot).

### Wing 2: The Industrial Forges (The Puzzle/Roleplay of the Keep)
**Architecture & Atmosphere:** A sweltering, deafening network of subterranean and semi-subterranean chambers. The glow of molten metal illuminates the soot-blackened walls. Machinery clanks and hisses, and the air is thick with ash and sulfur. Catwalks of rusted iron crisscross over bubbling vats.

6. **The Ventilation Shafts (Guardian):** Blistering heat and toxic fumes protect the entrance to the forges. Navigating this area requires overcoming environmental hazards rather than fighting guards.
7. **The Forecat's Office (Puzzle/Roleplay):** A suspended, glass-paned office overlooking the main foundry floor. The Vagabonds must acquire the daily maintenance schedule from the Forecat to avoid being caught on the gantries.
8. **The Piston Walkway (Setback):** A narrow, moving catwalk that threatens to crush or drop intruders into the slag pits below if they misstep or are interrupted by a roaming inspector.
9. **The Grand Crucible (Climax):** The heart of the industrial wing. The Vagabonds must sabotage or bypass a massive, guarded furnace mechanism that powers the Keep's internal defenses.
10. **The Prototype Armory (Reward):** A locked vault containing experimental or masterwork Marquise weaponry. (Loot: 1 weapon with a +valuable or unique tag).

### Wing 3: The Dungeons & Interrogation Wing (The Setback of the Keep)
**Architecture & Atmosphere:** Damp, cold, and eerily quiet compared to the rest of the Keep. Rough-hewn stone walls drip with condensation. Iron bars, heavy oak doors, and the faint, unsettling echoes of prisoners create a sense of dread and isolation.

11. **The Warden's Desk (Guardian):** A heavily fortified security station manned by a cruel Prevot and several specialized guards. They hold the keys to the lower levels.
12. **The Cell Blocks (Puzzle/Roleplay):** Rows of cages holding political prisoners, dissidents, and captured rebels. Vagabonds must decide whether to free them (causing a distraction but risking the alarm) or sneak past their desperate pleas.
13. **The Sensory Deprivation Pit (Setback):** An area shrouded in absolute darkness and magical or mechanical silence. Intruders can easily become disoriented or separated, making them vulnerable to ambushes.
14. **The Interrogation Theater (Climax):** A gruesome, well-lit room where high-value targets are questioned. The Vagabonds must confront the Chief Interrogator and their elite guards to proceed or rescue a vital ally.
15. **The Evidence Locker (Reward):** A secure cache containing confiscated items, blackmail material, and secret correspondence detailing Marquise troop movements (valuable intel).

### Wing 4: The Noble Quarters (The Climax of the Keep)
**Architecture & Atmosphere:** A stark contrast to the lower levels. Opulent, though still maintaining a rigid, militaristic aesthetic. Polished marble floors, rich red tapestries bearing the Marquise's crest, and polished mahogany furniture. The air smells of expensive perfumes and fine wine.

16. **The Honor Guard Corridor (Guardian):** A long, well-lit hallway patrolled exclusively by elite Chevaliers in gleaming armor. Stealth is difficult, and combat here is highly lethal.
17. **The Grand Banquet Hall (Puzzle/Roleplay):** A massive room where off-duty officers and nobles dine. The Vagabonds must navigate the room by posing as servants or musicians, blending into the high-society atmosphere.
18. **The Gallery of Conquest (Setback):** A museum-like hallway filled with trapped display cases and alarms. Stealing a shiny trinket or stepping on the wrong tile triggers the Keep's internal lockdown.
19. **The Commander's Solarium (Climax):** A heavily fortified, luxurious room where the Keep's commanders strategize. A confrontation here involves highly skilled duelists and strategic masterminds.
20. **The Treasury Vault (Reward):** A heavily locked safe containing the Keep's operational funds and rare artifacts. (High-value loot).

### Wing 5: The Command Zenith (The Reward/Resolution of the Keep)
**Architecture & Atmosphere:** The highest point of the Keep, offering a commanding view of the surrounding woodland. The architecture is imposing and austere, designed to intimidate. Large glass windows, heavy iron-wrought desks, and massive map tables dominate the space.

21. **The Spiral Ascent (Guardian):** A steep, exposed stairwell leading to the command tower. It is guarded by automated defenses (like mounted ballistae) and elite sentries.
22. **The War Room (Puzzle/Roleplay):** A massive chamber dominated by a strategic map of The Woodland. Vagabonds must quickly decipher the troop movements and alter the orders without the attending scribes noticing.
23. **The Pigeon Roost (Setback):** The communications hub. Sending a false message is vital, but the trained messenger birds are highly temperamental and loud, risking exposure if not handled delicately.
24. **The Marechal's Office (Climax):** The final confrontation. The Vagabonds must face the Marechal of the Bastion, a formidable opponent surrounded by their most loyal bodyguards, in a tense standoff or brutal fight.
25. **The Master Blueprint/The Marquis's Secret (Reward):** The ultimate prize of the Keep. Access to the Bastion's master plans, uncovering a major Marquise conspiracy, or securing an artifact that could shift the balance of power in the Woodland.
