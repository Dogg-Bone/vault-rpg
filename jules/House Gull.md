# House Gull

**Species:** Seagull
**Focus:** Coastal Raiding, Seafaring, and Plunder
**Naming Convention:** Nordic

## Overview
House Gull represents the wild, untamed fury of the Eyrie Dynasties' naval forces. Operating primarily on the major lakes and coastal borders of the Woodland, they are fiercely independent raiders and explorers. Where other Houses value political cunning, architectural grandeur, or strict military discipline, House Gull values strength, bravery, and the spoils of war. They are brash, loud, and largely self-sufficient, preferring the mobility of their swift raiding vessels to the stagnant life within a traditional roost.

## NPC Leader: Jarl Leif of House Gull
Leif is a scarred, boisterous seagull with sea-salt permanently crusted in his feathers. He leads his followers not by divine right, but by being the loudest, strongest, and most successful raider among them.
- **Location:** At the helm of a massive, heavily reinforced longboat, or holding court in a rowdy, temporary coastal encampment.
- **Fear:** That the Eyrie's leadership will become too "civilized" and soft, losing their warrior edge to endless political bickering in the canopy.
- **Secret:** Leif actively fences stolen Marquisate goods to the Corvid Conspiracy in exchange for detailed maps of poorly defended inland supply routes.
- **Request for the Vagabonds:** He needs Vagabonds to quietly sabotage a Marquisate blockade further up the river so his longboats can push into rich, unplundered territory. Compensation: A generous share of the resulting plunder (valuable trade goods) and a carved wooden token guaranteeing safe passage through Gull-controlled waters.
- **Opinion/Info:** He dismisses House Mallard as "soft, river-paddling merchants who play at being sailors," though he begrudgingly respects their logistical capabilities.

## Brief History
Historically, House Gull served as the Eyrie's coastal defense and expeditionary force. When the Dynasties fell into civil war, the Gulls largely abandoned the inner Woodland's political squabbles. They retreated to the coasts and deep lakes, surviving the chaotic years by raiding independent settlements and, later, the Marquisate's incoming supply lines. They have only recently reintegrated with the main Eyrie coalition, lured back by the promise of officially sanctioned plunder and the glory of driving out the invaders.

## Notable Facts, Connections, and Resources
- **The Long Fleet:** While House Mallard maintains an efficient network of flat-bottomed transports for river occupation, House Gull builds and commands swift, durable longboats designed for rough waters and lightning-fast coastal raids.
- **Raider Economy:** House Gull is highly self-reliant. Instead of depending on the logistical networks of House Cardinal or the trade routes of House Mallard, they sustain their forces almost entirely through plunder and foraging.
- **Fierce Independence:** They are notoriously difficult to control. Gull captains frequently ignore direct orders from central Eyrie commanders if a more lucrative raiding opportunity presents itself.
- **Rivalry:** They have a bloody, ongoing feud with the Riverfolk Company. House Gull views the Company's trading posts as prime targets for raiding, and the two factions frequently clash on the open water.
- **Resources:** They hold significant hoards of stolen Marquisate wealth, possess the most capable deep-water vessels in the Woodland, and boast a cadre of fearless, battle-hardened shock troops.
