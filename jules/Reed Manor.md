### Description & Stratified History
Reed Manor was once the crown jewel of Murkwater's independent mouse aristocracy. It was an elegant, multi-tiered wooden manor boasting intricate carved archways and open verandas that overlooked the misty swamp. The manor was intelligently built upon the solid, ancient stone foundations of a long-lost civilization to prevent it from sinking into the muck.

Since the Marquise de Cat occupation, the delicate architecture has been brutalized. The open verandas are now enclosed with thick iron plating and reinforced timber, turning the manor into a formidable, albeit leaning, fortress. The original opulence has been overwritten by industrial pragmatism and military occupation. However, glimpses of its former glory remain in the form of a forgotten basement treasury and occasionally visible ornate carvings beneath the rusted iron panels.

### At First Sight
As you approach Reed Manor, the heavy, suffocating heat of the swamp seems to condense around the structure, trapped by the massive, rusted iron plates that now barricade the once-elegant wooden archways. The building leans slightly, its ancient stone foundations struggling under the immense added weight of military fortifications and munitions.

Inside, the atmosphere is oppressively hot and thick with the scent of cheap cigars, oiled metal, and wet feline fur. Dim, flickering lamplight casts long, menacing shadows across the hastily reinforced walls. Heavy feline guards, their armor clinking, are stationed at every choke point, projecting an aura of condensed, unyielding military authority that leaves no room for dissent. Maps of the swamp, bristling with dagger-pins, cover whatever original wallpaper remains.

### Distinct Areas
- **The War Room (Former Grand Parlor):** A spacious room on the ground floor where the elegant furniture has been pushed aside or broken down to make way for a massive tactical map table.
  - *Notable Features:* A large, swamp-stained map of Murkwater peppered with daggers; a heavy, iron-bound lockbox containing operational funds; overturned velvet chairs used as makeshift barricades near the boarded-up windows.
- **The Commandant's Office (Former Master Bedroom):** Chevalier Ardent's personal quarters on the second floor. It retains a shred of the manor's original luxury, though heavily militarized.
  - *Notable Features:* A large, sturdy desk cluttered with requisition orders and troop manifests; an ornate but locked wooden wardrobe holding personal arms and armor; a hidden switch behind a defaced portrait of a mouse lord.
- **The Munitions Hold (Former Wine Cellar):** The cool, damp basement of the manor, repurposed to store weapons and black powder.
  - *Notable Features:* Racks of newly forged swords and halberds; several volatile barrels of black powder; a crumbling section of ancient stone foundation that conceals a forgotten passage.

### Current Occupants & Hazards
The manor is heavily guarded by Marquise de Cat shock troops, commanded by Chevalier Ardent. Their primary goal is to maintain absolute control over the Murkwater training operations and secure the region against any threats, especially the Keepers in Iron. Hazards include the overwhelming number of heavily armed guards, tight choke points designed to trap intruders, and the risk of a catastrophic explosion in the Munitions Hold.

### Secrets & Clues
- *The Hidden Treasury:* A crumbling wall in the Munitions Hold leads to the forgotten, sealed family treasury of Elara Reed, containing wealth that the Marquisate has yet to discover.
- *The Keepers' Threat:* Troop manifests on the Commandant's desk reveal that Chevalier Ardent is growing increasingly paranoid about the Keepers in Iron and is quietly preparing a preemptive strike, despite orders to the contrary.
- *The Defaced Portrait:* The defaced portrait in the Commandant's office conceals a hidden switch that disables the mechanical alarm system for the armory.
