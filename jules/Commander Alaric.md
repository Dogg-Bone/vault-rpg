# Alaric von Strix, The Commander

**Species:** Owl
**Faction:** Eyrie Dynasties (House Strix)
**Role:** The Commander

## Concept
Alaric is a battle-hardened veteran who views the woodland solely as a theater of war. He is scarred, unyielding, and demands absolute discipline from his troops. He believes that the only way for the Eyrie to retake their ancestral home is through overwhelming martial force and superior tactics, completely disregarding diplomacy or cultural overtures.

## Details
- **Location:** Inside a heavily fortified, high-altitude war room, poring over tactical maps of Marquisate troop movements.
- **Fear:** That the Eyrie's political infighting and the other leaders' "soft" approaches will lead to a disastrous defeat, destroying the Dynasties forever.
- **Secret:** Alaric intentionally allowed a Marquisate raiding party to destroy a woodland village that had been sympathetic to the Woodland Alliance, calculating that the denizens would then turn to the Eyrie for protection.
- **Request for the Vagabonds:** He needs Vagabonds to assassinate a high-ranking Marquisate supply officer behind enemy lines. Compensation: High-quality military armor and weaponry, plus a writ of safe passage through all Strix-controlled territories.
- **Opinion/Info:** He views the Charismatic (Fiona Kestrel) as a dangerous populist rabble-rouser who undermines traditional military hierarchy and discipline.
