# The Rook Family

The Rook Family represents one of the oldest and most aristocratic pillars of the Corvid Conspiracy. Modeling themselves after an archaic, deeply traditional mafia family, they operate with a strict code of silence and absolute loyalty. While they are predominantly Rooks—distinguished by their bare, pale faces and sophisticated attire—they employ a vast network of mice, foxes, and rabbits as their eyes, ears, and enforcers. They do not soil their hands with petty street crimes; instead, they deal in the currency of power, secrets, and high-level manipulation.

## History
The Rooks claim their lineage predates the current incarnation of the Corvid Conspiracy, originally serving as spymasters and fixers during the height of the early Eyrie Dynasties. When the royal courts fractured, the Rooks realized that true power did not lie in sitting on a throne, but in controlling who sat upon it. They went underground, forming a syndicate bound by blood and oath. Over generations, they have perfected the art of the "invisible hand," ensuring their influence is felt in every major political shift in the Woodland without ever leaving a feather behind.

## Current Operations
The Rook Family specializes in manipulating the Woodland's elite, particularly the deeply political and ambitious factions like the Eyrie Dynasties. Their operations favor insidious, subtle, and long-term tactics:
* **Blackmail & Information Brokerage:** They maintain extensive dossiers on prominent figures, trading secrets to ensure favorable policies or to ruin political rivals.
* **Forgery:** Their master calligraphers and counterfeiters produce flawless forged documents, from land deeds and trade agreements to royal decrees and military orders.
* **Infiltration:** They place operatives—often non-corvids with clean backgrounds—into the courts of House Strix or House Cardinal, subtly steering decisions to benefit the Conspiracy.
* **Inciting Paranoia:** They frequently plant false evidence to ignite internal feuds among the Woodland's ruling class, keeping the elites too busy fighting each other to notice the Conspiracy's moves.

## Hierarchy & Structure
The Family is structured rigidly, resembling an older, formal syndicate:
* **The Patriarch/Matriarch:** The absolute head of the family. Their true identity is known only to a select few.
* **The Consiglieri (The Whisperers):** Advisors and master strategists who plan the long-term operations.
* **Captains (The Shadows):** Mid-level bosses who oversee specific regions or operations.
* **Operatives (The Claws):** The boots on the ground, often made up of recruited local denizens (mice, rabbits, foxes) who carry out the day-to-day tasks.

## Base of Operations
**The Whispering Woods (Oakhaven Clearing):** The Rook Family's ancestral seat is hidden within Oakhaven, a clearing known for its dense, ancient trees and towering canopy. They operate out of the "Blackwood Spire," a hollowed-out ancient oak that serves as an exclusive, invitation-only club for the Woodland elite. The true business of the family is conducted in the deep, soundproofed root-cellars beneath the club.

## Notable NPCs

### The Matriarch: Eleanor "The Widow" Sterling (Operative Code Name: *The Crown*)
A frail, ancient Rook draped in mourning veils who has ruled the family for decades. She speaks softly, but her word dictates the rise and fall of woodland nobles.
* **Location:** The deepest, most opulent private parlor beneath the Blackwood Spire.
* **Fear:** That the younger generation of the Conspiracy, favoring explosives and chaos, will destroy the delicate web of control she has spent her life weaving.
* **Secret:** She is the rightful, but unacknowledged, heir to a defunct Eyrie Dynasty, giving her a legitimate claim to the Woodland throne—a claim she uses merely as leverage.
* **Request for the Vagabonds:** She requests the Vagabonds locate a specific, ancient family heirloom currently held by the Marquise. *Reward:* A permanent, powerful favor from the Rook Family, functioning as an absolute get-out-of-jail-free card with woodland nobility.
* **Opinion/Info:** "The Magpies are crude, noisy beasts. They hoard coin like common bandits, but they lack the vision to see what coin is actually meant to buy: obedience."

### "Uncle" Barnaby (Operative Code Name: *The Quill*)
An elderly, impeccably dressed Rook who operates an antique bookstore and scriptorium in a major Eyrie-controlled clearing.
* **Location:** The dusty back room of "Barnaby's Rare Tomes."
* **Fear:** That one of his many forged decrees will be traced back to his distinct calligraphy style.
* **Secret:** He is secretly funding a minor rebellion in a neighboring clearing just to test the response time of a new Eyrie Commander.
* **Request for the Vagabonds:** He needs the Vagabonds to discreetly swap a sealed ledger in an Eyrie courier's bag with a forged copy. *Reward:* A genuine, highly valuable map of an ancient ruin, plus a favor from the Family.
* **Opinion/Info:** "That Cardinal architect they brought in? He's not as pious as he seems. I hear he has a gambling debt with the Magpies that would make a Marquise recruiter blush."

### Miss Clara (Operative Code Name: *The Silk*)
A charming and seemingly innocent mouse who works as a high-end tailor for the woodland nobility.
* **Location:** A lavish tailor shop frequented by Marquise officers and Eyrie nobles.
* **Fear:** That the Family will view her as expendable if she fails to extract a crucial piece of blackmail.
* **Secret:** She isn't actually loyal to the Conspiracy; she works for them because they hold her brother hostage in another clearing.
* **Request for the Vagabonds:** She asks the Vagabonds to deliver a "special" velvet cloak to an Eyrie diplomat. The cloak contains a hidden pocket with a forged letter designed to frame the diplomat. *Reward:* High-quality, custom-tailored stealth gear (grants +1 to stealth rolls) and a large sum of money.
* **Opinion/Info:** "If you're looking for the Patriarch, don't look for a bird. Look for the one who never speaks at the meetings, but whom everyone watches before making a decision."
