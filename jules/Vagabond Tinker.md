## Details

**Location:** Huddled over a workbench in a cluttered shed, or scrounging through a battlefield for salvageable parts.
**Fear:** That they will one day encounter a machine or a puzzle they completely lack the skill to comprehend or fix.
**Secret:** They have been quietly sabotaging the local sawmill's main gears because they despise the foreman's shoddy engineering practices.
**Request for the Vagabonds:** "The Marquise forces abandoned a broken mechanical loom near the river. If you can retrieve the main cogwheel for me before they return to scrap it, I'll repair and upgrade your weapons for free."
