# Lord of Hundreds Brainstorming

Here are a few ideas for who the Lord of Hundreds NPC could be:

## Idea 1: The Child Prophet
The current Lord of Hundreds is surprisingly young, a child who inherited the mantle after their father was assassinated. They are surrounded by fiercely protective guards and manipulative viziers. Their youth makes them unpredictable; one day they order a massacre because they had a bad dream, the next they demand the entire horde build a massive toy out of scavenged Marquise lumber. They are terrifying because they do not fully understand the consequences of their power.

## Idea 2: The Reluctant Heir
The Lord of Hundreds never wanted the title. They are a scholarly, quiet mouse who was forced into the role by tradition and the aggressive expectations of the horde. They hate the violence and the destruction, but they know if they show weakness, their own lieutenants (like Iron or Grudge) will tear them apart. They are desperately trying to find a way to steer the Hundreds toward a more peaceful, agrarian lifestyle without getting assassinated in their sleep.

## Idea 3: The Zealot
This Lord of Hundreds believes they are divinely chosen by the woodland itself to cleanse it of the "unnatural" factions (the birds and cats). They are highly charismatic, delivering fiery sermons from atop piles of burning rubble. They don't just want conquest; they want a holy war. They are unshakeable in their belief, making them completely immune to logic, bribery, or intimidation.

## Idea 4: The Corpse King
The actual Lord of Hundreds died months ago. However, the inner circle of lieutenants, fearing the horde would fracture into a bloody civil war without a leader, decided to prop up his body. They issue orders from behind a curtain or use a carefully constructed puppet system during public appearances. The "Lord" is just a figurehead, and the real power struggle is happening in the shadows among the lieutenants who know the secret.
