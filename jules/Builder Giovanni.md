# Giovanni di Cardinal, The Builder

**Species:** Cardinal
**Faction:** Eyrie Dynasties (House Cardinal)
**Role:** The Builder

## Concept
Giovanni is a visionary architect who sees the reclamation of the Woodland not as a military conquest, but as a grand construction project. He is constantly surrounded by blueprints, measuring tools, and a flurry of apprentices. He believes that by rebuilding the ancient, majestic roosts of the Eyrie, the Dynasties will naturally command the respect and loyalty of the woodland denizens.

## Details
- **Location:** Atop the massive scaffolding surrounding a partially rebuilt ancient Eyrie Roost, shouting orders to workers below.
- **Fear:** That the Marquisate will harvest all the old-growth trees needed to complete his grand architectural visions before the Eyrie can secure them.
- **Secret:** Giovanni has been deliberately diverting resources and delaying construction on necessary military fortifications to prioritize his artistic and civilian structures, falsifying supply reports to hide this from the war council.
- **Request for the Vagabonds:** He needs Vagabonds to recover a cache of rare, pre-collapse marble from a sunken ruin before Marquisate scouts find it. Compensation: Masterwork artisan tools or a piece of exquisite, custom Cardinal craftsmanship.
- **Opinion/Info:** He thinks the Commander (Alaric von Strix) is a narrow-minded brute who is incapable of seeing the long-term vision of a culturally reborn Woodland.
