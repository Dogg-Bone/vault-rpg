### Description & Stratified History
The Elmwood Company Docks were once a modest collection of wooden piers serving a quiet fishing village before the Marquise took control of the clearing. Now, it has exploded into a sprawling, chaotic hub of commerce and barely legal activity. The Riverfolk have built outwards and downwards, establishing deep-water moorings over the muddy riverbanks and constructing tall, teetering warehouses that overshadow the older, crumbling stonework of the original docks. It is a place where Marquisate authority is constantly negotiating with Riverfolk wealth, creating a tense environment where money changes paws rapidly and everything has a price.

### At First Sight
The first thing to hit the senses is the smell: a pungent mix of muddy river water, exotic spices, and the sharp tang of Marquisate steel from patrolling guards. The air is filled with the cacophony of haggling voices, clinking coins, and the heavy thud of crates being unloaded from flat-bottomed boats.

Tall, rickety cranes made of hastily assembled timber swing dangerously overhead, moving cargo between ships and the towering warehouses. Despite the Marquisate banners fluttering in the wind, the Riverfolk Company's green and gold insignia is plastered everywhere, asserting their economic dominance over this specific corner of Elmwood Reach.

### Distinct Areas
- **The Toll Gates:** A series of heavily reinforced checkpoints connecting the docks to the main clearing, manned by both Riverfolk accountants and Marquisate guards.
  - *Notable Features:* A massive, complicated mechanical scale for weighing cargo, a wall plastered with convoluted tax regulations, and a heavy iron strongbox for immediate toll collection.
- **The Deep Moorings:** The furthest docks reaching out into the river, reserved for the largest Riverfolk trading vessels and specialized cargo.
  - *Notable Features:* The rickety airship currently tethered to a reinforced mooring post, deep murky waters hiding dropped cargo (or bodies), and large mechanical cranes used for loading heavy freight.
- **The "Overstock" Warehouses:** A labyrinthine series of storage buildings where the Riverfolk keep goods they haven't found a buyer for, or items they prefer the Marquise not inspect too closely.
  - *Notable Features:* Teetering stacks of unstable crates, hidden trapdoors leading to the river, and shadowy corners used for illicit negotiations.

### Current Occupants & Hazards
The docks are swarming with Riverfolk merchants, hired muscle, and sweating laborers moving cargo. [[Inspector Valerius]] and her small contingent of Marquisate guards patrol the area, constantly frustrated by the Riverfolk's bureaucratic evasion. [[Finnian Rapids]] oversees the operation from a central toll booth. The primary hazards are the swinging cranes, unstable cargo stacks, and the aggressive Riverfolk muscle who don't take kindly to unauthorized individuals poking around the high-value moorings.

### Secrets & Clues
- Finnian Rapids keeps a secondary, hidden ledger detailing the massive bribes he pays to certain Marquisate officers to look the other way.
- The rickety airship's mooring lines are frayed and could easily be cut, but doing so would cause the massive balloon to drift dangerously into the overstock warehouses.
- A false-bottomed crate in the warehouse contains stolen Marquisate weapons that the Riverfolk are planning to sell to the Woodland Alliance.
