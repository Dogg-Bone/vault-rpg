# Woodland Lost: Campaign Expansions and Connections

This document outlines broader suggestions for expanding the *Woodland Lost* campaign, drawing from both the Root RPG Core Book and the *Travelers & Outsiders* expansion. These ideas build upon the events of "The Sunken Tomb" and offer new clearings, faction interactions, and hooks for the Vagabonds.

## 1. Expanding the Sunken Tomb Aftermath
The events of the Sunken Tomb (Woodland Lost 1) left several loose ends that are perfect for driving the narrative forward.

### The Sibilant Snake's Rampage
The Sibilant Snake has been unleashed into the forest near Swanholde Clearing.
*   **The Lair:** The snake may have taken refuge in the abandoned cellars of a ruined mill on the outskirts of Swanholde, or within a dense, marshy thicket where the river pools.
*   **The Threat:** Instead of outright attacking the clearing, the snake is using its cunning and love of riddles to extort the locals. It might be demanding tributes of food or shiny baubles (perhaps trying to replace its lost hoard), attacking supply wagons traveling to and from Swanholde.

### The Twin Blades of the Sun and Moon
Warren the Exile looted these ancient blades from the Mouse Knight.
*   **The Consequence:** The blades are broken but clearly masterwork. Blacksmiths of House Cardinal (Eyrie Dynasties) or skilled Marquise armorers might recognize their historical significance and seek to claim them.
*   **The Hook:** The gems in the hilts (the ruby and the diamond) might be the "keys" to another ruin, or a local historian (perhaps an Eyrie scholar of House Finch) offers a hefty sum to study them.

## 2. Introducing Factions from Travelers & Outsiders

### The Riverfolk Company
With Swanholde Clearing presumably near water (given the name and the flooded ruins nearby), the Riverfolk Company is a natural fit.
*   **The Hook:** A Riverfolk trade barge, captained by a pragmatic otter skipper (e.g., Skipper Finnian), arrives in Swanholde. They offer to sell the Vagabonds "snake repellent" or specialized gear to handle the Sibilant Snake.
*   **The Catch:** The Riverfolk don't produce goods, they trade them. The "repellent" might just be a rare, pungent herb they traded for from the Lizard Cult. The Skipper's true motive is securing the rights to salvage the flooded Sunken Tomb now that the monsters are mostly cleared out.

### The Corvid Conspiracy
The Corvid Conspiracy thrives in the shadows of established powers like the Marquise de Cat (who chased the party initially).
*   **The Syndicate:** Agents of the Magpie Syndicate (one of the two major Corvid families) approach the Vagabonds. The Sibilant Snake's disruption of Marquise supply lines is highly beneficial to them.
*   **The Plot:** The Corvids offer to pay the Vagabonds to *protect* the snake or redirect its attacks toward Marquise patrols rather than slaying it. Their operative, a mouse using the cover name "Miller" (Codename: Blackbeak), orchestrates this from a local tavern.

## 3. Core Faction Entanglements

### The Marquise de Cat
The party is already on the run from Marquise soldiers.
*   **The Pursuers:** The Marquise forces haven't given up. A specialized tracker unit is investigating the ruined birch stump where the party exited the tomb.
*   **The Complication:** The Marquise might discover the Sibilant Snake and attempt to capture it for use as a biological weapon or guard beast, putting them in direct conflict with the party's efforts to deal with it.

### The Eyrie Dynasties
If the Vagabonds venture further from Swanholde, they may encounter the Eyrie.
*   **The Encounter:** A patrol from House Kestrel (known for numbers) or House Condor (known for despotic repurposing) is looking to establish a new roost near Swanholde.
*   **The Conflict:** House Condor might want to demolish the ancient ruins (like the Sunken Tomb) to use the stone for their fortresses, erasing the history the Vagabonds just uncovered. Commander Wilhelm Strix could lead this detachment, offering the Vagabonds a pardon from the Marquise if they mercenary for the Eyrie.

## 4. New Clearings and Locations

### Oakhaven (New Clearing)
*   **Concept:** A major trading hub located downriver from Swanholde, currently under tense, contested control between the Riverfolk Company (economic control) and the Marquise de Cat (military occupation).
*   **The Problem:** The Riverfolk trade routes are being strangled by the Marquise's heavy taxation.
*   **The Hook:** The Vagabonds can smuggle goods (or the Twin Blades) into Oakhaven, getting caught in the middle of a Magpie Syndicate extortion racket aimed at the local Marquise magistrate.

### The Whispering Woods
*   **Concept:** A dense, unnerving section of the forest where the Lizard Cult has established a hidden garden.
*   **The Connection:** The Cultists worship serpentine figures and might view the Sibilant Snake as a divine messenger. They will actively oppose anyone trying to harm the snake.

## 5. Vagabond Playbook Hooks

*   **The Exile (Warren):** His "Greatest of the Age" ability makes him a legend in the making. The Marquise might put a specific bounty on him, or resistance fighters might seek him out as a symbol of hope.
*   **The Thief/Scoundrel (Robin Banks):** Robin's use of fire in the tomb might have unintended consequences. Perhaps the smoke signaled a nearby Woodland Alliance cell who now want to recruit Robin for sabotage missions.
*   **New Playbooks (T&O):** If a new player joins or someone wants a secondary character, introducing a **Pirate** (tied to the Riverfolk or House Mallard navies) or a **Chronicler** (eager to document the truth of the Mouse Knight's flooded valley) would tie perfectly into the existing lore.
