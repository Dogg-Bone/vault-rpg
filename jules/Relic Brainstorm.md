# Relic Brainstorm

## Wren Greenboot (Harrier Scourge)

### 1. The Hollow-Sounder (Smuggling Focus)
An Ancient acoustic device made of interlocking brass rings, delicate tuning forks, and a stethoscope-like earpiece. When tapped against a wall or floor, the intricate internal gears and resonating chambers catch and amplify the acoustic feedback. It allows the user to literally "hear" the structural blueprint of a building, easily identifying hidden rooms, hollow walls, and secret smuggler tunnels hidden behind solid stone or earth.

### 2. The Pneumatic Whisper-Bow (Revenge Focus)
A compact, wrist-mounted projectile launcher forged from a dark, lightweight alloy unknown to modern crafters. Instead of a bowstring, it utilizes a complex system of highly pressurized pneumatic cylinders that can be pumped up beforehand. When fired, it launches quarrels with terrifying force but absolutely zero sound. It is the ultimate assassin's tool, allowing Wren to strike down targets on his List from the shadows without ever alerting their guards.

### 3. The Labyrinth Orrery (Story-Advancing)
A staggering piece of Ancient clockwork consisting of spinning dials, gears, and cryptographic cylinders. Wren discovers that Perry the Platypus didn't just kidnap his family; he locked them away in a supposedly impenetrable Ancient vault to use as leverage against Vulcan's syndicate. The Orrery functions as an infallible decryption engine and lock-analyzer. By finding it, Wren gains the only tool capable of decoding Perry's syndicate communications to locate the vault, and the only key complex enough to open it once he gets there.

## Savage Wyrmbourne (Hunter Heretic)

### 1. The Leviathan's Snare (Trapping/Hunting Focus)
A seemingly small, heavy puzzle-box of gears and spooling mechanisms that, when triggered, violently expands into a massive, self-resetting mechanical trap. Using razor-thin, unbreakable wires and incredibly high-tension springs, it can instantly entangle and ground even the largest of woodland monsters or a full squad of soldiers. Its mechanical design is so perfect that the more the prey struggles, the tighter and more complex the bindings become.

### 2. The Wyrm's Breath Bellows (Heretical Beliefs Focus)
A heavy bronze apparatus worn on the back, connected to a nozzle by thick, articulated metal tubing. By combining rare mineral dusts with a powerful, hand-cranked pneumatic compressor, it can expel a terrifying, roaring torrent of alchemical fire and thick black smoke. Savage can use this awe-inspiring relic to mechanically simulate a "miracle" of the Great Wyrm, putting the fear of his god into unbelievers and swaying persuadable crowds to his heretical cause.

### 3. The Obsidian Scale (Story-Advancing)
An Ancient artifact carved from an unknown, indestructible black metal, shaped unmistakably like a massive reptilian scale. It is deeply magnetic and hums with a low, vibrating frequency, essentially acting as a compass that points deep underground. It leads towards the true "Great Wyrm"—which Savage may discover is not a biological god, but rather a colossal, slumbering Ancient subterranean boring-engine or lost underground city. Revealing this truth would give Savage the ultimate proof needed to challenge the mainstream Lizard Cult leaders and free his followers from oppressive dogma.
