# Undertow Shipping

**Led by:** Skipper Silas Reed (Beaver)
**Primary Niches:** Bulk cargo shipping, raw materials (lumber, ore), river tolls, heavy mercenary contracts
**Headquarters Base:** Often operates out of massive, slow-moving armored barges that link together to form floating fortresses.

## Description
Undertow Shipping is one of the largest and most militarized Riverfolk companies operating in the Woodland. Unlike companies that deal in fine goods or subtle manipulation, Undertow Shipping deals in bulk and brute force. They control massive, heavy-bottomed barges capable of hauling massive quantities of raw materials—timber from [[Marquise de Cat|Marquise]] clear-cuts, iron ore from deep mines, and stone for [[Eyrie Dynasties|Eyrie]] roosts. They are the undeniable masters of heavy logistics.

However, they are perhaps best known for their aggressive monopolization of key river checkpoints. Undertow Shipping frequently anchors their massive barges across narrow straits or critical junctions, effectively blockading the river and demanding exorbitant "protection tolls" from anyone wishing to pass. To enforce these tolls and protect their massive shipments, Undertow Shipping employs legions of heavy mercenaries—often heavily armored turtles, badgers, and bears—armed with polearms and heavy crossbows.

## The Skipper: Silas Reed
Silas Reed is a massive, heavily scarred beaver who looks more like a warlord than a merchant. He wears practical, thick leather armor reinforced with plates of rusted river-iron. His tail is uniquely broad, heavily calloused, and bears the distinct brand of a Marquise prison camp from his youth—a testament to his survival and a fueling source for his current ruthless ambition.

He speaks in a deep, rumbling baritone that commands immediate attention. Silas does not believe in subtlety or flowery negotiations. He slams his heavy fists on tables to emphasize points and views every interaction as a transaction of power. He surrounds himself with heavy turtle mercenaries and operates from the deck of the *Iron Draft*, viewing the woodland clearings not as communities, but as resource nodes waiting to be exploited by his fleet.

- **Location:** Aboard his massive flagship barge, the *Iron Draft*, usually anchored at strategic river junctions.
- **Drive:** To control the flow of the Woodland's resources and amass unassailable wealth and power.
- **Fear:** That a coalition of factions will realize they outnumber him and sink his fleet to break his monopoly.
- **Secret:** His flagship, the *Iron Draft*, has a severe structural flaw in its hull that he hides desperately; a well-placed explosive could sink his entire floating fortress.
- **Request:** He needs the [[Vagabonds]] to quietly eliminate a charismatic [[Woodland Alliance]] leader who is successfully organizing a boycott of Undertow goods in a major clearing downriver.
- **Opinion/Info:** He warns that [[House Mallard]] of the Eyrie Dynasties is secretly building a fleet of shallow-draft war galleys in the northern lakes, preparing for a major river offensive.
- **Current Wares:** Bulk timber, raw iron ore, heavy mercenary contracts, safe (but expensive) passage past blockades.
- **Can Acquire:** Siege engines, massive quantities of black powder, forged architectural plans for major fortresses.

## Methods
Undertow Shipping operates with the subtlety of a falling tree. They believe that power is derived from control over infrastructure and the sheer mass of their presence.
- **The Blockade:** Their signature move. They link their armored barges together across a river, creating a physical and economic wall. Pay the toll, or turn back.
- **The Heavy Contract:** They rent out their heavily armored mercenary bands to the highest bidder, often turning the tide of sieges or defending critical resource nodes for the Marquise or Eyrie.
- **Resource Squeezing:** If a clearing relies heavily on imported raw materials (like coal for forges or grain for winter), Undertow Shipping will buy up the supply upriver, artificially inflate the price, and refuse to ship it until their demands are met.

## Relations
- **Marquise de Cat:** A tense but necessary alliance. The Marquise needs Undertow Shipping to move the massive amounts of lumber and ore they produce. The Undertow knows this and charges premium rates, often angering local commanders.
- **Eyrie Dynasties:** The Eyrie despises paying tolls to "commoners," but frequently hires Undertow mercenaries when their own forces are stretched thin by internal political squabbles. House Mallard, in particular, views them as a significant naval rival.
- **Woodland Alliance:** The Alliance views Undertow Shipping as just another oppressive force. Undertow Shipping's heavy tolls often starve out poorer clearings, making them ripe grounds for Alliance sympathy. The Undertow ruthlessly crushes any Alliance sabotage attempts on their barges.
- **The Corvid Conspiracy:** The Undertow is difficult for the Corvids to blackmail directly, as they operate in the open and rely on brute strength. However, the Corvids often incite unrest in clearings blockaded by Undertow Shipping to create chaos.

## Custom Move: The Undertow Squeeze
When you **attempt to negotiate a lower toll or better shipping rate with an Undertow representative**, roll with **Charm**.
- **On a 10+**, they respect your audacity. They offer a significant discount, but you must do them a public favor (like roughing up a rival merchant or clearing a minor river hazard) before you leave.
- **On a 7-9**, they refuse to budge on the price, but they will offer you a piece of valuable, tactical information regarding the river ahead or a faction's troop movements as a consolation.
- **On a 6-**, the representative is insulted by your attempt. The toll is doubled, and a squad of heavy mercenaries 'escorts' you back to your vessel to ensure you pay or leave immediately.
