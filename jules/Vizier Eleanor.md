# Vizier Eleanor Finch

**Species:** Finch
**Faction:** Eyrie Dynasties
**Role:** Vizier of Archives and Lore

## Concept
Eleanor is a tiny, unassuming bird often overlooked in the grand halls of the Eyrie. She is perpetually covered in dust and ink, managing the vast historical records and treaties of the Dynasties. Despite her frail appearance, she wields immense power because she controls the narrative of the Eyrie's past and holds the secrets of every major political figure.

## Details
- **Personality Word:** Calculating
- **Prime Drive:** To preserve the knowledge and historical continuity of the Eyrie Dynasties, prioritizing the survival of their history over the survival of any single leader.
- **Will Never Do:** She will never destroy a historical document, even if its contents directly contradict the current ruler's decrees or threaten to cause a civil war.
- **Location:** Deep within the labyrinthine, restricted archives of the Eyrie, surrounded by towering stacks of scrolls and ancient tomes.
- **Fear:** That an illiterate, despotic leader will burn the archives to erase a political rival's legitimate claim to leadership.
- **Secret:** Eleanor possesses an ancient treaty that legally grants a significant portion of the woodland to the indigenous mouse and rabbit populations, a document she keeps hidden because it would delegitimize the Eyrie's current war of conquest.
- **Request for the Vagabonds:** She needs Vagabonds to delve into a ruin currently occupied by the Lord of Hundreds to recover a set of pre-collapse Eyrie tax ledgers. Compensation: Access to restricted historical maps revealing hidden, forgotten woodland caches of ancient treasure.
- **Opinion/Info:** She knows that Vizier Ignatius is poisoning a rival, but she chooses not to intervene because the dying vizier once advocated for burning old texts to save space.
