> [!tip] Hundreds Murkwater Encampment
> **Type:** Landmark (Hidden Camp)
> **Atmosphere & Effort State:** Twitchy & Flick
> **Shape Flow:** Chaotic mob energy is fracturing and festering

### Description & Stratified History
Hidden deep within a grove of weeping willows and bioluminescent mushrooms, this makeshift encampment is a stark contrast to the rigid military structure of the rest of Murkwater. The Lord of Hundreds have slipped into the swamp, utilizing an area that was once a serene, forgotten graveyard of the ancient civilization, where weathered stone markers poke through the muck.

The Lord of Hundreds have scavenged both the ancient stones and the swamp's detritus to build a chaotic, temporary base. The current structures are violently strapped together with rusty wire and scavenged scrap, built directly atop the ancient, moldering graves. The faction's obsession with hoarding relics and their destructive nature are on full display, turning a place of ancient rest into a festering wound of anarchy within the swamp.

### At First Sight
Approaching the encampment, the twitchy, paranoid energy is immediately apparent. The air smells strongly of rusted iron, unwashed bodies, and the earthy tang of bioluminescent fungi. The visual landscape is chaotic: mismatched armor pieces, scavenged scrap metal, and bone totems hang from the weeping willow branches, clinking together in the damp breeze.

There is no orderly layout; instead, a mob of ragged mice dart around the camp in quick, flicking movements, always looking over their shoulders. At the center of the camp, guarded by heavily armed, nervous sentries, sits a massive pile of looted relics and scrap. The chaotic mob energy here feels like it could fracture into violence at any moment, either directed outward at the Marquisate or inward against themselves.

### Distinct Areas
- **The Hoard (Center of Camp):** A massive, disorganized pile of scavenged scrap metal, ancient stone tablets, and rusted relics, serving as both a treasury and a crude altar.
  - *Notable Features:* A large, surprisingly intact, but rusted, pre-Dynastic gear mechanism; heavily armed, twitchy guards; a crude throne made of bound scrap metal.
- **The Envoy's Tent:** A slightly larger, patchwork tent made from stolen Marquisate banners and cured hides, where Scrapper Rustpaw plots his diplomatic overtures.
  - *Notable Features:* A map of the region with crude, charcoal markings; several forged letters bearing the Warlord's seal; a hidden lockbox buried in the mud beneath a rug.
- **The Scavenger's Pits:** A series of shallow, muddy excavations around the ancient grave markers where the mob is actively trying to dig up more scrap or relics.
  - *Notable Features:* Discarded, broken shovels and pickaxes; a recently unearthed, sealed stone sarcophagus; a precarious, rigged alarm system made of tin cans and wire.

### Current Occupants & Hazards
The camp is occupied by a contingent of the Lord of Hundreds, mostly consisting of ragged, fanatic mice led by the envoy, Scrapper Rustpaw. They are desperate, paranoid, and highly aggressive toward outsiders. Hazards include the twitchy, unpredictable nature of the mob, hidden pit traps filled with rusted spikes around the perimeter, and the risk of triggering a crude, explosive booby trap near the Hoard.

### Secrets & Clues
- *The Rogue Envoy:* Forged letters in the Envoy's Tent reveal that Scrapper Rustpaw is acting completely independently; the Warlord has not sanctioned this alliance, and discovering this would fracture the camp's loyalty.
- *The Sarcophagus Trap:* The recently unearthed sarcophagus in the Scavenger's Pits is not filled with treasure, but is a rigged trap containing a volatile alchemical substance from the ancient era.
- *The Hidden Arsenal:* A partially buried, hollowed-out log near the perimeter contains a cache of stolen Marquisate black powder, meant to be used if the diplomatic overtures fail.

### Loot & Resources
- *Scavenged Scrap Armor:* A mismatched, noisy set of armor made from pots, pans, and rusted plates. (1-Armor, 3-Wear, Clumsy)
- *Rusted Pre-Dynastic Gear:* A strange, heavy mechanical part from the Hoard. (Value: 2, +valuable, +heavy)
- *Forged Diplomatic Seal:* A crude but convincing replica of the Warlord's seal found in the Envoy's Tent. (Value: 1, +valuable to specific political rivals)
