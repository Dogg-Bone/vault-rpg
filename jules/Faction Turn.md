# Faction Turn Rules

> [!tip] Overview
> This document synthesizes the Faction Turn rules from the Root RPG Core Book and Travelers & Outsiders expansion.

## Faction Roll

Here is the simpler system from the core book, reprinted with all boons and moves needed to incorporate all the new factions.

> [!tip] Move: Faction Roll
> When time passes, roll for each non-denizen faction. Start with the faction that controls the most clearings, then work down the list:
> - +1 if the faction operated unfettered by PC actions since the last roll
> - -1 if the faction has recently suffered a blow from the PC vagabonds
> - -1 if the faction controls the most clearings (and is thus a juicy target)
>
> Add +1 if the faction in question controls the following:
> - Marquisate: 5+ total clearings, workshops, sawmills, or recruiters
> - Eyrie Dynasties: 2+ Roosts or 4+ clearings
> - Woodland Alliance: 3+ sympathy or at least one base
> - Lizard Cult: 2+ gardens
> - Riverfolk Company: 3+ trading posts
> - Grand Duchy: 2+ clearings and at least one market and one citadel
> - Corvid Conspiracy: plots in 3+ clearings
>
> On a hit, the faction scores a victory; choose one minor boon. On a 10+, choose another minor boon—in the same clearing or another—or a major boon instead. On a miss, the faction suffers a defeat—it loses control of a clearing (returning it to the control of its own denizens); a fortification or structure (Roost, base, Marquisate building, garden, citadel, market, trading post, or other at GM’s discretion); or a valuable resource.

### MINOR BOONS

- **Attack:** Take control of a clearing adjacent to one already controlled. If that clearing has fortifications, destroy those instead. If that clearing has no fortifications but has a Roost, a Woodland Alliance base, any Marquisate structures, or a Grand Duchy citadel or market, destroy all of those instead.
- **Fortify:** Fortify a controlled clearing with defensive structures and more troops. The next time an enemy faction would take control of a fortified clearing, they only remove the fortifications instead.
- **Obtain:** Gain a valuable resource from the forests, ruins, or a clearing. On the next faction roll, the faction takes +2 if it retains control of the resource.
- **Establish cells:** Add sympathy to any two clearings. [Woodland Alliance]
- **Stamp cells:** Remove sympathy from all clearings the faction controls. [All factions except Woodland Alliance]
- **Build industry:** Construct one sawmill, workshop, or recruiting post in each of two different controlled clearings. [Marquisate]
- **Begin/finish building garden:** Begin construction of a single garden in a clearing with Lizard Cult presence, or finish a garden begun on a prior faction roll. [Lizard Cult]
- **Proselytize:** Add Lizard Cult presence to any clearing. [Lizard Cult]
- **Conduct commerce:** Add Riverfolk Company presence to any clearing. [Riverfolk Company]
- **Build trading post:** Construct one trading post in a clearing with Riverfolk Company presence. [Riverfolk Company]
- **Connect tunnel:** Build a tunnel from the Burrow to any clearing, making it "adjacent" to the Burrow for the Duchy. [Underground Duchy]
- **Build market or citadel:** Build a market or a citadel in a controlled clearing. [Underground Duchy]
- **Expand network:** Add Corvid Conspiracy presence to up to two clearings adjacent to its presence. [Corvid Conspiracy]
- **Enact plot:** Launch a plot in a clearing with Corvid Conspiracy presence. (A plot here means the Corvid Conspiracy is undertaking illicit action to gain power—exactly what they are doing is up to you.) [Corvid Conspiracy]
- **Stamp plot:** Remove all plots from all clearings the faction controls. [All factions except Corvid Conspiracy]

### MAJOR BOONS

- **Revolt:** A revolt occurs in a clearing with sympathy; the Woodland Alliance gains a base there, taking control and removing all other faction structures. [Woodland Alliance]
- **Build Roost:** Add a Roost to a clearing the Eyrie controls without a Roost. [Eyrie]
- **Capture:** Seize an important figure in an enemy faction; that faction takes -1 on all faction rolls while that figure is held captive.
- **Rapidly build garden:** Begin and finish construction of a single garden in a clearing with Lizard Cult presence. Take control of that clearing. [Lizard Cult]
- **Trade war:** Take control of a clearing with a trade post and add a trade post to an adjacent clearing. [Riverfolk Company]
- **Culminate plot:** Bring a plot to fruition, giving the Conspiracy control over the clearing and either destroying or subverting all buildings and warriors in the clearing (as fits the fiction). The plot is not removed—it remains active (think ongoing protection racket, ongoing extortion, etc.). If the Conspiracy loses control, the plot must be stamped out, lest it culminate in a new form. [Corvid Conspiracy]

## The Faction Phase

The faction phase is a more complex and intricate way to handle time passing, giving each faction a much more robust and unique system that dictates its own actions, plots, and events.

### Faction Order Table

In general, follow the faction phase in the order the factions are presented here.

1. Marquisate
2. Eyrie Dynasties
3. Woodland Alliance
4. Lizard Cult
5. Riverfolk Company
6. Grand Duchy
7. Corvid Conspiracy
8. Denizens

## Time Passes

All of the faction-specific rules presented here kick into action during the faction phase, when time passes—that is to say, when you advance the timeline of the Woodland without portraying every individual moment. Time can pass when the vagabonds travel between distant clearings or when they rest and recover, staying in a single clearing for a couple weeks.

## Rumors

> [!tip] Move: Rumors
> When you pick up rumors while time passes, roll with your Reputation with a faction of your choice. On a hit, you pick up rumors pointing at one important event or undertaking of that faction while time passed. On a 10+, you may ask a follow-up question. On a miss, you hear a couple of rumors, some of which might be true, some of which might be false.

## The Marquisate

The Marquisate represents an invading foreign empire, industrialized and militaristic, aiming to extract resources and conquer.

### Portraying the Marquisate
The Marquisate's two drives are:
- To transform the Woodland into an industrialized engine of resources
- To establish order and stability at the end of a sword

### Marquisate Actions
To represent this, the Marquisate works on projects. An individual project consists of a clear aim, an obstacle, and a countdown clock of segments.

During the faction phase, the Marquisate advances a number of project segments based on the clearings they control:
- 1-3 Clearings Controlled: 1 segment
- 4-6 Clearings Controlled: 2 segments
- 7+ Clearings Controlled: 3 segments

> [!tip] Move: Marquisate Projects
> When the Marquisate advances its projects during a faction phase, allocate segments to any ongoing clocks. For each project that completes a clock, the Marquisate achieves the project's aim.

## The Eyrie Dynasties

The Eyrie Dynasties represent the old powers and aristocracies of the Woodland, attempting to reclaim their former glory.

### Portraying the Eyrie Dynasties
The Eyrie Dynasties' two drives are:
- To reclaim their rightful place as the rulers of the Woodland
- To extract vengeance from those who abandoned or betrayed them

### Eyrie Actions
The Eyrie Dynasties rely on bureaucracy and their decree. During the faction phase, they spend dice on actions based on their current leadership and controlled Roosts.

> [!tip] Move: Eyrie Major Crisis
> When the Eyrie faces a random major crisis, roll to determine the source of the breakdown. A crisis stalls all Eyrie progress until it is resolved.

## The Woodland Alliance

The Woodland Alliance is a nascent rebellion, striking out against the oppressive powers that dominate the clearings.

### Portraying the Woodland Alliance
The Woodland Alliance's two drives are:
- To tear down the tyrannical structures of the Woodland
- To empower the denizens to rule themselves

### Woodland Alliance Actions

**Military**
When time passes, for each base the Woodland Alliance has in the Woodland, it attacks a clearing controlled by another non-denizen faction connected by paths to a Woodland Alliance–controlled clearing.

**Revolt**
> [!tip] Move: Woodland Alliance Revolt
> Roll for that clearing to see if a revolt occurs. If successful, the Woodland Alliance destroys all enemy faction presence, taking control and building a base.

**Sympathy**
> [!tip] Move: Woodland Alliance Sympathy
> Roll for the Woodland Alliance to see if a clearing is preparing for revolt or rising in sympathy. Roll with the following:
> - +1 if the clearing is adjacent to a clearing with a Woodland Alliance base
> - -2 if the Marquisate keep, Eyrie Roost, or equivalent stronghold of the controlling faction is present
> - +1 if the denizens are in control of that clearing

## The Lizard Cult

The Lizard Cult is a religious movement and organization, drawn in origin from a far-off theocratic power.

### Portraying the Lizard Cult
The Lizard Cult's two drives are:
- To convert all of the Woodland to the true word of the Great Wyrm
- To punish the heretics and nonbelievers who stand in the way

### Lizard Cult Actions

**Crusade**
> [!tip] Move: Lizard Cult Crusade
> Roll to see if the Lizard Cult builds a new garden or successfully launches a crusade. The Cult gains significant momentum when its members fall in battle, creating Outcast martyrs.

**Inquisition**
> [!tip] Move: Lizard Cult Proselytize
> When the Lizard Cult converts denizens, they expand their presence and sympathy within clearings, establishing covert networks.

## The Riverfolk Company

The Riverfolk Company is an independent mercantile faction that sees the War entirely as an economic opportunity.

### Portraying the Riverfolk Company
The Riverfolk Company's two drives are:
- To amass wealth and economic power
- To corner every market and eliminate all competition

### Riverfolk Company Actions

**Earning Funds**
On its turn within the faction phase, the Riverfolk Company earns a baseline amount of funds based on how it is spread throughout the Woodland:
- 1 fund for each functional trading post in the Woodland
- 1 fund for every two clearings with a non-trading post Riverfolk Company trade contract
- 1 fund for each new mercenary contract sold to a non-denizen faction

**Spending Funds**
The Company uses its funds to expand influence, build trading posts, and hire mercenaries.

**Eviction**
> [!tip] Move: Eviction
> Roll for that faction to see if it ousts the Company from one of its clearings. Roll with the following:
> - -1 if the faction owes any debt to the Company
> - -1 if the faction owes 3-debt or more to the Company
> - +1 if the faction has suffered any ill effects at the hands of the Company or thanks to a Company-sold mercenary contract

## The Grand Duchy

The Grand Duchy is a fiercely feudal underground nation, well-funded and secure in its position.

### Portraying the Grand Duchy
The Grand Duchy's two drives are:
- To bring the Woodland to heel under the Duchy's rule and leadership
- To eliminate chaos, strife, and disorder

### Duchy Actions
The Duchy takes actions to further its invasion of the Woodland, but it also tries to rally the support of its top leaders in parliament.

> [!tip] Move: Duchy Invasion
> Roll to see if the Duchy manages to successfully invade a given clearing through their tunnel network.

**Ministers**
> [!tip] Move: Duchy Ministers
> When the Duchy assesses the state of its ministers with regard to the War, roll with the following:
> - +1 if the Duchy took control of at least two new clearings since the last faction phase
> - +1 if the Duchy has at least five clearings under its control
> - +1 if the Duchy has at least two citadels and two markets
> - -1 if the Duchy lost a clearing since the last faction phase
> - -1 if the Duchy suffered a severe blow from vagabonds since the last faction phase
> - -1 if another faction controls more clearings than the Duchy

## The Corvid Conspiracy

The Corvid Conspiracy is an anarchic, criminal syndicate embedded within the population of the Woodland.

### Portraying the Corvid Conspiracy
The Corvid Conspiracy's two drives are:
- To tear down the established order and build a new world
- To exert influence through fear, manipulation, and extortion

### Corvid Conspiracy Actions
The Corvid Conspiracy operates through deep, long-term plots rather than direct military confrontation.

**Plots**
During the faction phase, the Conspiracy expands presence and advances their secret plots (Extortion, Raid, Snare, Bomb).

## The Denizens

The denizens are the ordinary inhabitants of the Woodland—the mice, rabbits, and foxes trying to survive the War.

### Portraying the Denizens
The Denizens' drive is:
- To live peacefully and comfortably

### Denizen Uprisings
A denizen uprising can never happen in a well-fortified and controlled clearing. A Keep, a Roost, a Citadel, or a well-armed Woodland Alliance Base can block a denizen uprising. Don't even roll for those clearings.

> [!tip] Move: Denizen Uprisings
> During the faction phase, roll for each non-denizen faction-controlled clearing, with the following modifiers:
> - +1 if the clearing has undergone intensely oppressive, chaotic, or destructive circumstances
> - +1 if the clearing has changed control since the last faction phase
>
> On a 12+, the denizens of the clearing have an uprising and overthrow the controlling faction, restoring control to themselves…at least for now.
