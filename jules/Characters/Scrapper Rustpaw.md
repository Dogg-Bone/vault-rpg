LORD OF HUNDREDS
**Scrapper Rustpaw**
*Envoy & Instigator*
Drive: to secure a powerful alliance and drown the Eyrie in a tidal wave of mob violence
*"A wiry, twitchy mouse whose fur is matted with swamp mud and grease. He wears a clattering, absurdly heavy suit of piecemeal scrap armor scavenged from dozens of ruined outposts and carries a wide, unblinking, unsettling grin."*

**Equipment**
- Jagged, rusted scrap-metal spear (Close, 1-injury)
- A heavy sack filled with looted baubles and clinking relics
- Haphazard patchwork scrap armor (1-wear)

**Found at:** The heavily concealed Lord of Hundreds Encampment in the deeper swamps.

**Found doing:** Pacing nervously amidst piles of scavenged junk, rehearsing diplomatic overtures to the Marquisate while compulsively sharpening his spear on a rock.

**Moves**
- Offer a piece of absurdly valuable looted junk as a casual bribe
- Shift nervously and glance over his shoulder constantly
- Whip his followers into a sudden, chaotic frenzy

**Movement Anchor:** Light, Sudden, Indirect, Free energy

**Center:** Hips

**Key Facts**
1. He is acting somewhat independently, hoping to secure this alliance before the Warlord discovers his gambit.
2. He views the Marquisate not as allies, but as a temporary battering ram to smash his enemies.

**Immediate Pressure:** He must secure an agreement with the Marquisate before his own restless, chaotic mob tears the hidden encampment apart out of boredom.

**Persuasion Tactics:**
1. Appeal to mutual hatred of the Eyrie Dynasties
2. Promise the overwhelming, unstoppable numbers of the mob

**Social Status:** Lower

**Perception of Vagabonds:** *"Wandering scavengers? Good. We always need more bodies for the front line."*
