Underground Duchy
**Mozz, Underlord of Bone Gulch**
*Gambler & Smuggler*
Drive: to enrich himself and the Duchy by controlling the flow of illicit goods and secrets.
*"Mozz is a corpulent mole with tiny, squinting eyes hidden behind thick, dark spectacles. He dresses in ostentatious, heavy silks despite the desert heat, constantly dabbing his brow with a lace handkerchief. His fingers are adorned with gaudy rings, one of which doubles as a wax seal for Duchy missives."*

**Equipment**
- Ostentatious silk handkerchief and spectacles
- Heavy gold signet ring (worth 2-Value)
- Hidden, ornate hold-out pistol (1-injury, close, tricky, +1-exhaustion)

**Found at:**
- The Velvet Cavern (his subterranean gambling den below Bone Gulch)

**Found doing:**
- Counting chips, haggling over exotic smuggled goods, or holding court with indebted locals.

**Moves**
- Offer a loan with exorbitant, dangerous interest
- Call upon Duchy muscle to intimidate a rival
- Reveal a secret about someone's hidden vices or debts

**Movement Anchor:** Heavy, Sustained, Indirect, Bound energy

**Center:** Stomach

**Key Facts**
1. Mozz holds the debts of several key Marquisate guards, ensuring they turn a blind eye to his operations.
2. He has established a lucrative smuggling route that connects Bone Gulch to deeper Duchy tunnels, bypassing surface patrols entirely.

**Immediate Pressure:** The Lizard Cult is beginning to scrutinize his operations, seeing his gambling den as a distraction from the Great Wyrm's teachings.

**Persuasion Tactics:**
1. Bribery and financial leverage
2. Veiled threats utilizing Duchy assassins

**Social Status:** Upper

**Perception of Vagabonds:** *"Vagabonds are simply independent contractors with flexible morals. They are useful tools for tasks my own people find too messy, so long as they don't ask too many questions or demand too much coin."*
