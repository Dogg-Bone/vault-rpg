KEEPERS IN IRON
**Warden Kaelen**
*Expedition Commander*
Drive: to secure relics by any means necessary
*A massive, scarred badger clad in heavily dented but meticulously maintained iron plate. His face is locked in a permanent, stoic scowl, and a missing left ear speaks to past battles. He moves with a slow, undeniable momentum, like a boulder rolling downhill.*

**Equipment**
- An intricately carved, ancient iron pendant (personally significant)
- Massive iron greatsword (Close, 2-injury, +1-wear, heavy)
- Heavy iron plate armor (Wear tracks apply)

**Harm**
```statblock: inline
Injury: 4
Exhaustion: 3
Wear: 4
Morale: 4
```

**Found at:**
A heavily fortified, temporary Keeper waystation, reviewing excavation reports or inspecting the perimeter defenses.

**Found doing:**
Barking gruff, precise orders to his subordinates, or personally reprimanding a scout for sloppy work.

**Moves**
- Advance relentlessly despite taking blows
- Demand immediate surrender and compliance
- Utilize overwhelming physical force to crush obstacles

**Movement Anchor:** Heavy Weight, Sustained Time, Direct Space, Bound Flow

**Center:** His broad, plated chest

**Key Facts**
1. He secretly doubts if the relics they are unearthing are truly meant to be saved, having seen the destructive power of one firsthand, though he remains outwardly fanatical.
2. He views all non-badgers as chaotic, untrustworthy children who need a firm hand to be controlled.

**Immediate Pressure:** An excavation team in a nearby ruin has gone silent, and he cannot spare the manpower to investigate without leaving the current waystation vulnerable.

**Persuasion Tactics:**
1. Intimidation through sheer physical presence and veiled threats
2. Appeals to order, tradition, and rigid hierarchy

**Social Status:** Upper

**Perception of Vagabonds:** *"Mercenaries and looters. Useful tools if pointed in the right direction, but they must be discarded the moment they reach for what is ours."*
