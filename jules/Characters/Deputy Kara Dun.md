MARQUISE DE CAT
**Deputy Kara Dun**
*Clearing Deputy*
Drive: To execute the orders of her superiors with absolute, unflinching loyalty.
*A stark, unsmiling wolf with immaculate, perfectly tailored uniform. She stands at attention even when resting, and her gaze is piercing and judgmental. She always carries a well-maintained crossbow strapped to her back.*

**Equipment**
- Heavy Crossbow (Far, 2-injury, +reload)
- Standard issue short sword (Close, 1-injury)
- Polished badge of office
- Restraining manacles

**Found at:** The local jailhouse or patrolling the most tense, riot-prone districts of the clearing.

**Found doing:** Aggressively questioning a suspected dissident, meticulously logging confiscated contraband, or lecturing a subordinate on proper protocol.

**Moves**
- Arrest someone on a perfectly legal technicality
- Refuse any bribe or appeal to emotion
- Coordinate a precise, overwhelming guard response

**Movement Anchor:** Heavy, Sustained, Direct, Bound energy

**Center:** Straight spine

**Key Facts**
1. She views disobedience to superiors as the ultimate cardinal sin; to her, the law and the orders of her faction are indistinguishable from morality.
2. She is deputy to Chief Clancy "Pants" Morgan, and while she respects his rank, she is deeply suspicious of his recent bouts of leniency.

**Immediate Pressure:** She has been ordered to crack down on a local baker's union, but her Chief seems to be stalling the operation.

**Persuasion Tactics:**
1. Citing local laws and regulations
2. Threats of legal incarceration and force

**Social Status:** 2 - Lower (Authority figure, but not wealthy)

**Perception of Vagabonds:** *"Roving catalysts for disorder. They have no respect for authority, no chain of command, and no place in a civilized clearing."*
