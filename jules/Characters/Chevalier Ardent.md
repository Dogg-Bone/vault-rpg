MARQUISE DE CAT
**Chevalier Ardent**
*Training Commander*
Drive: to forge the ultimate soldiers out of the swamp's cruelty
*"A battle-scarred feline with striking amber eyes and a permanently stiff posture. He wears a meticulously clean, polished breastplate over a dark, mud-resistant tunic, refusing to let the swamp tarnish the Marquisate's image."*

**Equipment**
- Masterwork heavy sabre (Close, 2-injury)
- Commander's whistle and signet ring
- Polished, reinforced breastplate (1-wear)

**Found at:** The mud-slicked obstacle courses of the Training Grounds.

**Found doing:** Screaming drill commands at exhausted recruits while inspecting their muddy weapons for microscopic flaws.

**Moves**
- Bark a rigid command that demands immediate obedience
- Point out a microscopic flaw in someone's posture or equipment
- Escalate punishment for a perceived lack of discipline

**Movement Anchor:** Heavy, Sustained, Direct, Bound energy

**Center:** Chest

**Key Facts**
1. He secretly fears that he is losing control over his own troops in this hostile environment.
2. He views the swamp not as a place, but as a personal enemy to be conquered through sheer willpower.

**Immediate Pressure:** The Keepers in Iron's expedition is undermining his absolute authority in the region.

**Persuasion Tactics:**
1. Appeal to hierarchy and strict duty
2. Threaten severe physical or disciplinary consequences

**Social Status:** Elite

**Perception of Vagabonds:** *"Mercenaries are undisciplined scum, but useful scum if pointed at the right enemy."*
