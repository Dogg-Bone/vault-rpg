MARQUISE DE CAT
**Vicomte Cousteau**
*Marquise Officer and Sycophant*
Drive: To climb the Marquise social ladder through treacherous opportunism while avoiding any actual danger.
*An orange tabby cat who dresses with excessive, theatrical flair. He wears obscenely tall, polished leather boots, a wide-brimmed hat adorned with a massive, vibrant feather, and a cape that swooshes dramatically when he turns.*

**Equipment**
- Ornate Rapier (Close, 1-injury, 1-exhaustion, +valuable, +fast)
- Hidden, very small throwing dagger (Intimate, 1-injury, +concealed)
- Flashy, but thin, duelist's armor (1-wear)
- A highly polished silver mirror
