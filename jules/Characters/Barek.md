KEEPERS IN IRON
**Barek**
*Expedition Leader*
Drive: to uncover the truth buried within the ancient ruins at any cost
*"A massive, grey-furred badger whose body is crisscrossed with thick leather straps holding numerous tools, chisels, and iron spikes. He moves with a slow, deliberate weight and rarely blinks, his eyes always scanning for structural weak points."*

**Equipment**
- Heavy iron mattock (Close, 1-injury, 1-wear)
- Bandolier of specialized climbing pitons
- Thick leather delving harness (1-wear)

**Found at:** The crumbling, vine-choked entrance to the Sunken Ruins near the clearing's edge.

**Found doing:** Carefully brushing away mud from an ancient stone carving while barking orders to his underlings to secure a collapsing archway.

**Moves**
- Ignore a direct threat in favor of examining an artifact
- Issue a blunt, completely humorless directive
- Utilize physical bulk to aggressively block a path

**Movement Anchor:** Heavy, Sustained, Indirect, Bound energy

**Center:** Shoulders

**Key Facts**
1. He is utterly obsessed with a specific pre-Dynastic mechanism he believes is buried here.
2. He views the Marquise occupation as a temporary, irrelevant annoyance compared to history.

**Immediate Pressure:** The water levels in the ruins are rising, threatening to drown the excavation before it yields results.

**Persuasion Tactics:**
1. Offer ancient, valuable scraps as payment
2. Stubbornly refuse to engage until his demands are met

**Social Status:** Upper

**Perception of Vagabonds:** *"Ruin-robbers and opportunists, the lot of them. Keep them away from the dig site."*
