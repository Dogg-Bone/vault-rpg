KEEPERS IN IRON
**Delver Bran**
*Chief Excavator and Archivist*
Drive: to unearth and understand the forbidden history of the Woodland
*A wiry, older badger whose fur is permanently stained with soot and ancient dust. He wears a heavy leather apron over chainmail, his spectacles constantly slipping down his snout. His movements are sharp and jerky, like a startled bird, always examining his surroundings.*

**Equipment**
- A crumbling, leather-bound journal filled with forbidden translations (personally significant)
- Heavy iron pickaxe (Close, 1-injury, 1-wear, piercing)
- Several satchels of volatile alchemical dusts

**Harm**
```statblock: inline
Injury: 2
Exhaustion: 3
Wear: 2
Morale: 3
```

**Found at:**
Deep within a ruin, meticulously brushing dirt away from an ancient inscription, or huddled over a makeshift desk in the waystation's archives.

**Found doing:**
Muttering to himself while translating archaic text, or eagerly inspecting a newly discovered, dirt-caked trinket.

**Moves**
- Reveal a startling, obscure piece of woodland history
- Trigger an ancient mechanism or trap (intentionally or not)
- Use a strange, ancient device to cause a distraction

**Movement Anchor:** Light Weight, Sudden Time, Indirect Space, Free Flow

**Center:** His constantly shifting eyes and spectacles

**Key Facts**
1. He has discovered a cache of relics that directly contradict the Keepers' dogmatic teachings about their sacred origins, and he is actively hiding them to prevent his superiors from destroying the evidence.
2. He respects knowledge far more than the chain of command, making him a wild card within the rigid Keeper hierarchy.

**Immediate Pressure:** He needs specialized, delicate tools to open a newly discovered vault without destroying the contents, tools that the Keepers' smiths cannot provide.

**Persuasion Tactics:**
1. Offering rare knowledge or historical secrets
2. Bartering with minor, less dangerous artifacts

**Social Status:** Lower (but highly valued for his specialized skills)

**Perception of Vagabonds:** *"Fascinating creatures! They travel so far and see so much. I wonder if they'd be willing to slip past the guards and help me open a certain door..."*
