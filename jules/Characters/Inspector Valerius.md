MARQUISE DE CAT
**Inspector Valerius**
*Customs Inspector*
Drive: to bring absolute, orderly control to a chaotic mercantile hub
*"A tall, rigid cat whose fur is meticulously groomed. She wears a crisp Marquisate uniform adorned with several polished brass medals. She carries a clipboard at all times, checking off boxes with a heavy, sharp quill that makes an intimidating scratching sound."*

**Equipment**
- A rapier of Marquisate steel (Close, 2-injury)
- A large clipboard filled with convoluted tax regulations
- A sharp, menacing writing quill

**Found at:** The main gangways connecting the Riverfolk Docks to the rest of Elmwood Reach.

**Found doing:** Berating a Riverfolk merchant for improperly logging the weight of a shipment of grain, much to the merchant's visible frustration.

**Moves**
- Cite an obscure, highly specific Marquisate tax law
- Confiscate goods pending a lengthy bureaucratic review
- Signal nearby Marquisate guards to form a blockade

**Movement Anchor:** Light, sustained, direct, bound energy

**Center:** Spine

**Key Facts**
1. She despises the Riverfolk Company's influence in Elmwood Reach but lacks the manpower to shut them down outright.
2. She suspects Finnian Rapids is smuggling dangerous contraband but hasn't found the proof she needs.

**Immediate Pressure:** Her superiors in the Keep are demanding higher tax yields from Elmwood Reach to fund the war effort.

**Persuasion Tactics:**
1. Demand strict adherence to protocol and threaten arrest
2. Offer a bureaucratic loophole in exchange for valuable intelligence

**Social Status:** Upper

**Perception of Vagabonds:** *"A chaotic variable in an otherwise ordered equation. They must be registered, taxed, and monitored closely."*
