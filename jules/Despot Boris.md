# Boris Condor, The Despot

**Species:** Condor
**Faction:** Eyrie Dynasties (House Condor)
**Role:** The Despot

## Concept
Boris is a massive, terrifying authoritarian who believes that order can only be maintained through fear and absolute control. He is ruthless, extracting exorbitant taxes and enforcing draconian laws. He views the woodland denizens as subjects to be broken and commanded, utilizing brute force and brutal repurposing of conquered infrastructure to cement his rule.

## Details
- **Location:** At the top of a violently repurposed and heavily fortified Marquisate keep, surrounded by his brutal enforcers.
- **Fear:** That the denizens of the woodland will realize that the Eyrie's hold is currently fragile, sparking a massive, unified rebellion that his forces cannot contain.
- **Secret:** Boris is secretly terrified of the Corvid Conspiracy after receiving a credible death threat; he has hired outside mercenaries to guard him, not trusting his own inner circle.
- **Request for the Vagabonds:** He needs Vagabonds to track down and make a bloody, public example of a prominent tax evader who has fled into the deep forest. Compensation: A significant cut of the hoarded tax revenue and a pardon for any past crimes against the Eyrie.
- **Opinion/Info:** He views the Builder (Giovanni di Cardinal) as a naive fool wasting valuable resources on pretty towers when those resources should be used to build prisons and siege engines.
