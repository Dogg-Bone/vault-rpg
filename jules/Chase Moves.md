# Chase Moves

## Give Chase
When you **pursue a fleeing quarry**, declare your approach and roll.
- If you use raw speed and stamina to close the distance, roll with **Might**.
- If you use shortcuts, anticipation, or tracking to cut them off, roll with **Cunning**.

On a hit, you catch up to them or corner them.
On a 10+, choose one:
- You catch them entirely off guard; take +1 forward against them.
- You keep the pursuit quiet or avoid drawing unwanted attention.
- You recover something they dropped or left behind in their haste.

On a 7-9, you catch them, but the GM chooses a **Chase Complication** from the list below. You must roll the appropriate stat to overcome it.
- On a 10+, you overcome it cleanly.
- On a 7-9, you push through but mark 1-exhaustion.
- On a miss, you stumble and must choose one consequence:
  - You end up in a dangerous or disadvantageous location.
  - You draw the attention of guards, enemies, or a hostile crowd.
  - You drop or lose something important in the pursuit.

On a miss, they slip away completely, or lead you straight into an ambush or trap.

## Flee
When you **run from pursuers**, declare your approach and roll.
- If you rely on agility, parkour, or navigating complex terrain, roll with **Finesse**.
- If you rely on losing them in a crowd, a fortunate distraction, or a desperate blind leap, roll with **Luck**.

On a hit, you escape their immediate pursuit.
On a 10+, choose one:
- You leave them completely lost; you have plenty of time to hide or rest.
- You lead them into a hazard or trap.
- You steal or grab something valuable on your way out.

On a 7-9, you escape, but the GM chooses a **Chase Complication** from the list below. You must roll the appropriate stat to overcome it.
- On a 10+, you overcome it cleanly.
- On a 7-9, you push through but mark 1-exhaustion.
- On a miss, you stumble and must choose one consequence:
  - You leave behind a clear trail or something identifiable.
  - You end up separated from your allies or in an unfamiliar, dangerous area.
  - You drop or leave something valuable behind.

On a miss, you are cornered, captured, or fall right into their hands.

---

## Chase Complications
If the GM wants to add a sudden twist to a chase, they can introduce one of these complications. Overcoming the complication requires a quick reaction, testing a specific stat.

1. **A Sudden Blockade (Might):** A heavy cart, a falling pillar, or a sturdy door blocks your path. You must smash through it or muscle it aside immediately to keep your momentum.
2. **A Perilous Shortcut (Finesse):** The fastest route requires balancing on a narrow beam, leaping across a crumbling rooftop, or sliding under a closing gate.
3. **A Confusing Intersection (Cunning):** The path splits into a maze of alleyways, dense thickets, or identical corridors. You must quickly deduce the right way to go or predict your opponent's route.
4. **An Unwanted Audience (Charm):** You burst into a crowded market, an ongoing ceremony, or a private meeting. You must quickly talk your way through, blend in, or convince bystanders not to interfere.
5. **A Twist of Fate (Luck):** A sudden downpour turns the ground to mud, a random animal gets in your way, or a structural collapse happens right as you pass. You must trust your instincts to avoid disaster.
